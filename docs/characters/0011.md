# #0011

#0011's own specification: the young spider in the black and red suit.
Its art, each of its moves, its guard and its Deflect, and how they play.
Its fantasy is a teenager bitten by a spider of its own: a spider's
strength and agility, web-shooters at its wrists, and two gifts no other
spider-hero has, a bioelectric venom it gathers in its fists and a
camouflage that turns it all but invisible. That is in its moves (a ball
of webbing that sticks its target where it stands, web lines that reel it
in, a punch that stuns it stiff, an uppercut that carries it up off the
ground, a fade out of sight), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and
Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: Punches
is a string (`hits`) that holds its target for the jab; the Web Shot's
webbing sticks whoever it strikes where it stands (`paralyze`); Web Lines
reel whoever they catch in (`pull`, its front only); the Venom Punch stuns
whoever its lightning reaches stiff (`paralyze`, a burst with its art);
the Venom Jump lifts it off the ground (`motion: rise`) carrying its
target up with it (`carry`) before throwing it high; and Camouflage fades
it out of sight (`cloak`, a capability any technique may now release),
drawn as a faint shimmer and invisible to a CPU until it strikes or is
struck.

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): bitten by a spider of its
own, it has a spider's strength, speed and agility, sticks to walls and
senses danger. Its web-shooters fire balls of webbing that stick a target
in place and lines it swings and zips on and yanks things in with. Its own
gifts are its venom, a bioelectric sting it gathers in its fists that
stuns whoever it touches stiff, and that it can drive up through an
uppercut that carries it off the ground; and its camouflage, which turns
it all but invisible until it moves to strike. It heals nobody, summons
nobody, throws nothing but its webbing and never homes in on anyone. So:
its moves are its fists and its feet, its webs, its venom and its
camouflage; its guard is its forearm raised; its Deflect a flip that
sweeps a great arc; its air dash a web line that zips it along; and
nothing it does is a power its canon does not give it.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; and an extra attack, its Venom Jump (on the ground and, once
per airtime, in the air). The sheet's crouch and its spider stance, its
taunts, intros, wins and losses (the hoodie, the mask off, the leap of
faith, the comic-book panel, the portrait and the heads), its other
punches and kicks (the backhand, the low swipe, the flips, the low sweep,
the rising and the upward punches, the flying, diving and drop kicks), its
web swinging, the web then venom combination and its glowing version, the
venom uppercut's separate arcs and sparks, and its falls and getting up
are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): Camouflage, out of
sight until it strikes; and the Venom Punch, its bioelectric sting that
holds whoever it reaches stiff. Both sit under Special abilities in the
controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats over #0011 while
it performs one (Camouflage 0.3 s + 0.3 s, the Venom Punch 1/3 s + 1/3 s).
Its Web Shot holds too, briefly, but is its everyday shot; its Web Lines'
reel is a pull like others'; its Venom Jump is its riser: all ordinary to
the player.

### Camouflage: out of sight

Camouflage is the roster's first **cloak**
([combat: techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)): its technique
releases `cloak: 2.5`, seconds its own fighter is cloaked from the release
(`CombatState.cloakFor`). Meanwhile:

- it is drawn as a faint shimmer of its own art (its body, shadow and
  trail at a fifth of their strength, wavering a little; steady under
  reduced motion: [rendering](../systems/rendering.md));
- a CPU cannot see it at all: from the moment the picture its reaction
  time shows has it cloaked, the CPU sees it where it vanished, standing
  still and doing nothing, and swings at that ghost; it sees #0011 again
  only once #0011 has shown itself and its reaction time has passed
  ([AI: perception](../systems/ai.md));
- it is no invulnerability: it is struck, pushed and pulled exactly as
  ever, and any hit that reaches it, blocked or not, shows it at once.

Its own first move shows it too: an attack, the Deflect, a technique.
Running, jumping and Dashing never do. The cloak ends when its 2.5 s run
out. The cast (crouched, its body fading from the feet up) can be broken
by a hit like any cast, and then nothing is cloaked and no cooldown
starts. A cast that only cloaks harms nobody, so it is no attack: it gives
way to any move, and a move made in its release (the outline fading) cuts
the pose short with the cloak kept. The policy gives a cloak its 3 s from
the release ([cooldowns](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)), and it costs no
Energy.

### The venom and the webs

- **The Venom Punch** (`attack5`) is a technique: a third of a second
  gathering venom in its fist (nothing protects it), then a burst in front
  of it, `{ x: 4, y: -80, w: 80, h: 80 }`, from its body out to 84 units
  and from the floor to over its head, the ring of lightning its art,
  centred on its fist (`effect: { animation: 'attack5_object', offset: {
  x: 42, y: -42 } }`). Whoever it reaches takes 3 and is stunned stiff for
  1.2 s (`paralyze`: no launch, helpless). A raised Shield blocks it like
  any hit. The policy's 3 s, for its hold; no Energy.
- **The Venom Jump** (`extra_attack`): crouched for a frame (the hang),
  then it springs up at 520 units/s (`motion: { type: 'rise', speed: 520
  }`) for two frames and carries on up under gravity. Its uppercut catches
  whoever is in front and above (1, no launch) and carries them up with it
  (`carry: { lift: 0 }`); the jolt at the top throws them high (3, Base
  Launch 2 straight up). From the ground it is still a grounded move,
  never steered by what is held even once it has left the floor (the
  [attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement) rule for a
  grounded move its own motion lifts); started in the air it keeps the
  universal air control, once per airtime (`airUses: 1`): its way back to
  the stage.
- **The Web Shot** (`attack3`, on the ground): arms flung wide, then the
  web shot from its wrist, its ball of webbing (`projectiles.attack3_object`)
  leaving 46 units in front of it at its chest, at 760 units/s for 0.55 s.
  Whoever it strikes takes 1 and is stuck where they stand for 0.35 s
  (`paralyze`), long enough to close in; the policy's 3 s for that hold
  from the shot, so it costs no Energy.
- **Web Lines** (`midair_attack3`): diving, both hands thrown forward, then
  both web lines fired down ahead: from its hands 18 units in front of it
  down to 66 units out and past its feet (`{ x: 18, y: -40, w: 48, h: 48
  }`), 3 and a stun, and meanwhile it reels in whoever is within 100 units
  of its hands in front of it (`pull: { radius: 100, speed: 700, offset: {
  x: 24, y: -30 }, front: true }`). The lines are its webbing striking,
  never a projectile.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Punches** (`attack1`) and the **Spin Kick** (`attack2`), its
two close-range strikes (out to 40 and 56 units). Its webs reach far, the
Venom Jump lifts it off the ground, and its techniques are no strikes of
the body, so none could be a partner. Five rapid presses of either one's
button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its own
launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its two-frame `mouvment`
clip, a low, spider-like lunge, one hand up, its back leg stretched out
behind, played once across the whole Dash, and its one-frame
`midair_mouvment`, a web line shot out ahead that zips it along, across
the whole air dash. Its canon's agility is in its moves (the Venom Jump,
its flips), never in how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: its forearm
brought up before its face, the one guard pose the sheet draws (its sheet
draws no raise or lower of its own, so it has none: the Shield goes up and
down at once). In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
a flip:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 3 / 20, recovery: 1 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: -32, y: -96, w: 84, h: 98 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Tucked (the startup), then the flip and its legs' great sweep for three
frames (live: 32 units behind it to 52 in front, from its feet to well
over its head), straightened (its recovery): a quarter of a second,
keeping the whole of its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every
Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's
projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now #0011's. No cooldown; it
costs 15 Energy as it starts, as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15.
Its one projectile, the Web Shot's webbing, holds whoever it strikes and
so has the policy's 3 s instead of a cost; its Specials have their 3 s
too. It leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a second.

Its `launchReaction`: its spider-sense and agility get it back into the
fight sooner than most, 0.12 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.4 s, tumbling from
1150 units/s, and 25° of steering. Its launches are as strong as anyone's,
and it rebounds off the scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its
own).

Where the sheet repeats a drawing (the Web Shot's arms-wide pose, a pose
held), the clip plays the same file again: one file on disk. The sheet's
own guard-raising and guard-lowering poses are the same drawings as its
landing's, so the Shield has no raise or lower clip rather than a copy.
The webbing and the lightning are drawn centred where they are, at the
fighter's art-pixel size, never fitted to its height. Each clip's
`heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 49, so every frame normalizes at
exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height` (49 × 88 / 52 ≈
82.9 world units) puts #0011's art pixels at the roster's common size.

Its webbing, its venom's lightning and the blur of a kick or a flip are
drawn beside or round it, and would pull the automatic anchor (the upper
art's opaque middle) off its body, so every clip authors its own anchors
(`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art): the
middle of its suit's own colours in its upper rows; on a frame where a
blur still does (the Deflect's sweep, the roundhouse, the crescent, the
Air Kick's swing), the middle of its head and chest. Everything stands on
the bottom of its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

its universal glyph, named and drawn as Deflect while #0011 is in the air.

## How it plays

An agile all-rounder. Up close, Punches and the Spin Kick are its quick
strikes and its Mash Combo; the Venom Jump is its launcher from under an
opponent and its way back to the stage. From afar the Web Shot sticks an
approaching opponent in place long enough to close in, and in the air Web
Lines reel one in. The Venom Punch stuns whoever its lightning reaches
stiff, a long hold to convert. Camouflage takes it out of sight: against a
CPU it is invisible until it strikes, against a player a faint shimmer
that is easy to lose. Its weaknesses: its plain strikes are light, its
strongest tools (the venom and the camouflage) are rooted casts any hit
breaks and then wait 3 s, its webbing holds only a moment, and the cloak
ends the moment it strikes or is struck.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, a hold from afar,
a reel, a rising launcher that carries, a long cast with a hold) and uses
every one of them, and casts its Camouflage now and then in a safe moment
(`cloakPlan`, beside the heal's), then goes in unseen. A CPU facing it
loses track of it while it is cloaked.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24
matches against each opponent, 240 a level): #0011 wins 0.55 at Medium,
0.52 at Hard and 0.53 at Brutal, within the roster's usual range. At
Hard: 13 of 24 against #0001, 10 against #0002, 17 against #0003, 15
against #0004, 13 against #0005, 12 against #0006, 6 against #0007, 9
against #0008, 13 against #0009 and 16 against #0010. Its weakest matchup
is #0007's (4 to 6 of 24 at every level), whose ground-bound weapons
outrange its webs. Its CPU uses its Mash Combo most, then the Venom Jump
and the Web Shot (about fifteen presses a match each), its aerials and the
Spin Kick; Camouflage about five times a match and the Venom Punch two or
three. Its first tuning won 0.7 to 0.88: the Web Shot's hold was cut from
0.6 s to 0.35 s and the Venom Jump's throw from Base Launch 3 to 2. The
results swing by about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the next.

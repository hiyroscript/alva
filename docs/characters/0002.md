# #0002

#0002's own specification: the speedster. Its art, each of its moves, its
guard and its Deflect, and how they play. "Speedster" is its fantasy, its
art and its moves (a homing dash, a plunge, a roll, a rising tornado), not
its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter
does. It is part of
the product specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9):
the shared rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is a mechanic of its own, not just a pose and damage, built on
attack features any fighter can use ([combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):
strikes (`hits`), `carry`, attack `motion` (`homing`, `bounce`, `rise`,
`roll`), `airUses`, `freeFall`, `passThrough`, attack `hurtboxes`,
piercing projectiles and a Deflect that knocks projectiles back
(`deflectProjectiles`).

## Buttons and moves

Three numbered attacks, each an ordinary attack on a button of its own
with a mid-air version, and an extra attack. It has no summon or
technique, and no `attack4` or `attack5` button.

| Button | On the ground | In the air |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attack1` (U) | **One-Two**: two strikes in one press, the jab holding the target for the straight, which pushes it away | **Homing Attack**: a lock-on dash. It hangs for 7/60 s, then dashes at 1100 units/s at its opponent (within 240 units and not behind it), re-aimed every step; with nobody there it dashes straight ahead. A hit pops the target up and #0002 springs off it with both its air jumps back. Once per airtime |
| `attack2` (I) | **Rapid Kicks**: after a 0.2 s wind-up, three kicks hold the target in place and a fourth flings it away sideways. A Shield stops the flurry at the kick it blocks | **Bounce Attack**: it plunges at 1400 units/s until it meets the ground or an opponent (which it spikes downward), and bounces back up about a jump's height, the attack over so it can bounce again. Twice per airtime |
| `attack3` (O) | **Spin Attack**: it curls up (8/60 s), then rolls at 400 units/s plus 0.8 of the running speed it had (up to 1000: out of a Dash, a cannonball), as a smaller target (the ball's hurtbox), rolling on through the opponent it bowls over. A Shield stops it dead and sends it back | **Blue Tornado**: spun into a tornado it rises about 175 units, carrying its target up with it, then flings it upward. Once per airtime, and it leaves #0002 in free fall (no attack or air jump until it lands) |
| `extra_attack` (J) | **Whirlwind**: it spins up a whirlwind and sends it off as a tornado (360 units/s) that catches its target, dragging and lifting it through four strikes, then flings it upward on the fifth. A trap to set, its long spin-up the price of each | — |
| `shield` (L) | its guard, a Shield (held) | **Deflect** (a fresh press): a swat of the hand that knocks the other fighter's projectiles back at their thrower |

Its Homing Attack, Bounce Attack and Blue Tornado never start while it is
still flying from a launch: it has to recover first (an air jump, a fast
fall or landing), so none of them can cancel a launch that carries it
away.

```js
actions: {
  extra_attack: 'extra_attack',                          // the Whirlwind
  transform: null,                                       // reserved
  attack1: { ground: 'attack1', air: 'midair_attack1' }, // the One-Two / the Homing Attack
  attack2: { ground: 'attack2', air: 'midair_attack2' }, // the Rapid Kicks / the Bounce Attack
  attack3: { ground: 'attack3', air: 'midair_attack3' }, // the Spin Attack / the Blue Tornado
},
```

### Hits

| Hit | Damage | Base Launch | Directional Launch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One-Two jab, then straight | 1, 3 | 0, 1 | none, horizontal |
| Rapid Kicks (three kicks, then the finisher) | 1 each, 3 | 0, 2 | none, horizontal |
| Homing Attack | 3 | 1 | vertical |
| Bounce Attack | 3 | 2 | reverse vertical |
| Spin Attack | 3 | 1 | horizontal |
| Blue Tornado (three strikes, then the finisher) | 1 each, 3 | 0, 2 | none (carried along), vertical |
| Whirlwind tornado (four strikes, then the finisher) | 1 each, 10 | 0, 2 | none (carried along), vertical |
| Deflect | 3 | 2 | horizontal |

Every hit is one of the four damage tiers ([combat](../systems/combat.md#damage)):
its ticks 1, ordinary solid strikes and finishers 3, and Whirlwind's final blow 10. A multi-hit
move's whole is the sum of its strikes: the One-Two 4, the Rapid Kicks
6, the Blue Tornado 6, the Whirlwind 14.

### Each move in detail

- **One-Two** (`attack1`, 4 frames at 20 fps, a quarter of a second in
  all): two strikes, the jab (frame 2: 1, no launch, holding the target, a
  two-step freeze) and the straight (frame 4: 3, Base Launch 1 sideways);
  hit-cancel once the straight is out. It keeps all the speed it is thrown
  at, under half the ground deceleration.
- **Homing Attack** (`midair_attack1`, the ball): `homing`, range 240,
  speed 1100 for up to 0.22 s after a 7/60 s hang; 3, Base Launch 1
  upward; springs off at 760 up and 140 back, both its air jumps given
  back by a hit; a miss keeps a quarter of its speed. Once per airtime.
- **Rapid Kicks** (`attack2`, 4 frames at 20 fps played twice): a 0.2 s
  wind-up (its heaviest launcher keeps its telegraph), three kicks (1
  each, no launch, each stun outlasting the gap to the next), then the
  finisher (3, Base Launch 2 sideways); once the finisher is out, a
  hit-cancel (a jump or a Dash may chase it). It keeps most of a run
  (`momentum` 0.8). Its repeat delay separates flurries; different-move follow-ups can
  still chase the fling.
- **Bounce Attack** (`midair_attack2`, the ball): `bounce`, a 5/60 s hang,
  then 1400 units/s down, rebounding at 900; 3, Base Launch 2 downward.
  Twice per airtime.
- **Spin Attack** (`attack3`, the ball): `roll`, 400 + 0.8 × the run (up
  to 1000), friction 420, after an 8/60 s curl, for 0.55 s; `passThrough`,
  the ball's hurtbox; 3, Base Launch 1 sideways; recoil 260 off a Shield.
- **Blue Tornado** (`midair_attack3`, 4 frames looped): `rise` at 460
  (≈175 units in all), three strikes that carry the target up (1 each),
  then the finisher (3, Base Launch 2 upward); once per airtime, and free
  fall after.
- **Whirlwind** (`extra_attack`, 9 frames at 20 fps, a planted cast): sends its tornado
  (`extra_attack_object`) on frame 6: 360 units/s for 1.6 s, piercing
  five strikes 0.14 s apart, four of 1 that carry the target along and
  lift it 300 units/s, then the finisher (10, Base Launch 2 upward).

Cooldowns: One-Two, Homing Attack, Rapid Kicks, Bounce Attack, Spin Attack
and Blue Tornado use the universal 0.5-second repeat delay. Whirlwind explicitly
uses 5 seconds, starting when its attack ends (or is cut short). Its cooldown
ring shows the touch button's tornado art in the center and remaining seconds
below. Deflect keeps its existing 0.05-second delay; running, jumps and Shield
are exempt. Dash and air dash each have a separate 0.5-second timer.
Ground only: One-Two, Rapid Kicks, Spin Attack and Whirlwind.

## Movement

None of its own. Until universal movement it had a faster run (Speed
Power 3, 360 against #0001's 330), quicker acceleration and a longer,
faster Dash (1100 against 950), for no reason but its "speedster" lore;
now it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls, Dashes and air
dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
#0001 does. Its speed is in its moves: the Homing Attack's lock-on dash,
the Bounce Attack's plunge, the Spin Attack's roll on its running speed,
the Blue Tornado's lift. It brings the art: its four-frame `mouvment`
clip (the figure-eight blur) played once across the whole Dash, and its
one-frame `midair_mouvment` (stretched out flat) across the whole air
dash.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield',
groundAnimation: 'shielding', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`,
with no raise or lower pose. In the air the Shield button is its
**Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), the swat:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 2 / 20, recovery: 2 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: 6, y: -64, w: 32, h: 56 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  cooldown: 0.05, airMomentum: 1, airControl: 0.4,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the arms flung wide, then the swat (frames 2 and 3, live: the hand
up and out in front, then carried on through), frame 3 then held two frames
more: a quarter of a second, quicker than #0001's and shorter in reach,
falling as it swats and carrying the whole of its drift. While it is live its box (the front from the head to
the knees, out to 38 units) strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2,
here sideways, and knocks the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at
their thrower, now #0002's. A 0.05 s cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it
starts, as every Deflect does. Its perfect Shield, like any block, costs
15.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 25, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15
(a perfect block's included); Combat Assist costs nothing. Its own is
only its refill, 12 / s (`energy: { regen: 12 }`). Its `launchReaction`:
0.2 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.7 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, 15° of
steering.

## Art and animations

`#0002` frames live in `assets/characters/0002/`: 89 frames cut from
one supplied sprite sheet, each cropped tight to its art with the sheet's
flat green background (0, 90, 20) made transparent and nothing else
changed (1×, 8-bit RGBA, one file pixel per art pixel; the visual
config forces `pixelSize: 1`, so no grid is looked for). Eight idle
frames (the fighting stance, 29–30 × 38–39 px, the reference: 39 art
pixels), twelve run frames (≤ 38 × 40), eight jump frames (the spin
ball, 30 × 28–30, looped), one fall frame (arms spread, 35 × 48), four
mouvment (Dash) frames (the figure-eight blur, 37–39 × 36), one
`midair_mouvment` (air dash) frame (stretched out flat, arms swept back,
one foot leading, 45 × 33: the sheet's one airborne dash pose), one hurt
(34 × 42) and one mid-air hurt (38 × 32), one `shielding` guard (29 × 39),
three `deflect` frames (the sheet's aerial swat: the arms flung wide, the
hand up and out, the hand carried through; ≤ 35 × 48; the first is the
same drawing as the fall frame, its own file),
four attack1 frames (the One-Two), four attack2 frames (the Rapid Kicks,
74–94 × 71–80 px: the kick trails reach far in front and below the
standing foot), the eight ball frames again for each of midair_attack1
(the Homing Attack), midair_attack2 (the Bounce Attack) and attack3 (the
Spin Attack), four midair_attack3 frames (the Blue Tornado, 34–35 ×
45–46), nine extra_attack frames (the Whirlwind, 29–56 × 37–39) and four
extra_attack_object frames (the tornado it sends, the same drawing as
the Blue Tornado's, as a projectile). Every clip is its own files, named
for it, so the ball and the tornado are copies on disk under their own
codenames. Everything faces right. No Land art.
Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 39, so every frame
normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height`
(66 world units) puts #0002's art pixels at #0001's size (88 / 52 units
each).

Where automatic anchoring would drag the body, a clip authors its own
anchors: `anchorX` (art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art)
on #0002's ball and tornado (their own middle), Whirlwind (the body
inside the wind), One-Two (its planted feet) and Deflect (its face and
chest, 19, 14 and 14.5, never dragged out by the swatting hand), and
`anchorY` (art pixels
down from the top of each frame's art to the feet) on its Rapid Kicks,
whose trails sweep below the standing foot: without it the bottom-anchored
frame would lift the whole body off the ground by the trail's depth. A
frame with no `anchorY` stands on the bottom of its art, as every other
does (`drawFrame` draws it at `-anchorArtY`).

#0002's clips play at their own rates (`FPS_0002` in its definition): Idle
10 fps, Run 24 (following the speed, from 0.6 of it up to 1.6 of it on a
Dash's run-on), the Dash (`mouvment`) 24 and the air dash
(`midair_mouvment`) 6 (each shown across its whole Dash), the spin Jump
30, the balls 30, the Deflect 20, attack1 20, attack2 20, the tornado 30,
the Whirlwind 20 and its tornado projectile 16: every attack's rate a
whole number of 60 Hz steps per frame. It has no Land clip: it lands
straight into its stance, and that makes it no more responsive than
#0001, whose land clip is a pose only. If
its airborne or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first idle frame; an
attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or the air dash whose frames are
missing is refused.
Its art faces right. Drawn at the roster's common art-pixel size
(88 / 52 world units per art pixel), its 39-pixel idle stands 66 units
tall, a head shorter than #0001.

## Touch buttons

**Whirlwind** shows its projectile's tornado (`projectileAnimations.extra_attack_object`
frame 2, file `0002_extra_attack_object_3.png`), with a tornado glyph if
that image cannot load; **One-Two** `attack1` frame 3 (`attack1_4`) / in
the air **Homing Attack** `midair_attack1` frame 0 (`midair_attack1_1`);
**Rapid Kicks** `attack2` frame 1 (`attack2_2`) / **Bounce Attack**
`midair_attack2` frame 4 (`midair_attack2_5`); **Spin Attack** `attack3`
frame 4 (`attack3_5`) / **Blue Tornado** `midair_attack3` frame 2
(`midair_attack3_3`). Its touch cluster uses slots 1 to 3; the Shield
button keeps its universal glyph, named and drawn as Deflect while #0002 is
in the air.

## Balance

Its tuning came out of seeded CPU-vs-CPU fights against the first #0001
(120 one-minute fights per level on Desert); the measurements and the
fixes that got there are in
[`UPDATES.md`](../../UPDATES.md#0002-the-speedster). Against the current
#0001, with universal movement and the faster game (200 one-minute fights
per level, Void falls #0001 : #0002): Easy 130 : 7, Medium 94 : 111, Hard
103 : 119, Brutal 114 : 81. Its lost run and Dash advantage alone changed
little (Hard 67 : 39 in 120 fights with both fighters' old moves); the
measurements are in [`UPDATES.md`](../../UPDATES.md#universal-movement-and-momentum).
Before, with the Deflect and the air dash: Easy 109 : 1, Medium 111 : 60,
Hard 81 : 45, Brutal 82 : 93. #0002's Deflect turning #0001's orbs back is most
of what moved since #0001's air Shield went; #0001's tuning and those
measurements are in [its specification](0001.md#balance). All of them
predate the shared damage tiers, the universal Energy costs and the
current repeat cooldowns and Whirlwind tuning, and have not been measured again since.

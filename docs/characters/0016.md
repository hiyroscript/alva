# #0016

#0016's own specification: the adventurer in a teal tank top, combat
trousers and boots, her hair in a long braid, a pistol in each hand. Her
art, each of her moves, her guard and her Deflect, and how they play. Her
fantasy is the adventurer who has an answer for every distance:
her **feet and her acrobatics** up close (a high kick, a kick driven out
off her hands, a leg sweep, a backflip kick), **two pistols** fired in turn
for quick chip at mid range, a **hunting bow** whose arrow flies most of a
stage and lands far harder in a back turned to it, a **grapple** that
hauls her back up to the stage, and a **medipack** to patch herself up
when she finds the room. That is in her moves (a volley of shots, an arrow
that punishes a turned back, a line that drags opponents to its hook, a
heal), never her locomotion: she runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every
other fighter does. It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules she relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: her
**Twin Pistols** are a `volley` (four shots from one press, one Energy
cost, a Shield that blocks one stopping the rest); her **Silent Arrow** is
a technique, a long breakable draw before a fast arrow whose hit lands as
the hunter's shot, 5 at Base Launch 3, in the back of an opponent turned
away from it (`backstab`); her **Grapple** is her recovery, a lift
(`motion: rise`, once per airtime) whose line draws in anyone near its
hook (`pull`) before it strikes; her **Medipack** is a heal, a kneel any
blow breaks; her Kick Combo's high kick holds its target for the kick
that follows.

## Her canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): an archaeologist and
adventurer who fights with two pistols fired in turn (with ammunition that
never runs out), a hunting bow whose arrows are her quiet weapon, kicks
and acrobatic flips up close, a grappling line fired from a gun to climb
and to drag enemies to her, and medipacks that heal her. She heals only
herself, summons nobody, makes no clone, never homes in and casts nothing.
So: her moves are her feet, her pistols, her bow, her grapple and her
medipack; her guard is her pistols crossed before her face, her Deflect a
somersault kick, her Dash her sprint and her air dash a somersault.

with their mid-air versions and two techniques (on the ground); and an
extra attack, the Leg Sweep, on the ground only (the sheet draws it low on
the floor; in the air its button does nothing).

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Medipack, the one
move that heals her, a long kneel any blow breaks: a fight with her is a
fight to keep her from finding the room for it. It sits under Special
abilities in the controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats
over #0016 while she kneels. Her kicks, her pistols, her bow and her
grapple are her everyday tools: ordinary to the player (the Silent Arrow
is a technique, but an ordinary one).

### The Twin Pistols: a volley

One press fires her pistols in turn, a shot a tenth of a second
(`projectile: { ..., volley: { count: 4, interval: 0.1 } }`, three in the
air), each its own projectile (`attack2_object`, `midair_attack2_object`:
760 units/s for 0.4 s, about 300 units, a box 28 × 14): it strikes on its
own (1, no launch, a stun just long enough for the next shot to land), is
blocked on its own and is turned back on its own. Together they are one
use: one press, one Energy cost (15, as the first leaves), and a raised
Shield that blocks any shot stops the volley there, its shots still flying
gone and no more fired, so a volley never costs a Shield more than one
block. A hit on her stops the shots still to come.

### The Silent Arrow: the hunter's shot

A technique, a cast ([combat: techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)):
she brings her bow round, drops to one knee and draws (`attack3_cast`, six
frames at 15 fps, two fifths of a second, nothing protects it: a hit breaks
it and no arrow flies), then looses (`attack3_release`, four frames more).
The arrow (`attack3_object`) leaves her bow 40 units before her at her
chest and flies 780 units/s for 0.6 s, about 470 units, a third of the
main stage, its box the arrow's length (44 × 14). It strikes 3 at Base Launch 1
sideways, the way it flies, a solid hit more than a throw; but an opponent
whose **back is turned** to it
(`backTurned` in
[`hit-effects.js`](../../js/game/combat/hit-effects.js): facing away from
where it comes from) takes it as the hunter's shot, its `backstab`: 5 at
Base Launch 3, held longer (hitstun 0.5). An opponent fleeing her, or
recovering toward the stage with its back to her, is her prey. A raised
Shield blocks it either way round; a Deflect turns it back like any shot.
An ordinary construct: 15 Energy, no cooldown (its backstab's 5 is no 10).

### The Grapple: her way back

In the air `attack4` fires her grapple gun straight up (`midair_attack4`,
`motion: { type: 'rise', speed: 520 }`): a hang of two fifteenths of a
second as she aims, then she is hauled up after the hook at 520 units/s
for four fifteenths of a second (about 140 units) and on up under gravity.
While she rises her line draws in every opponent whose middle is within
110 units of the hook over her head (`pull`, at up to 520 units/s,
`offset: { x: 4, y: -124 }`) and her line and hook strike (`{ x: -16, y:
-170, w: 48, h: 120 }`, from her chest to 170 up): 3 at Base Launch 2
straight up. Once per airtime (`airUses: 1`: landing or being hit gives it
back); half her drift kept. On the ground the same input is the Flip Kick.

### The Medipack: a heal

A technique, a cast that releases a `heal`: her hand to her belt, then
knelt over the medipack (`attack5_cast`, ten frames at 10 fps: a full
second, her longest commitment, and nothing protects it), and
as she gets back up (`attack5_release`) her Launch Point drops by 10, never
below 0. A heal is no hit: it neither restarts nor delays her Launch Point
recovery. A hit before the heal breaks it and heals nothing. A heal harms
nobody, so any move, a jump or a Dash cuts it short (before the heal,
nothing healed and no cooldown). Its cooldown is the policy's 3 s for a
heal, from the heal; it costs no Energy.

### Mash Combo

Her pair is the **Kick Combo** (`attack1`) and the **Leg Sweep**
(`extra_attack`), her two close-range strikes (out to 62 and 48 units).
Her Flip Kick is a launcher on the right stick, her pistols and her arrow
are shots, her grapple a lift: none could be a partner. Five rapid
presses of either one's button on the ground set off her [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its
own launch.

## Movement

None of her own: she runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. She brings the art: her `mouvment` clip, her
sprint flung low, a pistol thrown back (1 frame), shown across the whole
Dash, and her `midair_mouvment`, a somersault (4 frames: tucked, over,
upside down, coming out of it), across the whole air dash. Her canon's
agility is in her moves, never in how she runs or jumps.

Her attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and her close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Her `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', perfectWindow: 0.1,
perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: a pistol lowered, then both crossed before her
face. The sheet draws no lowering, so there is no lowering clip: the Shield
goes down at once. In the air the Shield button is her **Deflect** (her
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 3 / 20, recovery: 2 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: -16, y: -88, w: 62, h: 96 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Flung forward (the startup), then the somersault, her kick sweeping up
through an arc before her for three frames (live: 16 units behind her to
46 in front, from below her feet to over her head), coming round (its
recovery): three tenths of a second, keeping the whole of her drift.
While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2,
here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at
their thrower, now #0016's. No cooldown; starting it costs nothing (never
while exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back costs 15 Energy,
as every Deflect does. The somersault is the sheet's aerial flip kick.

Her Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Her three constructs, the Twin
Pistols, the Air Twin Pistols and the Silent Arrow, cost 15 each (a whole
volley once); the Medipack, a heal, costs nothing. She leaves `energy`
out and refills at the shared 12 a second.

Her `launchReaction`: an acrobat, she is flung a little less long than
most, 0.14 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.45 s, tumbling only from 1150
units/s, and 25° of steering: she twists out of a throw better than most.
Her launches are as
strong as anyone's, and she rebounds off the scenery as everyone does (no
`launchBounce` of her own).

Where a row repeats a drawing, the clip plays the same file again: one
file on disk, never a copy within a codename (her stance between kicks,
her aim between shots, the bow raised and lowered, her grapple gun aimed
before and after it fires). Where two rows draw the same picture, each
clip still plays its own codename's file
([`codename_rule`](../../codename_rule)): the sheet draws her backflip on
the ground and in the air alike (the Flip Kick's and the Deflect's, five
drawings), her crouch and spring before it as her landing and her jump's
takeoff, and her sprint as the leap of her shots in the air and the
Deflect's start. Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 55,
so every frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and
`visual.height` (55 × 88 / 52 ≈ 93 world units) puts #0016's art pixels at
the roster's common size.

Her pistols held out, her bow, her braid, her kicking legs and her
grappling line would pull the automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque
middle) off her body, so every clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art
pixels from the left of each frame's visible art): the middle of her tank
top, which every frame draws on her body. The frames lit by a pistol's
flash, where the light hides her top, are anchored where their unlit
twin's body is. Most clips stand on the bottom of their art; the Flip
Kick, the Deflect, the Kick Combo, the Air Kick and the Air Twin Pistols
keep the sheet's own ground line (`anchorY`), so the backflip rises off
the floor and its arc sweeps under her feet, and her somersault keeps its
middle at one height as it turns.

What of the sheet is no move: her crouch, her side and upward pistol
shots, the second string of the jab row that ends on a shot, both pistols
fired out to either side, her flips firing down, her aerial shots angled
down and her other aerial shots, her second flip kick, the bear trap and
the crouch that sets it, the rope arrow shot straight up and the rope she
climbs, the medikit, bow and ammunition pictures, the flying grapple hooks
(drawn apart from her), her falls and getting up, her roll's coming up on
the ground, her standing poses without her pistols, her intro on a rope,
taunts, wins and losses, the motorcycle and the recoloured palettes.

If her airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load she holds her first
stance frame; an attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or the air dash
whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

An all-rounder with an answer for every distance. Up close, the Kick
Combo and the Leg Sweep are her quick strings and her Mash Combo, the
Flip Kick her launcher and her answer to an opponent coming down on her.
At mid range the Twin Pistols chip and interrupt, four light shots for
one Energy cost. Further out the Silent Arrow is a slow draw that pays
off: a solid hit, and a heavy one on any opponent caught facing away,
running from her or recovering to the stage with its back turned. Off the
stage the Grapple hauls her back up, dragging in anyone waiting over her.
And when she finds the room, the Medipack takes the damage back. Her
weaknesses: her shots are light and a Shield stops them, her draw and her
kneel are long and broken by any blow, every shot and arrow costs Energy
she also needs to Dash and block, and an opponent who rushes her down
leaves her no time for either.

**The CPU** plays her from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads her moves as any fighter's (strings, a volley, a cast
that lets go of a shot, a lift that pulls, a heal), heals when the
opponent cannot reach her in time, and uses every one of her moves.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 360 a level): #0016 wins 0.47 at Medium, 0.54 at
Hard and 0.61 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range (0.43 to 0.63
over the same kind of runs). At Hard: 22 of 24 against #0001, 19 against
#0002, 11 against #0003, 21 against #0004, 13 against #0005, 9 against
#0006, 8 against #0007, 9 against #0008, 8 against #0009, 20 against
#0010, 11 against #0011, 8 against #0012, 11 against #0013, 11 against
#0014 and 13 against #0015. Her weakest matchups are the fighters whose
reach or shots outrange her kicks and outpace her draw (#0007, #0009 and
#0012, 8 of 24); her best, #0001's and #0004's, whose long casts her
arrow and her kicks punish. Most of her eliminations come from her
Deflect (her one sideways Base Launch 2 move in the air, which the CPU
uses to guard the ledge), her Kick Combo and her arrow; her pistols chip
and interrupt more than they finish.

Her first tuning won 0.50 at Medium, 0.54 at Hard and 0.65 at Brutal: a
Brutal CPU picked opponents off from across the stage with an arrow at
Base Launch 2 that flew 700 units, and healed at its leisure. Her arrow
now strikes at Base Launch 1 (the hunter's shot in a turned back is
still 5 at Base Launch 3) and flies about 470 units (0.6 s, not 0.9), and
the Medipack takes a full second (0.9 before), as the roster's other two
heals do: 0.61 at Brutal. Two other ways tried and dropped: a smaller
Deflect lowered Brutal a little but the lower levels far more (0.37 at
Medium), and so did a launch reaction like most of the roster's
(tumbling from 1100 units/s, 20° of steering: 0.37 at Medium, 0.52 at
Brutal); both keep their first values. The results swing by about 0.05
from one set of seeds to the next.

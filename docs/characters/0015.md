# #0015

#0015's own specification: the small pink hedgehog girl in a red dress,
red boots and gold bracelets. Its art, each of its moves, its guard and its
Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is a tiny fighter with a huge
hammer and a trick up its sleeve: it boxes up close in its gloves, swings
its hammer up, round and down, dives with it into the floor, whirls it
until the whirl tears loose as a pink tornado, and with a blown kiss leaves
a **present** on the stage that waits there for the first opponent
careless enough to touch it. That is in its moves (a dive whose landing
shakes the floor, a tornado that draws in, carries and throws, a trap that
stays where it was left, one at a time), never its locomotion: it runs,
jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the
product specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the
shared rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: its
present is a **trap** (a projectile with no speed that waits six seconds
where it was left, set off by the first opponent to touch it), bounded to
**one at a time** (`maxInPlay`, a projectile field any fighter may now
use: a new present takes the old one's place, gone without going off);
its Pink Typhoon whirls its hammer round, holding whoever it catches
(`carry`), then lets loose a tornado that draws opponents in (`pull`),
carries them along as it strikes again and again (`pierce`, `carry`) and
throws them up (`finisher`); its Girl Dunk is a plunge (`motion: slam`)
whose landing sends a shockwave along the floor (`impact`, a `quake`: a
jump clears it); its Girl Crush's sweep holds its target through a full
turn for the smash, which drives a blocking Shield back (`blockPush`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a cheerful, headstrong
girl of great strength who fights with a huge hammer pulled out of
nowhere, swung round and up and brought down on its foes; who, in the
fighting game the sheet comes from, boxes in gloves (a jab, a straight, an
uppercut), smashes and dunks with its hammer, whirls it until it raises a
pink tornado that catches its foe, and leaves a present, with a blown
kiss, that goes off on whoever touches it. It heals nobody (the sheet's
healing pose is that game's own, every fighter's there, not its
fighting), summons nobody, makes no clone, throws no hammer and never
homes in. So: its moves are its gloves, its hammer, its tornadoes and its
present, its guard is its arm raised, its Deflect a flying knee, its Dash
its sprint.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack3', 'midair_attack3']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Gift and the Air
Gift, a present that waits, seconds on, where it was left: a fight round
it is a fight round a trap. They sit under Special abilities in the
controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats over #0015 while
it leaves one. Its gloves, its hammer, its dive and its tornadoes are its
everyday tools: ordinary to the player.

### The Gift: a present that waits, one at a time

A present is a projectile with no speed: it appears where it is left and
never moves (`attack3_object` on the floor, `midair_attack3_object` in the
air, both drawn with the same art). It waits six seconds (`lifetime: 6`),
its box `{ x: -15, y: -15, w: 30, h: 30 }` a little wider than the present
(about 24 units by 27). The first opponent whose body touches it sets it
off: 5 at Base Launch 2, flung straight up (hitstun 0.45, hitstop 0.1),
and the present is gone. It is an attack like any projectile: a raised
Shield that touches it blocks it (nothing dealt, the Shield's 15 Energy,
the present gone), and a Deflect turns it into the deflecting fighter's
own present (it has no speed, so it stays where it is, waiting for #0015
now). Left alone, it is gone after six seconds.

`maxInPlay` is a shared projectile field
([combat: projectiles](../systems/combat.md#projectiles)), and #0015 is the
first to use it: a thrower never has more than `maxInPlay` of its move's
projectiles in play at once, and one more released takes the place of the
oldest still there, gone that step without striking. The move is its
attack family by codename (`moveFamily`), so the Gift's present and the
Air Gift's count together; only those #0015 left itself count (one turned
back is its deflector's). Both presents have `maxInPlay: 1`: one present at
a time, wherever it was left. A new one is never refused: the old one makes
room.

- **Gift** (`attack3`, 11 frames at 15 fps: files 1-5, 4 again, 6-10):
  its hands thrown up (1, 2), a kiss blown (3) and sent (4: the present
  appears at 3/15 s, 40 units before it and 16 up, its box clear of the
  floor), following it (5, then 4 again), its hands to its cheeks,
  giggling (6-9), and its stance (10): eleven fifteenths of a second, a
  long, deliberate pose. 15 Energy (the present, a construct). Ground only.
- **Air Gift** (`midair_attack3`, 7 frames at 15 fps): curled up in the
  air round a present (1-4), its arms flung out (5: the present appears at
  4/15 s, hanging 30 units before it at its waist), letting it go (6, 7):
  seven fifteenths of a second, its drift under the universal air control.
  15 Energy.

### The Pink Typhoon: a whirl that tears loose as a tornado

- **Pink Typhoon** (`attack4`, 19 frames at 30 fps: files 1-7, 4-7, 4,
  8-14): turned, its back to us, the hammer out behind it (1-3, a tenth of
  a second), then the whirl, twice round (4-7, 4-7): each turn its hammer

  { lift: 0 }`: whoever it catches takes #0015's own speed, none, and is
  held where it stands). Round once more (4) and the throw: as the hammer

  second (about 260 units), `{ x: -30, y: -40, w: 60, h: 80 }`, its swirl

The sheet draws one row for its whirl (the source game's Girl Tornado:
"frames 4-7 loop") and its tornado (its Pink Typhoon) and one for each in

### Mash Combo

Its pair is the **Girl Combo** (`attack1`) and the **Girl Swing**
(`extra_attack`), its two close-range strikes (out to 34 and 44 units).
Its Girl Crush is a heavy swing, its presents and tornadoes are
projectiles and constructs, so none could be a partner. Five rapid
presses of either one's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its
own launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its `mouvment` clip, its
sprint set off from its stance into long strides, its arms flung back (7
frames), shown once across the whole Dash, and its `midair_mouvment`,
from upright into a lunge, arms flung back and legs trailing (4 frames),
across the whole air dash. Its canon's speed is in its moves, never in
how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

| Move | Step-in | Its own fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Girl Combo, Girl Crush, Girl Swing | the shared one | — |
| Gift, Pink Typhoon | none (it lets something go) | the Pink Typhoon: `carry` |
| Girl Dunk | none (aerial, its own motion) | `motion: slam` with an `impact`, `airUses` 1, `airMomentum` 0.5 |
| Air Girl Crush, Air Gift, Air Pink Typhoon | none (aerial) | the Air Pink Typhoon: `carry` |
| Deflect | none (a defense) | — |

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', perfectWindow: 0.1,
perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: its arm raised to its face, then its palm thrust out
before it. The sheet draws no lowering, so there is no lowering clip: the
Shield goes down at once. In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect**
(its `deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -14, y: -50, w: 44, h: 52 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Tucked (the startup), then its knee driven up and out before it for two
frames (live: 14 units behind it to 30 in front, from its feet to its
head), held (its recovery): four fifteenths of a second, keeping the whole
of its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at
Base Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it
meets back at their thrower, now #0015's. No cooldown; starting it costs
nothing (never while exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back
costs 15 Energy, as every Deflect does. The knee is the sheet's dash
attack's leap, the one move of that row in the game.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Its four constructs, the Gift, the
Air Gift and both tornadoes, cost 15 each: a present and a tornado cost
it the same as a Dash. It leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12
a second.

Its `launchReaction`: small and light, it is flung a little longer, 0.15 s
per 1000 units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, and 20° of
steering. Its launches are as strong as anyone's, and it rebounds off the
scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its own).

canon.

quills would pull the automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque middle) off
its body, so every clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art pixels
from the left of each frame's visible art): the middle of its head, its
quills, which every frame draws over its body. Every frame stands on the
bottom of its art.

What of the sheet is no move: its turns, its stop and its turn while
running, its double jump (the jump is universal), its healing, its dash
attack's stance and crouch (its knee is the Deflect), the run-up to the
source game's aim attack, its hits against a wall and the ground, its
getting up, its blocked attack, its wall cling, its map walk and its
portraits; the first frame of its run (a start the run never needs), the
jab's and the straight's stances between blows, the hammer swings'
drawings repeated in a row, and the hearts of its blown kiss (drawn apart
from it, beside the body: the present is what the kiss leaves).

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
stance frame; an attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or the air dash

## Controller layout

Its recommended controller layout, authored in its definition
(`controller.layout`; see [input](../systems/input.md#game-controllers)).
The universal actions are everyone's: the left stick moves (and pushed
down in the air fast-falls), Cross / A jumps, R1 / RB Dashes, L1 / LB held
is its guard and pressed in the air the Deflect, Options / Menu pauses.
Every move of its own is one input, no combination.

## How it plays

A small bruiser with a trap. Up close, the Girl Combo and the Girl Swing
are its quick strings and its Mash Combo, both launching up; the Girl
Crush is its heavy blow, its sweep holding the target through the turn for
a smash that sends it flying sideways and shoves a Shield back. At range
the Pink Typhoon's tornado catches an opponent who stands or jumps short,
carries it and throws it up, its whirl first punishing anyone who rushes
in. The Gift changes the ground the fight is on: a present left in an
opponent's path, or hanging where it will jump, forces it to go round,
over, or through behind a Shield, and costs only one at a time. From above
the Girl Dunk spikes and its landing shakes the floor round it. Its
weaknesses: it is small but short of reach, its smash, its dive and its
tornado leave it open when they miss, every present and tornado costs
Energy it also needs to Dash and block, and a present can be popped
harmlessly by a raised Shield.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, a plunge with an
impact, a projectile that never moves, piercing tornadoes that pull), sees
a present on its own path as the threat it is, and uses every one of its
moves.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 336 a level): #0015 wins 0.53 at Medium, 0.51 at
Hard and 0.56 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range (0.43 to 0.63 over
the same kind of runs). At Hard: 12 of 24 against #0001, 10 against #0002,
19 against #0003, 19 against #0004, 10 against #0005, 6 against #0006, 15
against #0007, 10 against #0008, 8 against #0009, 10 against #0010, 10
against #0011, 15 against #0012, 13 against #0013 and 15 against #0014.
Its weakest matchups are #0006's and #0009's, whose ranged tools and wind
keep it from closing in (6 and 8 of 24); its best, #0003's and #0004's,
slower fighters its tornado and its presents catch on the ground (19).
Most of its damage comes from its Mash Combo and its Pink Typhoon; its
presents land rarely in CPU play, but turn an approach aside where they
wait.

Its first tuning won 0.51 at Medium, 0.59 at Hard and 0.69 at Brutal: at
Brutal its finishes came above all from the Air Girl Crush (19 of the
opponents' falls over 28 matches) and the Air Pink Typhoon's sideways
finisher (17), both at Base Launch 2, together an edge-guard few could
survive. Slowing its jab, weakening the present, the Girl Crush's smash or
the ground tornado, enlarging its hurtboxes or taking the tornadoes' pull
away changed nothing beyond the noise (0.68 to 0.78 over 168 Brutal
matches each); lowering either aerial finisher alone left it at 0.68.
Both at Base Launch 1 (a quick swing and a push out of the air that knock
away more than they throw): 0.56 at Brutal, and the numbers above. Its
ground tornado also runs a little slower and longer than it first did
(260 units/s for a second, not 300 for three quarters), so that its
finisher lands on a target it meets at range. The results swing by about
0.05 from one set of seeds to the next.

# #0001

#0001's own specification: the limitless sorcerer, a fighter of space. It
pulls, pushes, erases and cannot be touched. Its art, each of its moves,
its Shield and its Deflect, and how they play. It is part of
the product specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9):
the shared rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Its moves are mechanics, not poses with damage, built on features any
fighter can use ([combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):
the `hover` motion (it stands on the air while it strikes), pulls (an
attack's or a projectile's `pull` drags opponents in), repelling and
erasing projectiles (`repel`, `erase`), the shared hit effects
(`unblockable`, `paralyze`, `blockPush`), a piercing projectile, a homing
dash, techniques cast in place, a Shield that stalls the blows it
blocks (`defense.stall`), a Deflect that turns projectiles back
(`deflectProjectiles`) and orbs that spin (`rotationSpeed`).

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own (see
[`js/data/loadout.js`](../../js/data/loadout.js)): `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; and an extra attack. Transform is reserved.

| Button | On the ground | In the air |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attack1` (U) | **Jab**: a quick punch that pushes the target away and opens a follow-up once it has hit | **Floating Straight**: a lunging punch thrown standing on the air (no fall while it plays). Twice per airtime |
| `attack2` (I) | **Red**: a repelling orb from both palms. It pushes its target away, shoves a Shield that blocks it back, and turns the other fighter's projectiles around: they are #0001's from then on, flying back at their thrower | **Red Kick**: a lock-on flying kick. It hangs for 0.1 s, then flies at its opponent (within 230 units and not behind it) re-aimed every step, blasts it away and springs off what it met. Once per airtime |
| `attack3` (O) | **Maximum Blue**: an attracting orb that crawls forward, drags an opponent near it into itself and grinds it, then collapses, popping it upward. A trap, held back only by its own cast | **Blue**: an open palm that yanks an opponent near it in to the palm, then strikes it upward, standing on the air. Once per airtime |
| `attack4` (M) | **Unlimited Void** (a technique): half a second of cast, then a sure hit round #0001 that no Shield stops and that paralyzes its target for 1.7 s | — |
| `attack5` (,) | **Hollow Purple** (a technique): a five-sixths of a second chant, then a vast sphere that no Shield stops, erasing the projectiles it meets and flying on through whatever it strikes | — |
| `extra_attack` (J) | **High Kick**: a leap in and a high kick, #0001's launcher: telegraphed and punishable | **High Kick**, standing on the air while it kicks. Once per airtime |
| `shield` (L) | **Infinity**, its Shield (held) | **Deflect** (a fresh press): the arm sweep, a strike that turns the other fighter's projectiles back at their thrower |

```js
actions: {
  extra_attack: 'extra_attack',                          // the High Kick
  transform: null,                                       // reserved
  attack1: { ground: 'attack1', air: 'midair_attack1' }, // the Jab / the Floating Straight
  attack2: { ground: 'attack2', air: 'midair_attack2' }, // Red / the Red Kick
  attack3: { ground: 'attack3', air: 'midair_attack3' }, // Maximum Blue / Blue
  attack4: { type: 'technique', id: 'attack4' },         // Unlimited Void
  attack5: { type: 'technique', id: 'attack5' },         // Hollow Purple
},
```

| High Kick | 5 | 2 | vertical | |

Every hit is one of the four damage tiers ([combat](../systems/combat.md#damage)):
its solid strikes 3, the High Kick (its launcher) the heavy 5, Hollow
Purple the top 10, and Maximum Blue's grinding ticks 1. Retuned from
the old 2s (to 3), the High Kick's 4 (to 5) and Hollow Purple's 12 (to
10), keeping the order of the moves.

No move of #0001's has a cooldown: every attack, the Deflect, Unlimited
Void and Hollow Purple are `cooldown: 0`. What holds each back is its own
startup, active phase, recovery and hit-cancel, or a technique's cast and
release, which must play out (or be cut short by the shared rules)
before the next move starts.

### Each move in detail

- **Jab** (`attack1`, 6 frames at 20 fps, 0.3 s): frames 1-2 the wind-up,
  3-4 the punch (startup 0.1 s, active 0.1 s, its box out to 40 units at
  the shoulders), 5-6 back. 3, Base Launch 1 sideways, a two-step freeze;
  hit-cancel 0.1 s after it starts, so a Jab that hit flows into another
  Jab, the High Kick, Red, a jump or a Dash. Its own push ends a string of
  Jabs within a few. It keeps all the speed it is thrown at (a running
  Jab slides on with the run, a Dash Jab lunges), under 0.6 of the ground
  deceleration, never steered. Ground only, no cooldown.
- **Floating Straight** (`midair_attack1`, 5 frames at 20 fps): 0.1 s
  wind-up, the lunging punch for 0.1 s, 0.05 s back; motion `hover` (no
  fall while it plays), drifting on most of its speed with some steering
  (`airMomentum` 0.8, `airControl` 0.5): out of an air dash, a flying
  punch. 3, Base Launch 1 sideways. Twice per airtime, no cooldown.
- **Red** (`attack2`, 5 frames at 20 fps): frames 1-3 the hand sign, 4 the
  palms thrust out as the orb leaves them (`spawnAt` 0.15 s, offset 44
  forward, 70 up), 5 the lunge after. A cast, planted on purpose: it keeps
  half a run (`momentum` 0.5, `friction` 0.8). The orb (`attack2_object`): 600
  units/s for 0.5 s (about 300 units), a 32 × 32 box; 3, Base Launch 1
  sideways; a Shield that blocks it is shoved back 520 units/s (its guard
  holds, the ground under it does not); `repel`: another fighter's
  projectile it meets is turned back the way Red flies, now #0001's, its
  strikes starting over. No cooldown, ground only.
- **Red Kick** (`midair_attack2`, 4 frames at 30 fps): motion `homing`,
  range 230, speed 1050 for up to 0.22 s after a 0.1 s hang (tucked,
  rolling, laid out); 3, Base Launch 2 sideways; `blockPush` 400; it
  springs off what it met at 620 up and 240 back; a miss keeps 0.3 of its
  speed. Once per airtime, no cooldown.
- **Maximum Blue** (`attack3`, 5 frames at 15 fps): frames 1-4 Blue
  gathering in the raised hand, 5 the palm out as the orb leaves it
  (`spawnAt` 4/15 s, offset 78 forward, 69 up), planted on purpose
  (`momentum` 0.4). The orb (`attack3_object`):
  160 units/s for 1.5 s (about 240 units), a 64 × 64 box; `pull`: an
  opponent whose middle is within 120 units of it is dragged in at up to
  360 units/s; `pierce`: three strikes 0.25 s apart, two of 1 with no
  launch, then the collapse (`finisher`: 3, Base Launch 1 upward): 5 in
  all. A raised Shield is not pulled, and a strike it blocks ends the orb.
  No cooldown, ground only.
- **Blue** (`midair_attack3`, 4 frames at 15 fps): 1/15 s, then the palm
  thrust and the point for 2/15 s, 2/15 s back. While its active phase is
  open its `pull` draws an opponent within 170 units of the palm (50
  forward, 72 up) in at up to 1300 units/s, and its box (out to 60 units,
  head height) strikes whoever it brought: 3, Base Launch 1 upward. Motion
  `hover`. Once per airtime, no cooldown.
- **Unlimited Void** (`attack4`, a technique): the cast is `attack4_cast`
  (6 frames at 12 fps, 0.5 s: gathering itself, then the hand sign
  rising), the release `attack4_release` (1 frame held 1/3 s: a step
  forward, the sign held). On the release step its burst strikes every
  opponent its box meets: 250 units either side of #0001, from 210 units
  over its feet down to 20 below them (`{ -250, -210, 500, 230 }`). 3, no
  launch, `unblockable` (a raised Shield takes it in full and pays
  nothing), `paralyze` 1.7 s (the target cannot act, its sideways speed
  held at 0, until the hold runs out or a hit launches it). #0001 is free
  1/3 s later: about 1.4 s of an opponent that cannot move. No cooldown:
  it may be cast again the moment #0001 is free.
- **Hollow Purple** (`attack5`, a technique): the chant is `attack5_cast`
  (5 frames at 6 fps, 5/6 s, a deep stance: still its longest telegraph),
  the release `attack5_release`
  (1 frame held 1/3 s: both hands thrust out). On its first release step
  the sphere (`attack5_object`) leaves the hands (95 forward, 60 up): 640
  units/s for 1.8 s, a 116 × 116 box; 10 (the top damage tier), Base
  Launch 3 sideways, `unblockable`; `erase`: a projectile of another
  fighter's it meets is gone, and it flies on through every fighter it
  strikes, each once. No cooldown: its 5/6 s chant is its price.
- **High Kick** (`extra_attack`, 5 frames at 12 fps): frame 1, the leap in
  on frame 2 (forward speed raised to at least 300 on the ground), 3 the
  leg rising, 4 the kick (startup 0.25 s, active 1/12 s, its box out to 58
  units, chest to head height), 5 the knee drawn back, held 1/6 s. 5 (the
  heavy tier), Base Launch 2 upward. It keeps the speed it is thrown at. Hit-cancel as
  it lands. In the air its motion `hover` holds it on the air while it
  kicks; once per airtime. No cooldown. The one move that kept its old
  rate: a launcher has to be read, and any quicker the Jab strings into it
  far longer (measured: see Balance).

A technique needs the ground from its first cast frame to its last, and a
hit before it lets go breaks it (nothing released; with no cooldown, it
may be cast again once #0001 is free): see [combat:
techniques](../systems/combat.md).

Each is a true combo (the target never free to act between the hits) on a

| --- | --- |

| High Kick, jump, Floating Straight | 30 |

## Movement

None of its own: #0001 runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
#0002 does. It brings the art: its Dash is its one-frame `mouvment` clip
(the long, low leap), shown across the whole Dash; its air dash its
one-frame `midair_mouvment` clip (the same flying leap, its own file),
shown across the whole air dash. Its reach is in its techniques.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is **Infinity**, a Shield all round it, on the ground:

```js
defense: {
  type: 'shield',
  groundAnimation: 'shielding',
  perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25,
  stall: 0.25,
},
```

The arms crossed. A melee blow it blocks stalls in it: the attacker
freezes for 0.25 s (its own hitstop if longer), time for #0001 to let go
and punish; a projectile's, a clone's or a technique's hit stalls nothing.
Its perfect Shield opens for 0.1 s after 0.25 s down: no blockstun, for
the same 15 Energy as any block. There is no Infinity in the air.

In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), the arm sweep:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 2 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: 6, y: -118, w: 44, h: 98 },
  directionalLaunch: 'vertical', hitstun: 0.32, blockstun: 0.14, hitstop: 0.05,
  cooldown: 0, airMomentum: 1, airControl: 0.4,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the arm drawn across the body, then the palm thrust out and swept
up over the head (frames 2 and 3, live), frame 4 the arm raised, held a
frame longer: a third of a second (its own timing, unchanged by the faster
game), falling as it sweeps and carrying the whole of its drift (a Deflect
redirects projectiles, never the body). While it is live
its box (the whole front the arm sweeps, from the waist to well over the
head, out to 50 units) strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2,
here upward, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at
their thrower, now #0001's. No cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts,
as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 25, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15
(a perfect block's included); Combat Assist costs nothing. Its own is
only its refill, 14 / s (`energy: { regen: 14 }`). Its
`launchReaction`: 0.2 s more stun per 1000 units/s up to 0.7 s, tumbling
from 1100 units/s, and a held direction bends a launch by up to 18° (it
reads a launch a little better than most).

## Art and animations

`#0001` frames live in `assets/characters/0001/`: 78 frames cut from one
supplied sprite sheet, each cropped tight to its art with the sheet's flat
navy background (33, 31, 63) made transparent and nothing else changed
(1×, 8-bit RGBA, one file pixel per art pixel; the visual config forces
`pixelSize: 1`, so no grid is looked for). Four idle frames (standing
easy, 24 × 62–63, the reference: 63 art pixels), eight run frames (the
walk, ≤ 30 × 61), two jump frames (≤ 35 × 66), two fall (≤ 41 × 70), two
land (≤ 35 × 59), one `mouvment` (Dash) frame (the low leap, 61 × 40) and
one `midair_mouvment` (air dash) frame (the same leap, its own file: the
sheet's one flying pose), two hurt (≤ 37 × 60) and one mid-air hurt
(38 × 51), the `shielding` pose (27 × 55), four `deflect` frames (the arm
across, the palm out, the sweep with its trail, the arm raised; ≤ 37 × 69,
cut from the last row of the sheet), six attack1 frames (the Jab), five
midair_attack1 (the Floating Straight), five attack2 (Red's hand sign and
release), four midair_attack2 (the Red Kick), five attack3 (Maximum Blue),
four midair_attack3 (Blue), seven attack4 frames (Unlimited Void: six cast
poses and the release), six attack5 (Hollow Purple: five chant poses and
the release), five extra_attack (the High Kick), and one frame each for
the three orbs it throws: `attack2_object` (Red, 30 × 33),
`attack3_object` (Maximum Blue, 52 × 55) and `attack5_object` (the purple
sphere, 89 × 78). Every clip is its own files, named for it; the two
technique clips of each technique share their codename's files (the cast
plays frames 1 to 6 of `attack4`, the release frame 7). Everything faces
right; the orbs are round, so never mirrored (`sourceFacing: 0`): instead
each spins as it flies, six whole turns a second (`rotationSpeed: 2160`,
art only; see [rendering](../systems/rendering.md#projectile-spin)).

Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 63, so every frame
normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height`
(63 × 88 / 52 ≈ 106.6 world units) puts #0001's art pixels at the roster's
common size (88 / 52 units each): a tall fighter, a head taller than
#0002.

The idle, run, jump, fall, land, Shield, Dash, air dash and Red Kick poses
use the automatic torso anchor. Where an arm thrust forward, a glow in the
hand, a sweep's trail or a deep lunge would drag it off the body, a clip
authors its own `anchorX` (art pixels from the left of each frame's
visible art): on the black shirt, which stays over the hips whatever the
arms do, for the Jab, the Floating Straight, Red, Maximum Blue, Blue, both
techniques, the hurt pose and the Deflect (11 in all four frames: the body
holds still, only the arm moves); the High Kick on its planted foot. Every pose stands on the bottom
of its art.

#0001's clips play at their own rates (`FPS_0001` in its definition):
Idle 8 fps, Run 16 (following the speed, from 0.6 of it up to 1.6 of it on
a Dash's run-on), Jump 15, Fall 12, Land 20 (a pose only), Dash and air
dash 6 (each shown across its whole Dash), Hurt 12, Shield 12, Deflect 15,
the Jab and the Floating Straight 20, Red 20, the Red Kick 30, Maximum
Blue and Blue 15, Unlimited Void's cast 12 and its release 3, Hollow
Purple's chant 6 and its release 3, the High Kick 12. Every attack's rate
is a whole number of 60 Hz steps per frame. If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it
holds its first idle frame; an attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or
the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## Balance

Its tuning came out of seeded CPU-vs-CPU fights against #0002 (one-minute
fights on Desert, Void falls #0001 : #0002, 200 fights per level, 100
each way round). With universal movement and the faster game: Easy
130 : 7, Medium 94 : 111, Hard 103 : 119, Brutal 114 : 81; with its High
Kick quickened like its other moves, #0002 fell far more (Hard 50 : 175),
so the kick kept its quarter-second startup. Before universal movement,
with the Deflect and the air dash: Easy 109 : 1, Medium 111 : 60, Hard
81 : 45, Brutal 82 : 93. Without an air Shield, and with
#0002's Deflect turning its orbs back at it, #0001 falls more often than it
did (Hard 49 : 70 in the same fights before); turning off #0002's
projectile catching alone brings Hard back to about even (65 : 66). At Easy
#0002's Whirlwind carries a slow-reacting #0001 off the stage far more
often than the other way round. The measurements, and the first cut's and
its fixes, are in [`UPDATES.md`](../../UPDATES.md#deflect-and-air-dash).
All of them predate the shared damage tiers, the universal Energy costs
and the removal of #0001's cooldowns (Hollow Purple 12 → 10, the High
Kick 4 → 5, its 2s → 3, no cooldown on any move), and have not been
measured again since.

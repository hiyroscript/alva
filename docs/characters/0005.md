# #0005

#0005's own specification: the dancing clown. Its art, each of its moves,
its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is an ancient
shapeshifter wearing a clown's shape to hunt: its claws and its teeth are a
predator's, its head stretches out on a long neck to bite, its maw opens
onto lights that leave whoever looks into them floating helpless, it is
never only where it stands (a bunch of red balloons is all the warning
before it bites from behind), and fear is what it feeds on. That is in its
moves (a float, a fear that feeds it, a gaze, a pull, a phantom that
appears behind its prey), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes
exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is a mechanic of its own, not just a pose and damage. Four of
them are features #0005 brought to the shared system, any fighter's to use
([combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):

- **The float** (`float: seconds`, a hit effect): a real hit that launches
  nothing makes its target float for that long. It is held exactly as a
  paralysis holds it (no acting, no sideways speed, its hurt pose), but
  lifted off the ground and rising slowly, `FLOAT_RISE_SPEED` (42 units/s)
  with no gravity, until it runs out and the fighter falls; a hit that
  launches it ends it at once. A blocked hit floats nobody. A float is an
  exceptional effect: its move waits the policy's 3 s.
- **The fear** (`fear: seconds`, a hit effect): a real hit frightens its
  target for that long, by its attacker. Until it runs out, every real hit
  the frightener lands on it (a strike, a projectile, a clone's, a burst)
  feeds the frightener: the hit lands in full, and its damage also comes off
  the frightener's own Launch Point (never below 0). The frightened fighter
  moves, acts and Shields as ever; a blocked hit frightens and feeds
  nobody. A trembling drop of cold sweat floats over a frightened fighter,
  and the frightener glows as each hit feeds it. Exceptional: 3 s.
- **The gaze** (`gaze: true`, a hit effect): the hit has to be seen. It
  reaches only a fighter facing where it comes from; one with its back
  turned is passed by with nothing used up, as a quake passes a fighter off
  the ground. A Shield raised toward it blocks it as any hit. Routine: it
  earns no cooldown.
- **A front-only pull** (`pull: { ..., front: true }`): a pull that reaches
  only the side its attack faces (or its projectile flies): an opponent
  behind it is left where it is.

It is also the roster's first fighter with a **summon** (a clone that
appears behind its target and performs an attack of its own:
[combat: summons](../systems/combat.md#summons)). It uses strikes (`hits`),
the `hover` motion, `airUses` and a Deflect that turns projectiles back
(`deflectProjectiles`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): an ancient, shapeshifting
predator that takes the shape of a dancing clown to lure its prey; it feeds
on fear, frightening its victims before it feeds (fear, it says, seasons the
meat); its true self is a set of lights within it, and whoever looks into
them is left catatonic, floating in the air; it can be anywhere, appearing
where it likes with its red balloons as the herald; its head and jaws
stretch far beyond a human's, its mouth opening onto rows of teeth; its
hands end in claws; its shape gives way to the dark that pours out of it.
So: the Deadlights are the lights, and floating is what they do, and only
to whoever looks (its gaze: turn away, and they do nothing); the Red
Balloon is its appearing behind its prey, out of a bunch of balloons, and
the fear it leaves feeds it; the Long Bite and Snapping Jaws are its
stretching jaws; the Claw Swipe and Claw Rake its claws; Phantom Hands and
Floating Hands its white-gloved hands bursting out of nowhere (it floats as
it does); Dark Tendrils the dark pouring out of its maw to drag its prey
in. Nothing it does is a power its canon does not give it: no projectile,
no heal of its own outside feeding on fear.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` a technique and
`attack5` a summon (both ground only); and an extra attack. It throws no
projectile. The sheet's intro, walk, victory dance and defeat poses, its
transformations (the spider and the giant face), its detached head, the
rising bite and its getting up off the ground are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Deadlights (whoever
faces them floats helpless) and the Red Balloon (a phantom that bites from
behind and leaves its prey afraid, feeding #0005 on every hit while the
fear lasts). Both sit under Special abilities in the controller editor, and
an orange exclamation mark floats over #0005 while it casts the Deadlights
(0.4 s + 1/3 s) or gathers its phantom (its third of a second of startup).
Dark Tendrils pull, but are an ordinary ground attack to the player.

### Each move in detail

- **Claw Swipe** (`attack1`, 3 frames at 12 fps, 0.25 s): the claw drawn
  back (frame 1), the swipe (frame 2, live: its arc in front, `{ x: 4, y:
  -84, w: 56, h: 56 }`), the claw raised (frame 3). 3, Base Launch 1
  sideways, hitstun 0.3. Hit-cancel from frame 3 once it has hit; it keeps
  all the speed it starts with, under 0.6 of the ground deceleration.
  Ground only.
- **Claw Rake** (`midair_attack1`, 5 frames at 15 fps, 1/3 s): drifting and
  the claws raised (frames 1-2), the arc swept from over its back down under
  it (frame 3, `{ -56, -64, 108, 72 }`; 1, no launch, hitstun 0.3: it holds
  for the next), the arc through the front, low (frame 4, `{ 4, -104, 56,
  106 }`; 3, Base Launch 1 downward: off the stage, a spike), the claws held
  out (frame 5). Steers half as well as it drifts; hit-cancel from frame 5.
- **Long Bite** (`attack2`, 13 frames at 15 fps, 0.87 s): crouched, its head
  shooting out from its face on its white neck (frames 1-4, 4/15 s), then
  four strikes all along the neck's length, from just in front of its face
  out to the jaws, `{ x: 16, y: -84, w: 182, h: 64 }` (the jaws close on
  whatever the head met on its way out, right up close as well as far
  away): the bite as they open
  (frame 5) and two chews (frames 6-7), 1 each, no launch, hitstun 0.3 (its
  prey held in the jaws), the last chew (frame 8): 5, Base Launch 3
  sideways, hitstun 0.42, a freeze of 0.08 s. The jaws shut and the neck
  drawn back along the frames it went out on (frames 9-13, 1/3 s). 8 in
  all: only the 0.5 s repeat delay. Ground only, no follow-up.
- **Snapping Jaws** (`midair_attack2`, 10 frames at 20 fps, 0.5 s): the head
  out from its face (frames 1-2), the bite (frame 3) and a chew (frame 5), 1
  each, the last chew (frame 7): 3, Base Launch 2 sideways, all along the
  shorter neck from just in front of its face out to the jaws, `{ 16, -84,
  102, 66 }`; closed and drawn back (frames 8-10). Steers 0.3.
- **Phantom Hands** (`attack3`, 7 frames at 12 fps, 0.58 s): the hands drawn
  back behind it (frame 1, two frames), four bursts of them in front (frames
  2-5, `{ 26, -106, 90, 96 }`), 1, 1, 1 holding the target, the last 3 at
  Base Launch 2 straight up; a hand held out (frame 6). Ground only. A Shield
  that blocks a burst stops the string.
- **Floating Hands** (`midair_attack3`, 7 frames at 12 fps, 0.58 s): `hover`
  (it stands on the air throughout), a hand drawn back (frames 1-2), four
  bursts (frames 3-6, `{ 24, -126, 108, 104 }`), 1, 1, 1, then 3 at Base
  Launch 2 sideways; a hand held out (frame 7). Once per airtime (`airUses:
  1`); steers 0.3.
- **Deadlights** (`attack4`, a technique): the cast is `attack4_cast` (2
  frames at 5 fps, 0.4 s: the fists raised, then the face splitting open
  onto rows of teeth), the release `attack4_release` (1 frame at 3 fps, 1/3
  s: the lights burning in its maw). On the release's first step the burst,
  `{ x: 0, y: -130, w: 190, h: 140 }` in front of #0005 (from the feet to
  well over the head): 1, no launch, hitstun 0.3, `float` 1.6, `gaze`. Only
  a fighter facing #0005 is met: one with its back turned is untouched. A
  raised Shield blocks it: no float. The policy's 3 s from the press (a
  float), spent even if a hit breaks the cast.
- **Red Balloon** (`attack5`, a summon): its startup `attack5_summon` (4
  frames at 12 fps, 1/3 s: its face swallowed by black, the maw gaping, a
  dark haze flaring), #0005 standing still and breakable by any hit; then
  its phantom (a clone of #0005, drawn with its own art) appears 60 units
  behind the opponent (`behindDistance: 60`), facing its back, through its
  cloud (`attack5_object`: a single red balloon on its string, then a whole
  bunch, 2 frames at 6 fps, 1/3 s each way, centred 65 units up), and
  performs `attacks.attack5`, the bite (5 frames at 12 fps, 5/12 s: the
  fists raised, the face splitting, a ghostly maw gaping out of it, snapping
  shut at frame 4, live `{ x: 22, y: -100, w: 76, h: 92 }`, then shut): 3,
  Base Launch 1 along the phantom's facing (toward #0005), hitstun 0.4,
  `fear` 2.5. No button of #0005's performs that bite itself. #0005 is free
  as soon as the phantom is sent: whatever it does, the phantom bites on its
  own. The opponent moving away makes it whiff; a Shield blocks it from
  behind as from anywhere (no fear). No opponent in play and the press does
  nothing. The policy's 3 s from the press (its bite's fear), more than the
  fear lasts: the fear cannot be kept up without a gap.
- **Dark Tendrils** (`extra_attack`, 9 frames at 15 fps, 0.6 s): the
  tendrils pouring out of its maw (frames 1-4, 4/15 s), at full length
  (frame 5, three frames): whoever they meet first, `{ x: 24, y: -100, w:
  300, h: 80 }`, is seized (1, no launch, hitstun 0.75), and while they are
  out every opponent in front of #0005 whose middle is within 280 units of
  its maw (40 ahead, 55 up) is reeled in toward it at up to 1100 units/s
  (`pull`, `front: true`: one behind it is left where it is; a raised Shield
  holds its ground and blocks the seizing); snapped back in (frames 3 and
  1, 2/15 s). Whoever they reel in from the far end is still rushing in as
  they let go: it slides on and stops against #0005's body, never shoving
  #0005 back (a pull never shoves its owner,
  [combat](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)).
  It roots #0005 where it stands: none of the speed it was moving at is
  kept (`momentum: 0`), so pressed on the run it neither slides on nor
  back. Hit-cancel from the end of the reel-in once it has seized. Ground
  only. Its hold is long enough for the Deadlights to catch the prey
  still held, if it faced #0005: a player's own press cuts the tendrils
  short at once, a CPU's once they are back.

Cooldowns are the shared cooldown policy's ([combat](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)),
never authored in #0005's definition. The Deadlights' float and the Red
Balloon's fear are exceptional: 3 s each, from the press. Every other move
(the Claw Swipe and Claw Rake, the Long Bite and Snapping Jaws, Phantom and
Floating Hands, Dark Tendrils) has only the universal 0.5-second repeat
delay: none deals 10 (the Long Bite's strikes add up to 8, both barrages' to 6,
Snapping Jaws' to 5). Its Deflect has
none; running, jumps and the Shield are exempt. Dash and air dash each have
a separate 0.5-second timer. Ground only: the Claw Swipe, the Long Bite,
Phantom Hands, Dark Tendrils, the technique and the summon.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. Its movement is in its moves: Floating Hands'
hover, the Long Bite's reach, the pull that brings its prey to it. It brings
the art: its two-frame `mouvment` clip (a crouch, then a head-first lunge,
speed lines streaming off it) played once across the whole Dash, and its
two-frame `midair_mouvment` (its sprint's long, flat leap) across the whole
air dash.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: black tendrils
sprouting from its mouth (two frames), fanned out before its face (held),
withdrawn (two frames). In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
the claw swept round:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -4, y: -100, w: 56, h: 104 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  airMomentum: 1, airControl: 0.4,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the claw raised high behind it, then the arc (frame 2, two frames,
live: the front of the body from over the head to the feet, out to 52
units), the claw thrust out a frame more: a little over a quarter of a
second, keeping the whole of its drift. While it is live its box strikes
for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other
fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now #0005's. No
cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts, as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 25, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15;
Combat Assist costs nothing. Its own is only its refill, 14 / s
(`energy: { regen: 14 }`). Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000 units/s up
to 0.55 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, 20° of steering.

Where the sheet repeats a drawing within a move (the stance's two
alternating drawings, the Long Bite's neck drawn back along the frames it
went out on, a chew held, the tendrils snapped back), the clip plays the
same file again: one file on disk. Across moves the sheet draws some poses

release; the sprint is the air dash): each is cut from its own section,
under its own codename. Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over
64, so every frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and
`visual.height` (64 × 88 / 52 ≈ 108.3 world units) puts #0005's art pixels at
the roster's common size.

Its claws, its stretching neck, its tendrils and the ghostly hands reach
far from its body, and the automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque middle)
would follow them off it, so every clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`,
art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art): the middle of its
body's own colours (its olive suit and its orange hair, which no effect
shares) over the upper part of the body, wherever its arms or its head go
(the Long Bite: 29, 28.5, 28.5, 28.5, 28, 28, 28, 28, 28, then back along the
same). Where an arc sweeps below the feet, the clip says where the feet are
(`anchorY`, art pixels down from the top): the Claw Rake (61, 62, 66, 64, 64)
and the Deflect (51, 57, 57, 42). Everything else stands on the bottom of
its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's or a summon's clip, the Shield, the
Deflect, the Dash, the air dash or the balloons' art whose frames are
missing is refused.

glyph, named and drawn as Deflect while #0005 is in the air.

## How it plays

A predator of reach and setups. Its Long Bite outreaches everything in the
roster, up close as well as far away, and is long to pull back; Dark Tendrils
reach further still and drag whoever they seize to its feet, where the
Claw Swipe, Phantom Hands' upward throw or the Deadlights wait. The
Deadlights are its turn of the tide: an opponent caught facing them floats
helpless for long enough to take a launch, but they fail on a turned back
and the cast can be seen coming. The Red Balloon sends a phantom behind the
opponent: it pushes its prey toward #0005 and leaves it afraid, and for the
fear's two and a half seconds every hit #0005 lands takes its own damage
back off #0005's Launch Point. In the air its claws drive opponents down
and its hands let it float in place. Its weaknesses are its size (the
tallest body in the roster, easy to hit) and its commitments: the Long Bite
and the Deadlights are slow to recover from, and a blocked one leaves it
open.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): it weighs a
float as the hold it is, values a fear by the seconds it buys and every hit
while it lasts by what it feeds, casts the Deadlights only where an
opponent facing it will be in reach, sends its phantom from afar, and
weighs what reeling an opponent exposed by the ledge back to safety with its
tendrils would cost it.
Frightened itself, it keeps just out of its opponent's reach until the fear
runs out.

## Balance

Tuned from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches (full Watch Mode battles, first to 3,
on Desert and City, five seeds each way round: 20 matches per cell; points
#0005 : opponent). Against #0001: Medium 35 : 47, Hard 37 : 49, Brutal
37 : 48. Against #0002: Medium 43 : 46, Hard 44 : 45, Brutal 45 : 42.
Against #0003: Medium 54 : 36, Hard 38 : 46, Brutal 42 : 41. Against #0004:
Medium 59 : 15, Hard 48 : 39, Brutal 47 : 37. 529 : 491 in all: even at
Brutal (171 : 168), a little behind at Hard (167 : 179) and ahead at Medium
(191 : 144), where a looser CPU walks into its long bites more often;
weakest against #0001, strongest against #0004.

As first written it scored 380 : 597 on the same seeds (Hard 110 : 213):
almost every hit it dealt was a 1-damage hold, and its Long Bite and
Snapping Jaws bit only far out (120 to 198 and 64 to 118 units), useless up
close and punished on more than half of their starts. Heavier finishers (5
on Phantom Hands and Snapping Jaws), a quicker Long Bite (20 fps) and a
higher Energy refill and launch steering made up for it (510 : 496). Then
both bites were made to bite all along the neck, from right in front of
its face (the owner's report: useless when super close), and Dark Tendrils
to root #0005 where it stands. With the dead zones gone the heavier
finishers were no longer needed: at 5 they made it 581 : 431 (and a 20 fps
Long Bite 597 : 422), so every finisher is back at its first value and the
Long Bite at 15 fps (0.87 s, slow for a move that reaches everything).
What stays of the tuning: an Energy refill of 14 (from the common 12) and
20° of launch steering (from 15). Floating Hands at 5 had also broken the
CPU's match-level behaviour scenarios
([tests/systems/cpu-behaviour.test.mjs](../../tests/systems/cpu-behaviour.test.mjs)):
an opponent breaking off to recover from #0005 did not come back in time.
A faster Deadlights cast (1/3 s), a longer float (2 s) and a quicker Energy
refill (16) were tried too and did not help. Before all that, while its
moves were being built, the fear was cut from 4 s to 2.5 (with a 3 s
cooldown, 4 s could be kept up for a whole fight) and the Deadlights' cast
from 0.5 s to 0.4.

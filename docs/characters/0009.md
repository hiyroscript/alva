# #0009

#0009's own specification: the young monk of the four elements. Its art,
each of its moves, its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its
fantasy is a boy monk raised among air nomads, the one person alive who
can bend all four elements, who would rather move an opponent than hurt
one: wind that blows its target away and blows shots back, water that
freezes, rock that rises out of the ground, fire and a wooden staff up
close, and a glowing trance that comes over it only in mortal danger.
That is in its moves (a gale that carries, a gust that floats it, a
freeze, a pillar of rock that walls off shots, a leap on the air, a move
that opens only in danger) and in how far it steers its own launches,
never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other
fighter does. It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: Swift
Strikes and the Fire Kick are strings (`hits`) that hold their target for
the last blow, the Fire Kick's fire sweeping round its feet on both sides;
the Air Blast is a cone of wind that drags whoever it meets along with it
before flinging it (`pierce` with `carry`) and blows other fighters' shots
back at them (`repel`); in the air the Air Gust pushes and blows shots back
while #0009 floats (`motion: hover`); the Rising Air Kick rides the air up
(`motion: rise`); the Earth Pillar is a column of rock that stands where it
rises, throwing up only a fighter standing on the ground there (`quake`)
and stopping every shot that meets it (`erase`); the Freezing Splash freezes
(`paralyze`); the Air Sphere, its trance, opens only in danger
(`minLaunchPoint`, a capability any technique may now use) and bursts all
round it; and a held direction bends its launches further than anyone's
(`launchReaction.steerAngle`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a boy monk of the air
nomads, the one person in each generation who masters all four elements,
air first (his own people's), then water, earth and fire, each learned
from a master. He fights the way his people taught him: evasive, light on
his feet and on the wind, and unwilling to hurt anyone he can simply move
out of the way. He blasts opponents away with gusts of air and blows
attacks back at them, rides the air to leap far higher than anyone,
spins his wooden staff to fend off what is thrown at him and swings it to
cut the air, draws water up and freezes it round his enemies, raises
columns of earth out of the ground to throw foes and wall off attacks,
and kicks fire. In mortal danger a glowing trance comes over him that
gives him the power of every life before his, and in it he sits inside a
sphere of air. He heals nobody (that is another's gift, not his), summons
nobody and carries no weapon but his staff. So: its moves are its four
elements and its staff; its guard is its arms raised before it; its
Deflect its staff spun over its head; its greatest move opens only in
danger; and nothing it does is a power its canon does not give it.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; and an extra attack, its Earth Pillar (ground only). The
sheet's staff on its own, its vaulting staff kick, its jumping uppercut,
its low staff sweep and the air blades of it, the air blades of its
aerial swing, its tornado, its water spout and its wave, its kicked
boulder, its low slab of rock (only the tall pillar is a frame, below),
its stream and its pillar of fire, the wind trailing from its trance, its
burning, falls and getting up, and its wins and losses are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Freezing Splash, a
freeze that holds its target helpless for a follow-up (as #0006's Ice
Breath is Special), and the Air Sphere, its trance, a 10-damage burst that
opens only in danger. Both sit under Special abilities in the controller
editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats over #0009 while it performs
either (the Freezing Splash 7/15 s + 0.5 s, the Air Sphere 7/12 s +
0.5 s). The Air Blast's drag and turned-back shots, the Air Gust and the
Earth Pillar's wall are ordinary to the player.

### Only in danger

The Air Sphere is the roster's first move that opens only in danger: its
technique's `minLaunchPoint` is 30 ([combat:
techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)). While #0009's own Launch
Point is under 30, a press of it does nothing at all (nothing paid,
nothing kept for later), the CPU never plans it, and the ability HUD shows
its tile held back (`danger`: "Only in danger: more Launch Point needed").
From 30 on, it casts as any technique does, and once cast it plays out
whatever happens to #0009's Launch Point; its 5 s cooldown runs from its
release as ever. Launch Point recovers over time, so the trance closes
again once #0009 has gone long enough without being hit. The rule is the
shared technique runtime's (`inDanger` in
[`technique.js`](../../js/game/combat/technique.js)), read by the Fighter's
press, the CPU (`moveReady`) and the HUD (`moveRestriction`) alike: any
fighter whose canon has a power that wakes only in danger may use it.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Swift Strikes** (`attack1`) and the **Fire Kick**
(`attack2`), its two close-range strikes (out to 60 and 62 units). The Air
Blast and the Earth Pillar reach far ahead, and its techniques are no
strikes of the body, so none could be a partner. Five rapid presses of
either one's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its own
launch (48 at the most over the series).

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its two-frame `mouvment`
clip, a low lunge, then speed lines streaming off it, played once across
the whole Dash, and its one-frame `midair_mouvment`, a flat leap forward
arms first, across the whole air dash. Its canon's lightness is in its
moves (the Rising Air Kick's lift, the Air Gust's float) and in how far it
steers a launch, never in how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: squared up
(one frame), its arms brought up across its body (one), lowered (one). In
the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), its staff
twirled over its head:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -40, y: -112, w: 80, h: 114 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the startup, then the whirl (frames 2-3, live: all round its body
from the feet up into the whirl over its head, 40 units either side), its
recovery a frame more: a little over a quarter of a second, keeping the
whole of its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3
at Base Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles
it meets back at their thrower, now #0009's. No cooldown; it costs 15
Energy as it starts, as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15,
and its three construct moves 15 each (the Air Blast, the Air Gust, the
Earth Pillar): its wind and its rock share one bar with its Dashes. It
leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a second.

Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from
1150 units/s, and **30° of steering**, the roster's most (everyone else 15°
to 20°): light on the wind as every air nomad is, a held direction bends a
launch further, so it can ride a blow toward the stage or away from a
ledge. Its launches are as strong as anyone's (the steering never changes
their speed), and it rebounds off the scenery as everyone does (no
`launchBounce` of its own).

float above the ground). Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame
over 49, so every frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel,
and `visual.height` (49 × 88 / 52 ≈ 82.9 world units) puts #0009's art
pixels at the roster's common size.

Its staff reaches far behind and in front of it, its water far ahead, its
fire round its feet and its whirl over its head, and the automatic anchor
(the upper art's opaque middle) would follow them off its body, so every
clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each
frame's visible art): the middle of its trousers, the colours none of its
staff, its fire, its water, its wind or the speed lines of its blows
shares (the Staff Swing: 41, 44.5, 14, 14, 9; the splash: 19, 19, 18, 19,
19.5, 19.5). Everything stands on the bottom of its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

An elusive elementalist that would rather move an opponent than hurt one.
Up close, Swift Strikes and the Fire Kick are its quick strings and its
Mash Combo; a step further out the Air Blast drags its target away before
flinging it, blowing any shot coming the other way back at its thrower,
and the Earth Pillar throws up whoever stands ahead and walls off shots
while it stands. In the air the Staff Swing reaches far, the Rising Air
Kick lifts it, and the Air Gust floats it while it pushes. The Freezing
Splash freezes a target for a follow-up, and once it is in danger its
trance bursts round it for 10. Its weaknesses: its wind and its rock spend
the same Energy as its Dashes, its two techniques are long, rooted casts
any hit breaks, it is light and easily knocked about, and its strongest
move asks it to survive into danger first.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, a carrying
gale, a hover, a lift, a freeze, a shot that stands where it rises and
reaches only a fighter on the ground, a long cast) and uses every one of
them; its trance it plans only once it is in danger, by the same
`inDanger` as a player's press.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24
matches against each opponent, 192 a level): #0009 wins 0.57 at Medium,
0.45 at Hard and 0.48 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range (the
other fighters 0.43 to 0.63 over the same kind of runs). At Hard: 9 of 24
against #0001, 12 against #0002, 14 against #0003, 13 against #0004, 13
against #0005, 7 against #0006, 8 against #0007 and 10 against #0008.
Strongest against #0003 and #0004; weakest against #0006, whose beam,
scream and breath keep it out, and #0007, whose shots and fire meet its
wind on equal terms. Its CPU uses its Mash Combo most, then the Air
Blast, the Fire Kick and the Staff Swing; the Earth Pillar two or three
times a match, and its trance in about two matches of every three. The results swing by
about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the next.

As first written it won 0.83 at Medium, 0.79 at Hard and 0.70 at Brutal,
far too strong: its Air Blast came out in a fifth of a second, flew 364
units and struck four times for 6 (its CPU fired it about 36 times a
match), and its strikes came out faster than anyone's (the jab at 0.05 s,
the fire sweep at 1/15 s). The Air Blast now takes 4/15 s to come out,
flies 280 units at 400 units/s and strikes three times for 5, the last
gust flinging: it moves its target more than it hurts it. Swift Strikes'
jab comes at 0.1 s, as #0008's does, and the Fire Kick's sweep at 2/15 s.
With the Air Blast's last gust at 1 (3 in all) it fell to 0.49, 0.33 and
0.48; its last gust at 3 brought it to 0.56, 0.43 and 0.42; and the Earth
Pillar, its least-used move until then, at 5 rather than 3 (a heavy blow
of rock, only for a fighter standing on the ground) to 0.57, 0.45 and
0.48.

# #0012

#0012's own specification: the cyborg ninja in silver armour with a
green-lit visor. Its art, each of its moves, its guard and its Deflect, and
how they play. Its fantasy is a swordsman rebuilt with a machine body: a
sword whose edge glows green, shuriken thrown from its wrist, a blade that
turns shots back, a dash that crosses a stage in one cut, and the dragon
spirit of its blade, woken for one devastating cleave. That is in its moves
(a volley of three shuriken, a stance that turns shots back, a dash that
cuts through its opponent, a 10-damage cleave all round it), never its
locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does.
It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage:
Shuriken throws three in a row (`volley`, a capability any attack may now
use: one press, one Energy cost, and a Shield that blocks one stops the
rest); the Deflect Stance turns back every shot that meets its blade
(`deflectProjectiles`) and gives way to any move; Swift Strike dashes
through its opponent (`motion: roll`, `passThrough`) and the Rising Strike
dashes straight up through it (`motion: rise`, once per airtime); Twin
Cuts is a string (`hits`) whose chop holds its target for the rising cut;
the Whirlwind Cut's three strikes are where its sweep is, ahead, behind and
over it; the Falling Cut spikes straight down; and Dragonblade is a cast
whose burst all round it deals the roster's top tier, 10, as its dragon
spirit coils round it.

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a swordsman whose body
was rebuilt by machines, it fights with a sword and a short blade, throws
shuriken from its wrist (three at a time, in a line), turns incoming shots
back with its blade, dashes forward in one cut through everyone in its way
(aimed any way it likes, upward too), climbs walls and jumps twice, and
for its greatest strike draws the sword that carries a dragon's spirit and
cleaves everything before it. It heals nobody, summons nobody, never homes
in and throws nothing but its shuriken. So: its moves are its blade, its
shuriken, its Deflect, its dash and its Dragonblade; its guard is its
blade held across; its Deflect, in the air, its canon's own whirl of the
blade; its Dash a blur of speed. Its double jump is everyone's triple jump
(movement is universal), and the sheet draws no wall climbing.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack4`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack5` a technique; and
an extra attack, Shuriken (on the ground and in the air). The sheet's
crouch, its other cuts (the thrust, the upward and low cuts, the ground
stab and its burst, the back and upward aerials), its leaps and kicks, its
smoke bomb (intro and exit), its taunts, wins and losses, its falls and
getting up and its portrait are no move; nor are the drawings the sheet
repeats from one row in another (Dragonblade's opening guard and draw, its
dash, its last guard), each cut once, for the move that has it.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): Dragonblade, its
dragon's cleave, the roster's top damage tier all round it. It sits under
Special abilities in the controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark
floats over #0012 while it performs it (half a second's stance and eight
fifteenths of a second's cleave). Shuriken, the Deflect Stance, Swift
Strike and the Rising Strike are its everyday tools: ordinary to the
player.

### Shuriken: a volley

Shuriken is the roster's first **volley**
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):
its `projectile` entry throws `count` 3 shots of its projectile,
`interval` a fifteenth of a second apart:

```js
projectile: {
  id: 'extra_attack_object', spawnAt: 2 / 15, offset: { x: 34, y: -58 },
  volley: { count: 3, interval: 1 / 15 },
},
```

Each shuriken (`projectiles.extra_attack_object`: 1050 units/s for 0.45
s, a 20 × 20 box, 1 and a stun of 0.2 s, no launch) leaves its hand 34
units in front of it at its chest, aimed where it faces as it leaves, and
strikes, is blocked or is turned back on its own; the stun of one holds a
target for the next, so the three add up to 3. The three are one use of
the move: one press, 15 Energy as it starts (an ordinary construct move,
[Energy](../systems/energy.md#construct-moves)), no cooldown (three of 1
are ordinary, [cooldowns](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)). A hit that
stops the throw stops the shots still to come; a raised Shield that blocks
one stops the volley (its other shots in flight are gone and no more are
thrown: `CombatSystem.stopVolley`), so a Shield pays for one block, never
three, as a blocked strike stops a string. On the ground and in the air.

### The Deflect Stance and the Deflect

- **The Deflect Stance** (`attack3`, on the ground): its blade drawn (a
  twelfth of a second), then held out across its body, flicking from guard
  to guard, for seven twelfths of a second (live: `{ x: 0, y: -100, w: 62,
  h: 100 }`, in front of it out to 62 units from its feet to over its
  head), lowered: three quarters of a second. While it is live it turns
  back every other fighter's projectile its blade meets
  (`deflectProjectiles`), now #0012's, flying back at its thrower; a
  fighter who walks into it takes a flick of the blade (1, no launch,
  hitstun 0.25). A move that turns shots back is a defense as well as an
  attack, so any valid request ends it (a move, a jump, a Dash, the Shield:
  `moveYields`), as #0008's Gum-Gum Balloon does. Drawn on the move, it
  slides on with the speed it had, as any attack does. It costs nothing
  and has no cooldown; it is never a Shield (a melee blow is struck through
  it as ever).
- **Its Deflect** (the Shield button in the air) is its canon's own: its
  blade spun in a whirl of orange cuts, see
  [below](#defense-energy-and-launch-reaction).

### Swift Strike and the Rising Strike

- **Swift Strike** (`attack4`, on the ground): its blade raised, drawn
  back and crouched to spring (three twentieths of a second, sliding on as
  any attack does), then it dashes the way it faces at 1300 units/s
  (`motion: { type: 'roll', speed: 1300, keep: 0, maxSpeed: 1300,
  friction: 4000, recoil: 320 }`: its own speed alone, none kept from the
  run), losing 4000 units/s every second on the ground, about 210 units in
  all. The cut is live for the dash's first fifth of a second (`{ x: -30,
  y: -95, w: 80, h: 95 }`, its body and blade, swept along the dash): 3,
  Base Launch 1 sideways. It passes through fighters (`passThrough`), so
  whoever it cuts is left behind it. A raised Shield stops it dead and
  throws it back at 320 units/s. Arrived, its trail thins to a grey blur
  and its blade is drawn in: eleven twentieths of a second in all. Off a
  ledge the dash flies on (a roll does).
- **The Rising Strike** (`midair_attack4`): its blade straight up (a
  twentieth of a second, the hang), then it dashes straight up at 850
  units/s (`motion: { type: 'rise', speed: 850 }`) for a fifth of a second,
  about 170 units, and on up under gravity, through a sweep of cuts over
  it (`{ x: -15, y: -150, w: 95, h: 150 }`): 3, Base Launch 2 straight up,
  passing through whoever it cuts. Once per airtime (`airUses: 1`: landing
  or being hit gives it back), keeping half its drift (`airMomentum: 0.5`):
  its way back to the stage.

### Dragonblade

**Dragonblade** (`attack5`) is a technique: half a second springing low,
its blade pointed down, and rising into its stance, the blade drawn
across before it (`attack5_cast`, nothing protects it), then the cleave:
a burst round it, `{ x: -60, y: -125, w: 180, h: 127 }`, from 60 units
behind it to 120 in front and from the floor to well over its head (the
whirl of green cuts the art draws), whose hit is 10, Base Launch 3
sideways (hitstun 0.5, blockstun 0.2, hitstop 0.12), dealt once to every
opponent it meets, either side. Its art as it goes out is the dragon
spirit (`effect: { animation: 'attack5_object', offset: { x: 10, y: -100
} }`): eleven frames at 20 fps, a wisp of green flame growing into the
dragon and coiling round it until it closes into a ring, centred over it,
behind the fighters, turned with the cut (`sourceFacing: 1`). Eight
fifteenths of a second more as the whirl dies down (`attack5_release`). A
raised Shield blocks it like any hit; a hit before it lets go breaks it,
and then nothing is released and no cooldown starts. The policy's 5 s for
its 10, from the release ([cooldowns](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)); no
Energy.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Twin Cuts** (`attack1`) and the **Cleave** (`attack2`), its
two close-range cuts (out to 52 / 54 and 56 units). Shuriken is thrown,
the Deflect Stance turns shots back, Swift Strike rolls through its target
and Dragonblade is a technique with a 5 s cooldown, so none could be a
partner. Five rapid presses of either one's button on the ground set off
its [Mash Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed
move first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its own
launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its seven-frame `mouvment`
clip, crouched, then a blur of speed, its blade a streak through it,
played once across the whole Dash, and its two-frame `midair_mouvment`, a
leap, tucked, then a foot thrust ahead, across the whole air dash. Its
canon's speed is in its moves (Swift Strike, the Rising Strike), never in
how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close cuts step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: its blade held
across its body, the one guard pose the sheet draws (no raise or lower of
its own, so the Shield goes up and down at once). In the air the Shield
button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), its canon's
own:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 4 / 20, recovery: 1 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: -55, y: -110, w: 125, h: 112 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Its blade spun in a whirl of orange cuts, setting off low behind it (the
startup), then ahead, over and behind it for four frames (live: 55 units
behind it to 70 in front, from its feet to well over its head), dying away
at its feet (its recovery): three tenths of a second, keeping the whole of
its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base
Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it
meets back at their thrower, now #0012's. No cooldown; starting it costs
nothing (never while exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back
costs 15 Energy, once however many it turns back, as every Deflect does.
On the ground its Deflect Stance turns shots back too: an attack, not its
Deflect, and free.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Shuriken, its one construct, costs
15 a throw, its three shots one use; Dragonblade has its 5 s instead. It
leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a second.

Its `launchReaction`: its cybernetic agility rights it sooner than most,
0.14 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.45 s, tumbling from 1150 units/s, and 25°
of steering. Its launches are as strong as anyone's, and it rebounds
off the scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its own).

Where the sheet repeats a drawing, the clip plays the same file again:
one file on disk, never a copy, and no two files in the folder are the
same picture. The sheet repeats some drawings from one row in another:
Dragonblade's row opens with the Deflect Stance's guard and Swift Strike's
draw and dash and closes on the Deflect's last guard, its shield pose is
one of its taunts, its jump's takeoff its getting up, and the Rising
Strike's frames its ground stab's and its jump's. Each such drawing is cut
once, for the one move that has it: Dragonblade's cast is its landing
from the dash, rising into the stance the sheet also opens its down
special with (a stance no other move uses), and the Deflect ends on its
whirl rather than the guard Dragonblade keeps. The shuriken and the dragon are drawn centred where they
are, at the fighter's art-pixel size, never fitted to its height (the
dragon is over two and a half times its height, the size the sheet draws
it). Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 57, so every frame
normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height` (57
× 88 / 52 ≈ 96.5 world units) puts #0012's art pixels at the roster's
common size.

Its blade, the green sweeps of its cuts, the Deflect's whirl and Swift
Strike's trail are drawn beside, round or under it, and would pull the
automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque middle) off its body, so every
clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each
frame's visible art): the middle of its body's mass over its head and
torso (its blade and every sweep set aside); its standing pose between its
planted feet (its breathing sways its shoulders, never its feet); its run
on its head, which every run frame draws in the same place; Swift Strike's
trail frames on its body, a blur at the trail's head, the trail stretching
behind it. Where the art reaches below its feet (a cut swept under it, its
blade's point dipped past its heel, the Deflect's whirl at its feet, the
Whirlwind Cut's ball under its sweep), the clip also says where its feet
are (`anchorY`), so the art never lifts its body off the ground.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A mobile duelist. Up close, Twin Cuts and the Cleave are its quick cuts
and its Mash Combo; from afar Shuriken pokes for 15 Energy a volley. Swift
Strike closes a gap in one cut and leaves its opponent behind it, the
Rising Strike gets it back to the stage, and the Deflect Stance answers a
zoner by sending its shots home. Dragonblade is its finisher: the roster's
top damage tier all round it, after half a second's stance any hit
breaks. Its weaknesses: its plain cuts and its shuriken are light, its
dashes commit it (a Shield stops one dead and throws it back, and the
ground dash flies on off a ledge), its stance only turns shots back (a
blow is struck through it), and its Dragonblade is rooted, breakable and
waits 5 s.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, a volley weighed
shot by shot, a stance that turns shots back, a dash and a lift that pass
through, a long cast with a 10) and uses every one of them.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, three seeds: 12
matches against each opponent, 132 a level): #0012 wins 0.60 at Medium,
0.48 at Hard and 0.50 at Brutal, within the roster's usual range (#0011,
measured the same way, wins 0.51 at Medium). At Hard: 6 of 12 against
#0001, 8 against #0002, 7 against #0003, 10 against #0004, 7 against
#0005, 5 against #0006, 4 against #0007, 2 against #0008, 5 against #0009,
4 against #0010 and 5 against #0011. Its weakest matchup is #0008's (2 to 4
of 12), whose rubber limbs reach past its blade. Its CPU uses its Mash
Combo most, then its aerials (the Falling Cut, the Rising Arc and the
Rising Strike, about a dozen presses a match each), Swift Strike about
five times a match, Shuriken four, Dragonblade two or three and the
Deflect Stance one or two. Its first tuning won 0.80 at Medium, its Mash
Combo landing nearly every turn: its two cuts' boxes, first the whole
sweep the art draws (60 × 120 units), were trimmed to where the blade cuts
(about 48 × 60 and 50 × 76), and its two big aerials' boxes made smaller;
a launch reaction made plainer as well dropped it to 0.37 at Hard, so its
own was kept. The results swing by about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the
next.

# #0003

#0003's own specification: the medic brawler. Its art, each of its moves,
its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is precise
chakra control: it holds its strength back until the instant of impact, so
its big blows split the ground, and it can mend its own body. That is in
its moves (quakes, a slam, a heal), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps
and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is a mechanic of its own, not just a pose and damage. Three
of them are attack features #0003 brought to the shared system, any
fighter's to use ([combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):

- **The quake** (`quake: true`, a hit effect): the hit runs through the
  ground, so it reaches only a fighter standing on it; a fighter in the
  air is passed by with nothing used up. Jumping clears it; a Shield,
  raised on the ground, blocks it like any hit.
- **The slam** (attack `motion: { type: 'slam', fallSpeed, impact }`): a
  plunge that lands. It strikes whatever it passes on the way down, and
  where it meets the ground its impact strikes every opponent round the
  landing point; the landing is its recovery, on the ground, in its own
  clip.
- **The heal** (a technique's `heal`): the cast lets go of a heal of its
  own fighter, Launch Points taken off at once; a hit before it lets go
  breaks it and heals nothing. A cast that only heals harms nobody, so it
  is no attack.

It also uses strikes (`hits`), attack `motion` (`rise`), `step`,
`airUses`, `blockPush` and a Deflect that turns projectiles back
(`deflectProjectiles`).

## Buttons and moves

Four numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` and `attack2`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack3` and `attack4`
techniques (ground only); and an extra attack. It has no summon and no
`attack5` button. The sheet's sealed-strength poses have no move yet:
Transform is reserved.

| Button | On the ground | In the air |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attack1` (U) | **Chakra Punch**: a quick straight (3, Base Launch 1 sideways) whose chakra shoves even a Shield that blocks it back | **Leaping Kick**: a chakra-driven leap off nothing, the knee driven up and the leg shot out (3, Base Launch 2 sideways), rising about 90 units: its anti-air and its way back to the stage. Once per airtime |
| `attack2` (I) | **Heel Drop**: a hop and an axe kick. The heel drives whoever is in front into the ground (3, downward), then the ground cracks round it: every opponent still standing there is popped up (1, a quake) | **Heavenly Foot of Pain**: a plunge heel-first at 1500 units/s, driving down whatever it passes (3), until it lands: the ground splits 110 units either side and throws up everyone standing on it (5, Base Launch 3 upward, a quake). Then the heel stays in the ground for 4/15 s. Once per airtime, every 2 s |
| `attack3` (O) | **Cherry Blossom Impact** (a technique): a 0.4 s cast, the fist raised, glowing, and driven into the ground, which erupts 150 units either side: everyone standing on it is thrown upward (5, Base Launch 3), anyone in the air is untouched. Every 4 s | — |
| `attack4` (M) | **Mystical Palm Technique** (a technique): a full second knelt, palms out; as it stands up it has healed itself of 10 Launch Points. Any hit before then breaks it and heals nothing. Every 3 s | — |
| `extra_attack` (J) | **Chakra Haymaker**: the glove tugged tight, a lunge, and an overhand blow (5, Base Launch 3 sideways) that drives a Shield back across the ground | — |
| `shield` (L) | its guard, a Shield of crossed arms (raised and lowered on a frame each) | **Deflect** (a fresh press): a rising backhand sweep from the feet to over the head that turns the other fighter's projectiles back at their thrower |

Its Leaping Kick and Heavenly Foot of Pain never start while it is still
flying from a launch: it has to recover first (an air jump, a fast fall or
landing).

```js
actions: {
  extra_attack: 'extra_attack',                          // the Chakra Haymaker
  transform: null,                                       // reserved
  attack1: { ground: 'attack1', air: 'midair_attack1' }, // the Chakra Punch / the Leaping Kick
  attack2: { ground: 'attack2', air: 'midair_attack2' }, // the Heel Drop / the Heavenly Foot of Pain
  attack3: { type: 'technique', id: 'attack3' },         // Cherry Blossom Impact
  attack4: { type: 'technique', id: 'attack4' },         // the Mystical Palm Technique
},
```

### Each move in detail

- **Chakra Punch** (`attack1`, 6 frames at 20 fps, 0.3 s): two frames of
  wind-up, the straight on frames 3-4 (its box out to 40 units, at the
  shoulders), two frames back. 3, Base Launch 1 sideways, a two-step
  freeze; `blockPush` 360 shoves a Shield that blocks it; hit-cancel once
  it has hit. It keeps all the speed it is thrown at, under 0.6 of the
  ground deceleration.
- **Leaping Kick** (`midair_attack1`, 3 frames at 15 fps): tucked for a
  frame, then `rise` at 420 units/s through frames 2-3 (the knee, then the
  kick: a box from the knee to the foot, out to 40 units), carrying on up
  under gravity (about 90 units in all); 3, Base Launch 2 sideways. Once
  per airtime (`airUses: 1`), steering half as well as it drifts;
  hit-cancel at once.
- **Heel Drop** (`attack2`, 10 frames at 30 fps, 0.4 s): the crouch and the
  hop (a `step` in at 220 units/s), the leg raised, the flip; then the heel
  (frame 6: a box in front, from the ground to the head, out to 40 units;
  3, Base Launch 1 downward) and the crack (frame 7: 50 units behind the
  heel to 90 in front, at foot height; 1, Base Launch 1 upward, a quake);
  frames 8-10 pull the heel out. A Shield stops the string at the strike
  it blocks (and is shoved 200 by the crack). Hit-cancel from frame 8.
  Driven down gently enough not to rebound, a target in front is still on
  the ground for the crack, which pops it up for a follow-up.
- **Heavenly Foot of Pain** (`midair_attack2`, 3 frames at 15 fps, then
  `midair_attack2_impact`, 4 frames): `slam`, a 2/15 s hang (the leg
  raised, the heel coming down), then 1500 units/s down (steering 0.4) for
  up to 0.9 s; its heel's box just under the body (3, Base Launch 1
  downward) strikes whatever it passes, and the plunge goes on. Landing,
  the impact (`{ x: -110, y: -36, w: 220, h: 40 }` round the landing point;
  5, Base Launch 3 upward, a quake, `blockPush` 420), then 4/15 s with the
  heel in the ground, never cut by the landing cancel; hit-cancel from its
  second frame once anything has been hit. Once per airtime, with only the
  0.5 s repeat delay. Off the stage it plunges into the Void: no ground, no landing.
- **Cherry Blossom Impact** (`attack3`, a technique): the cast is
  `attack3_cast` (4 frames at 10 fps, 0.4 s: the fist raised and flexed,
  glowing, driven down), the release `attack3_release` (5 frames at 15
  fps: the fist in the ground, bent over the impact, back up). On the
  release's first step the burst, `{ x: -150, y: -46, w: 300, h: 50 }`
  round #0003: 5, Base Launch 3 upward, a quake, hitstun 0.45, `blockPush`
  420. Only the 0.5 s repeat delay, from the press: a quake and a launch
  earn no cooldown.
- **Mystical Palm Technique** (`attack4`, a technique): the cast is
  `attack4_cast` (6 frames at 6 fps, a full second: a crouch, then knelt,
  palms out), the release `attack4_release` (1 frame at 4 fps: standing
  up). As the cast ends, `heal: 10` (never below 0; the passive Launch
  Point recovery runs on as it was). A soft green ring and plus marks
  show the heal ([rendering](../systems/rendering.md)). A 3 s cooldown
  from the press (the policy's for a heal), spent even if a hit breaks it.
- **Chakra Haymaker** (`extra_attack`, 8 frames at 15 fps, 0.53 s): the
  glove tugged tight with the teeth and the fist raised (frames 1-3), the
  lunge (frame 4: forward speed raised to at least 360), the overhand blow
  (frames 5-6, live: the whole arc it sweeps, from over the head to the
  ground in front, out to 46 units), driven through and back (7-8). 5, Base
  Launch 3 sideways, `blockPush` 620; no hit-cancel. Ground only.

Cooldowns are the shared cooldown policy's ([combat](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)),
never authored in #0003's definition. The Mystical Palm Technique's heal
earns 3 s, starting on the press. Every other move (the Chakra Punch,
Leaping Kick, Heel Drop, Heavenly Foot of Pain, Chakra Haymaker and Cherry
Blossom Impact) has only the universal 0.5-second repeat delay: no move of
#0003's deals 10, and a quake, a slam or a launch earns nothing. Its Deflect
has none; running, jumps and the Shield are exempt. Dash and air dash each have a separate 0.5-second
timer. Ground only: the Chakra Punch, the Heel Drop, the Chakra Haymaker
and both techniques.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
#0001 and #0002 do. Its movement is in its moves: the Leaping Kick's rise,
the Heel Drop's step, the Haymaker's lunge, the Heavenly Foot of Pain's
plunge. It brings the art: its two-frame `mouvment` clip (a low lunge,
speed lines streaming off the legs) played once across the whole Dash,
and its two-frame `midair_mouvment` (a body flicker, the figure breaking
into streaks) across the whole air dash.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield',
groundAnimation: 'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield',
groundReleaseAnimation: 'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm:
0.25 }`: the arms crossed before the face, raised and lowered on a frame
each (1/15 s). In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
the backhand sweep:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 2 / 20, recovery: 2 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: 4, y: -84, w: 42, h: 80 },
  directionalLaunch: 'vertical', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  airMomentum: 1, airControl: 0.4,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the hand drawn back low, then the backhand swung up and over the
head (frames 2 and 3, live), frame 4 the arm raised, held a frame longer: a
quarter of a second, falling as it sweeps and carrying the whole of its
drift. While it is live its box (the front from the feet to over the head,
out to 46 units: it catches what flies low, a travelling tornado
included) strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here upward, and
turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now
#0003's. No cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts, as every Deflect
does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 25, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15
(a perfect block's included); Combat Assist costs nothing. Its own is
only its refill, 15 / s (`energy: { regen: 15 }`), the quickest of the
roster: its precise chakra control. Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000
units/s up to 0.55 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, 20° of steering: quick to
come round, and it reads a launch better than most.

A clip that is part of a move's art plays that move's own files
(`midair_attack2_impact` plays `0003_midair_attack2_4.png` to `_7`,
`attack3_release` plays `0003_attack3_5.png` to `_9`), as #0001's technique
clips do. Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 48, so every
frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and
`visual.height` (48 × 88 / 52 ≈ 81.2 world units) puts #0003's art pixels
at the roster's common size: between #0002 (66) and #0001 (107).

Where automatic anchoring would drag the body, a clip authors its own
anchors: `anchorX` (art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art)
on the red top, which stays over the hips whatever the arms do: the
Chakra Punch (12, 13, 17, 19, 19.5, 14.5), the Chakra Haymaker (10.5, 11,
11, 15.5, 21, 23.5, 24, 13), the Heel Drop, both techniques, the Deflect,
the Dash (27, 31: its speed lines stream behind) and the Heavenly Foot of
Pain with its landing (the hair flung by the impact). The Heavenly Foot of
Pain's last plunge frame also says where the feet are (`anchorY` 36 of its
49 rows): the heel's arc sweeps below them, and must not lift the body.
Everything else stands on the bottom of its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A deliberate powerhouse. Its quick Chakra Punch and Heel Drop win the
ground at close range and pressure a Shield (the punch shoves it, the
crack round the heel catches a guard that steps back); its heavy blows,
the Haymaker sideways and Cherry Blossom Impact upward, are its finishers,
telegraphed and punishable. Its quakes reward reading who is standing on
the ground: a jump clears every one of them, so they are strongest against
an opponent landing, shielding or pinned. In the air it has a rising kick
to contest and to get back, and a slam that turns a jump into a crater
(and, off the stage, into a fall). Its heal is its sustain: a full second
of kneeling, so it needs room, after a launch that sent the opponent far,
or while the opponent respawns.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): its quakes
never counted on an airborne target, its slam's landing and impact
predicted along the plunge, and its heal cast only when it has taken enough
for the whole heal to count and nothing can reach it before the cast lets
go (or while the opponent is out of play).

## Balance

Tuned from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches (full Watch Mode battles, first to 3,
on Desert and City, five seeds each way round: 20 matches per cell;
points #0003 : opponent). Against #0001: Medium 38 : 46, Hard 38 : 45,
Brutal 33 : 47. Against #0002: Medium 28 : 54, Hard 28 : 59, Brutal
32 : 50. The first cut scored about 5 : 24 at Hard against either; what
got it here: the Leaping Kick (a rise, so it can contest the air and get
back to the stage, where it had nothing), Base Launch 3 on its big
blows (the Haymaker, Cherry Blossom Impact and the slam's impact), a faster
Heel Drop (30 fps), its Deflect reaching down to its feet (it never turned
#0002's tornado back before), and a quicker launch reaction and Energy
refill. #0002 stays its hard matchup: most of its falls there come from
#0002's quick Deflect swatting its jump-ins. On the same seeds #0001 and
#0002 score 18 : 16 at Hard. These matches predate the shared cooldown
policy (when Cherry Blossom Impact waited 4 s, the Heavenly Foot of Pain
2 s and the Mystical Palm Technique 12 s, and #0002's Deflect 0.05 s) and
have not been measured again since.

# #0013

#0013's own specification: the pale-eyed palm fighter in a lavender
jacket. Its art, each of its moves, its guard and its Deflect, and how
they play. Its fantasy is a fighter whose eyes see all round it and into
the flow of power inside its opponents, and whose palms strike that flow
rather than the body: every palm it lands cuts off some of its target's
Energy, so the Shield, the Dash or the recovery the target was counting on
may not be there. That is in its moves (a hit's `drain` on its palms, its
lions and its flurry, a dome of chakra that turns shots back, a dive
palm-first, a flurry that seals its target's whole bar), never its
locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does.
It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: its
palms, its lions and its flurry **drain** their target's Energy (`drain`,
a hit effect any fighter may now use: Energy taken off at once and given
to nobody, a target drained to 0 exhausted, with no Shield, Dash, air dash
or Deflect until its bar is full again); the Protective Palms are a dome
that strikes everyone inside, either side of it, and turns back every shot
that meets it (`hits`, `deflectProjectiles`); the Falling Palm dives
palm-first and bursts where it lands (`motion: slam` with an `impact`);
the Twin Lion Fists let lions of chakra surge out ahead of it (a
technique's projectile) that drain half the bar; and the Sixty-Four Palms
are a cast whose flurry deals the roster's top tier, 10, and seals its
target's whole bar.

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a young kunoichi of a
clan whose eyes see nearly all round them, through walls and into the
chakra network inside a body, and whose Gentle Fist strikes that network
with the palms, closing the points chakra flows through, so an opponent
struck enough cannot mould chakra at all. Its own techniques: a defensive
dome of chakra spun from its palms that cuts down whatever comes at it;
the Twin Lion Fists, its chakra shaped into a lion on each hand that
reach further and absorb the chakra they touch; and, with its eyes woken
and its field of divination spread round it, the Sixty-Four Palms, a
flurry that seals sixty-four points and leaves its target unable to use
its chakra. It fights with its palms and its kicks; it heals nobody,
summons nobody, makes no clone, throws nothing of its own (its lions are
its own chakra) and never homes in. So: its moves are its palms, its kicks,
its dome, its lions and its Sixty-Four Palms, and the chakra they cut off
is the game's Energy (taken, never given to it: nothing gives Energy back
in Alva); its guard is its arms crossed; its Deflect a palm swept up
through an arc of chakra; its Dash a blur of speed.

ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; no extra attack. The sheet's getting up, its intro, support,
wins and losses, its edit notes (a recoloured drawing beside the one it
became), its portrait and story panels, the dome drawn on its own and its
lion flames drawn on their own and in four directions (for laying over
frames: nothing is laid over anything here) and the second, near-identical
copy of its dome's row are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Twin Lion Fists,
whose lions drain half its target's bar at a bite, and the Sixty-Four
Palms, a 10-damage flurry that seals its target's whole bar. Both sit
under Special abilities in the controller editor, and an orange
exclamation mark floats over #0013 while it performs either. The
Protective Palms and its draining palms are its everyday tools: ordinary
to the player.

### The drain

`drain` is a shared hit effect
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox),
[Energy](../systems/energy.md#rules)), and #0013 is the first to use it:
a real hit with a `drain` takes that much Energy off its target at once
(`CombatState.drain`), never below 0, and gives it to nobody: #0013 gains
nothing (nothing gives Energy back). It is no payment, so the target's
refill runs on; reaching 0 exhausts the target as any 0 does, with no
Dash, air dash, Deflect, Shield or construct move until its bar is full
again. A blocked hit drains nothing: the Shield pays its own 15 for the
block, as ever. Its event says how much (`drained`). It is routine for
the cooldown policy (a light palm may drain and stay ordinary), and the
CPU values it, the more when it would empty the bar.

| Move | Drain |
| --- | --- |
| Gentle Fist, Air Palm, Protective Palms (each strike), Rising Palm | 5 |
| Falling Palm (its landing's burst) | 15 |
| Twin Lion Fists (its lions) | 50 |
| Sixty-Four Palms (its flurry) | 100: the whole bar |

### The Protective Palms and the Deflect

- **The Protective Palms** (`attack3`, on the ground): it turns, its back
  to us (three twentieths of a second), chakra swirls up from its feet,
  then round it, closing into a dome round it that bursts outward. Three
  strikes, each everywhere the dome is, either side of it: the swirl (`{
  x: -64, y: -92, w: 128, h: 94 }`, from 0.2 s), 1; the dome (`{ x: -68,
  y: -112, w: 136, h: 114 }`, from 0.3 s), 1; the dome bursting (the same
  box, from 0.4 s), 3, Base Launch 2 sideways; each drains 5. While the
  strikes are live (three tenths of a second) the dome turns back every
  other fighter's projectile that meets it (`deflectProjectiles`), now
  #0013's, flying back at its thrower. The wisps rise off it and it turns
  back round to its stance: three quarters of a second in all. A move that
  turns shots back is a defense as well as an attack, so any valid request
  ends it (a move, a jump, a Dash, the Shield: `moveYields`), as #0012's
  Deflect Stance does. Spun on the move, it slides on with the speed it
  had, as any attack does; it never steps in. It costs nothing and has no
  cooldown; it is never a Shield (a raised Shield blocks its strikes as
  any; a blocked strike stops the string).
- **Its Deflect** (the Shield button in the air): its palm swept up
  through an arc of chakra, see
  [below](#defense-energy-and-launch-reaction).

### The Falling Palm

**The Falling Palm** (`midair_attack2`): a somersault, turning over into
the dive (two fifteenths of a second, the hang), then it dives palm-first
at 1300 units/s (`motion: { type: 'slam', fallSpeed: 1300, impact }`) for
up to 0.8 s, its chakra driven down from its palm (`{ x: -6, y: -50, w:
38, h: 54 }`): whoever it meets on the way takes 3 at Base Launch 2
straight down, a spike. Where it meets the ground its chakra bursts round
its palm (the impact, `{ x: -50, y: -40, w: 100, h: 44 }`, either side):
3 at Base Launch 2 straight up and 15 drained; then a fifth of a second
landed, its palm low behind it (`midair_attack2_impact`). Once per
airtime (`airUses: 1`), keeping half its drift (`airMomentum: 0.5`). A
dive that runs out in the air lands nothing and falls on. It is the
right stick down on a controller: a plunge is never one press of an
attack button away from the Void.

### The Twin Lion Fists

**The Twin Lion Fists** (`attack4`) is a technique: two fifths of a
second in its stance, its arms low and open, gathering its chakra
(`attack4_cast`, nothing protects it), then the lions on its hands surge
out ahead of it: its projectile (`projectiles.attack4_object`), leaving it
40 units in front of it at its chest, the way it faces, at 750 units/s
for a quarter of a second (about 190 units more), a 44 × 36 box; whoever
it bites takes 5 at Base Launch 2, thrown the way it flies (hitstun 0.45,
blockstun 0.18, hitstop 0.08), and loses 50 Energy (`drain`: the lions
feed on the chakra they touch). Two fifths of a second more, the lions
blazing on its hands (`attack4_release`). A raised Shield blocks it like
any hit (nothing drained); a hit before it lets go breaks it, and then
nothing is released. No cooldown (5 and a drain are ordinary); its lions
are a construct: 15 Energy as it starts
([Energy](../systems/energy.md#construct-moves)).

### The Sixty-Four Palms

**The Sixty-Four Palms** (`attack5`) is a technique: two thirds of a
second with its arms opened, then its hands brought together, its eyes
woken, white, and its field of divination drawn round it in a ring of
chakra lines that spreads and fades, then dropping into its stance
(`attack5_cast`, nothing protects it); then the flurry, its palms whirling
chakra in arcs all round it: a burst, `{ x: -40, y: -100, w: 125, h: 102
}`, from 40 units behind it to 85 in front and from the floor to over its
head, whose hit is 10, Base Launch 3 sideways (hitstun 0.6, blockstun
0.2, hitstop 0.12) and `drain: 100`, dealt once to every opponent it
meets, either side: its chakra sealed, its target exhausted until its bar
is full again (no Shield, Dash, air dash or Deflect meanwhile). Two fifths
of a second more as its palms come to rest (`attack5_release`). A raised
Shield blocks it like any hit (nothing drained); a hit before it lets go
breaks it, and then nothing is released and no cooldown starts. The
policy's 5 s for its 10, from the release
([cooldowns](../systems/combat.md#cooldowns)); no Energy.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is the **Gentle Fist** (`attack1`) and the **Sweep Kick**
(`attack2`), its two close-range strikes (out to 56 and 48 units). The
Protective Palms turn shots back, and the Twin Lion Fists and the
Sixty-Four Palms are techniques, so none could be a partner. Five rapid
presses of either one's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage and their drain still count) and the
twelfth with its own launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its one-frame `mouvment`
clip, a blur of speed, its hair streaming, shown across the whole Dash,
and its one-frame `midair_mouvment`, a lunge, its palm out ahead, across
the whole air dash (the sheet draws its dash in two frames: the blur for
the ground, the lunge for the air). Its canon's speed is in its moves,
never in how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', perfectWindow: 0.1,
perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: its palm raised, then its arms crossed before it.
The sheet draws its guard's lowering as its raising again (the same
drawing), so there is no lowering clip: the Shield goes down at once. In
the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -20, y: -95, w: 75, h: 98 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Crouched (the startup), then its palm swept up before it through an arc
of chakra and over its head for two frames (live: 20 units behind it to
55 in front, from its feet to over its head, the arc the art draws), its
palm raised (its recovery): four fifteenths of a second, keeping the whole
of its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at
Base Launch 2, here sideways, batted away before it, and turns the other
fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now #0013's. No
cooldown; starting it costs nothing (never while exhausted), and the
first projectile it turns back costs 15 Energy, once however many it turns
back, as every Deflect does. On the ground its Protective Palms turn shots
back too: an attack, not its Deflect, and free.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. The Twin Lion Fists, its one
construct, cost 15; the Sixty-Four Palms have their 5 s instead. It
leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a second. Its drains
take Energy from its opponents and never give it any.

Its `launchReaction`: light, it is flung a little longer, 0.15 s per 1000
units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, and 20° of steering. Its
launches are as strong as anyone's, and it rebounds off the scenery as
everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its own).

`heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 50, so every frame normalizes at
exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height` (50 × 88 / 52 ≈
84.6 world units) puts #0013's art pixels at the roster's common size.

Its palms thrust out and their chakra, its dome, its field of divination,
its dive's streak and its Deflect's arc are drawn out round it, and would
pull the automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque middle) off its body,
so every clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art pixels from the
left of each frame's visible art): standing with both feet planted (its
stance, its guard, its landing, the Gentle Fist, its lions, the
Sixty-Four Palms' cast, its hurt), between its feet; anywhere else, on its
jacket (the middle of its body); its dome's frames on the dome's middle,
which the sheet draws round it. Where the dome's wisps are swept under its
feet as it breaks, the clip also says where its feet are (`anchorY`), so
the art never lifts its body off the ground.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
stance frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A close-range palm fighter that wears its opponent's resources down. Up
close, the Gentle Fist and the Sweep Kick are its quick strikes and its
Mash Combo, and every palm cuts some Energy off its target: a few strings
and a block or two later its opponent is exhausted, without the Shield,
the Dash or the air dash it was counting on. The Protective Palms answer a
zoner by sending its shots home and punish whoever crowds it from either
side; the Twin Lion Fists reach past its palms and bite off half a bar;
the Falling Palm spikes from above. The Sixty-Four Palms are its finisher:
the roster's top damage tier, and its target's whole bar sealed. Its
weaknesses: it is light and short of reach, its strikes are mostly light,
its lions and its Sixty-Four Palms need a breakable moment to gather (a
hit before they let go breaks them), and its drains give it nothing of its
own: a Shield it cannot break, it has to wait out.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings that hold and
drain, a dome that turns shots back, a dive, a technique's projectile, a
long cast with a 10 and a whole-bar drain), values a drain the more when it
would empty its opponent's bar, and uses every one of them.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 288 a level): #0013 wins 0.56 at Medium, 0.52 at
Hard and 0.50 at Brutal, within the roster's usual range (#0012, measured
the same way over fewer seeds, won 0.60 at Medium and 0.48 at Hard). At
Hard: 15 of 24 against #0001, 11 against #0002, 19 against #0003, 19
against #0004, 9 against #0005, 9 against #0006, 13 against #0007, 10
against #0008, 13 against #0009, 13 against #0010, 8 against #0011 and 10
against #0012. Its weakest matchup is #0011's (6 to 8 of 24), whose webbing
holds it out of reach of its palms. Its CPU uses its Mash Combo most, then
the Gentle Fist and the Sweep Kick (about twenty presses a match each),
its Deflect (about eighteen) and the Air Palm (about eleven), the
Protective Palms and the Rising Palm (about six), the Twin Lion Fists
(about four), the Falling Palm (about two) and the Sixty-Four Palms (one
or two).

Its first tuning won only 0.30 at Medium and 0.24 at Hard: it dealt nearly
as much as its opponents but finished almost none of them (about one fall
a match against #0012's two), most of its launchers sending straight up,
where the Void is farthest, and nothing reaching past its palms. Its dome's
burst and its Deflect were turned sideways and the Falling Palm's spike made
Base Launch 2 (0.39 at Medium); the Twin Lion Fists' lions, first a bite
just before its hands, became a short projectile that surges out ahead of
it (its canon's lions reach further than its palms), and the Gentle Fist's
third palm its heavy blow, 5 (0.60 to 0.72 at Medium, 0.56 to 0.62 at
Hard); the Gentle Fist's first palm, out at a twentieth of a second, was
given a standard jab's tenth, and the lions a quarter of a second's flight
rather than three tenths, which brought it to the numbers above (with a
third palm of 3 it fell back to 0.38 at Hard). The results swing by about
0.1 from one set of seeds to the next.

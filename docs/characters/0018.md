# #0018

#0018's own specification: the pink-haired schoolgirl in a navy sailor
uniform and a red bow. Her art, each of her moves, her guard and her
Deflect, and how they play. She is a devoted stalker who will cut down
anyone in her way, and her fantasy is that her prey can never see her
coming: a **diary** that tells her where her prey will be, after which she
**slips out of its sight**, a **knife lunge** that lands far harder **in a
turned back**, a **flash grenade** that blinds whoever looks at it and
holds it still long enough to be got behind, a **katana** drawn in one
cut, and an **axe** swung up in a great arc through a raised guard.
That is in her moves (a cloak, a backstab, a lob, a paralysis that must be
seen, a block shoved back), never her locomotion: she runs, jumps and
Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules she relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage. Her
**Flash Grenade** is the roster's first lobbed projectile, `arc`, a
capability added to the shared combat for her and open to any fighter: a
projectile that leaves going up at its `lift` and falls under its own
`gravity`, rising and coming down ahead of its thrower along one curve the
game and the CPU's predictions share. Its flash is a `gaze` that holds
still (`paralyze`): it must be seen, and since this capability a gaze
projectile is seen, or not, from the side it comes from, the way it
travels. Her **Diary** is a technique that cloaks her (`cloak`); her
**Stalker's Lunge** and **Stalker's Dive** land far harder in a turned
back (`backstab`), the lunge a run along the ground (`motion: roll`), the
dive a homing dash (`motion: homing`); her **Axe Swing** is a technique
whose 10-damage burst sends its target sky-high and shoves a raised
guard back (`blockPush`).

## Her canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a schoolgirl, top of her
class and athletic, obsessed with the boy she loves, who keeps a diary on
her phone recording what he does every ten minutes, and uses it to know
where he will be and to be there first. She stalks unseen and kills
whoever threatens him, with whatever she carries: a kitchen knife, a
katana, a hatchet and an axe; once, a flash grenade, whose flash knocked
everyone out instead of killing them. She has no power beyond her weapons,
her body and her diary: she does not heal, summon, make clones or fly. So:
her moves are her knife, her katana, her axe and her flash grenade; her
diary is how she gets out of her prey's sight; her ambushes land far
harder from behind; the flash holds whoever sees it. Her guard is her
katana held up, her Deflect a flying knee, her Dash and air dash a sprint
leaning far forward. Her launch reaction is a light girl's: thrown a
little further, but quick to steer.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the **Diary**, her
phone telling her where her prey will be and her slipping out of its
sight; and the **Axe Swing**, 10 that sends its target sky-high and
shoves even a raised guard back, after a breakable wind-up. They sit under Special abilities in the
controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats over #0018 while
she performs one. Her knife, her katana, her lunge, her dive and her
grenade are her everyday tools: ordinary to the player.

### The Flash Grenade: lobbed, and it has to be seen

An ordinary attack, on the ground and in the air, that throws a projectile
(`attack3_object`, `midair_attack3_object`). On the ground she crouches,
hops (`motion: rise`, 240 units/s for three twentieths of a second: about
45 units in all) and whips her arm over her head, the grenade leaving her
hand 16 units before her and 86 up as she starts down; she is back on the
ground as the move ends, so the button pressed again is this move, held
by its cooldown, never the Air Flash Grenade at once. In the air she
whips her arm round and lets it go from the same place.

The grenade is **lobbed** (`arc: { lift, gravity }`,
[projectiles](../systems/combat.md#projectiles)): from the ground 520
units/s the way she faces, leaving going up at 120 units/s and falling at
1500 units/s every second; from the air 400 units/s, dropped level (no
lift). It tumbles as it flies (a turn and a half a second, art only). From
her hop it comes down on a standing fighter about 150 to 270 units off and
passes over one nearer; thrown from the air it falls on whoever is below
and ahead, further the higher she is. The floor ends it, as it ends any
shot; a raised Shield blocks it.

Its flash **has to be seen** (`gaze`, [combat: hit
effects](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)): a
fighter facing the side it comes from takes 1 and is **held still for 1
s** (`paralyze`: no launch, no acting, its hurt pose); one with its back
turned to it is passed by with nothing used up, the grenade flying on.
That second is the time to get behind it for the Stalker's Lunge. The
cooldown policy gives the hold 3 s, from the throw, and so no Energy.

### The stalker: the Diary and the backstab

The **Diary** (a technique) is seven tenths of a second with her phone out
and her eyes in shadow, any blow breaking it, then she is cloaked for 3 s
(`cloak`, [techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)): drawn as a faint
shimmer of her own art, and a CPU cannot see her at all (it sees her where
she vanished, doing nothing, until a reaction time after she shows
herself). It is no invulnerability: she is struck, pushed and pulled
exactly as ever, and her first move or any hit or block shows her again.
Two fifths of a second more as the phone is put away, which any move cuts
short (it harms nobody, so it gives way). The policy's 3 s, from the
release.

The **Stalker's Lunge** and the **Stalker's Dive** each carry a
`backstab`: a real hit on a fighter with its back turned to where it comes
from deals 5 at Base Launch 3 sideways, held 0.5 s, instead of its own 3.
The lunge is a run along the ground (`motion: roll`: 700 units/s and 0.3
of her running speed, up to 900, running down at 2000 units/s every
second); a Shield that blocks it stops her dead and sends her back. The
dive is a homing dash (`motion: homing`: range 220, 950 units/s), once per
airtime. Her backstab is 5, never 10, so neither earns a cooldown.

### The Axe Swing

A technique: her axe hefted low before her, its head drawn back down by
her feet (three fifths of a second; any blow breaks it, nothing released, no
cooldown), then swung up and round in a great arc, from low behind her to
high before her (the sheet's upward move): every opponent its box meets,
from 24 units behind her round to 60 in front and from the floor to over
her head, takes **10 at Base Launch 3 straight up**, sent sky-high; her
one big launcher, and the one that finishes; a raised Shield blocks
it and is shoved back along it (`blockPush: 600`). Four fifteenths of a
second more as the axe comes up on her shoulder. Once every 5 s (the
policy's, for 10), from the release; no Energy.

### Mash Combo

Her pair is the **Kitchen Knife** (`attack1`) and the **Katana Draw**
(`extra_attack`), her two close-range strikes (out to 40 and 62 units).
Her lunge is a roll, her grenade a projectile and the Diary and the Axe
Swing techniques: none could be a partner. Five rapid presses of either
one's button on the ground set off her Mash Combo: twelve turns, the
mashed move first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns
1 to 11 launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with
its own launch.

None of her own: she runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as

sprint leaning far forward, streaks behind her (2 frames), shown across

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Her `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: her hand at her
sheathed katana, the katana held up before her, and her hand back at its
hilt. In the air the Shield button is her **Deflect** (her `deflect`
entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -12, y: -78, w: 48, h: 80 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Her leg drawn up (the startup), her flying knee for two frames (live: 12
units behind her to 36 in front, from her feet to over her head), and
tucked (its recovery): four fifteenths of a second, keeping the whole of
her drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base
Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it
meets back at their thrower, now #0018's. No cooldown; starting it costs
nothing (never while exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back
costs 15 Energy, as every Deflect does.

Her Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Her one projectile, the grenade, has
its 3 s instead of a cost, and the Diary and the Axe Swing cost nothing:
nothing of hers costs Energy. She leaves `energy` out and refills at the
shared 12 a second.

Her `launchReaction`: light and quick, 0.15 s more stun per 1000 units/s
up to 0.45 s, tumbling from 1150 units/s, and 20° of steering. She
rebounds off the scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of her own).

drawing, the clip plays the same file again. A clip plays its own

clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 50, so every frame
normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height`

common size.

arms thrown out would pull the automatic anchor off her body, so every
clip authors its own anchors (`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each
frame's visible art): the middle of her navy uniform, which every frame
draws on her body. Every clip stands on the bottom of its art.

What of the sheet is no move: her entrance, her ordinary rising and
landing poses, her falls, her lying and getting up, her losing and winning
poses (her axe, the boy and the hearts), her other cuts (the rising

and its cuts, the cuts that open before her), the three ultimates (the
frenzy of cuts, the shredding cuts and the black cloak's blinding light),
the glow of her diary, the grenade rolling and going off, the portrait,
the palette swaps and the sheet's logo.

If her airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load she holds her first
stance frame; an attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or the air dash
whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A stalker who wins from angles her prey cannot see. Up close, the Kitchen
Knife and the Katana Draw are her quick strings and her Mash Combo; the
Stalker's Lunge closes
distance low and fast and the Stalker's Dive finds her prey from the air.
Her plan is to get behind: the **Flash Grenade**, lobbed from a hop, holds
whoever is looking at it still for a second, time to Dash past and drive
the lunge into the back it cannot turn (5 at Base Launch 3); the **Diary**
hides her from a CPU entirely and from a player's eye but for a shimmer,
for three seconds to reach the same back; the **Axe Swing** is her finish
and punishes a turtle: 10 that sends its target sky-high and shoves even
a raised guard back. Her weaknesses: her
grenade passes over anyone too close and sails past a turned back, her
casts (the Diary and the axe) are long commitments any blow breaks, her
cloak is no protection, and her ordinary blades launch lightly: her
finishes come from a backstab or the axe.

**The CPU** plays her from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads her moves as any fighter's (strings, a roll, a homing
dash, a lobbed throw it predicts along its curve, a cloak, a cast whose
burst shoves a guard), and uses every one of them.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, four seeds: 16
matches against each opponent, 272 a level): #0018 wins 0.47 at Medium,
0.56 at Hard and 0.53 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range (the 95%
interval is about ±0.06 at this count). At Hard: 11 of 16 against #0001,
7 against #0002, 7 against #0003, 12 against #0004, 8 against #0005, 9
against #0006, 6 against #0007, 7 against #0008, 8 against #0009, 13
against #0010, 10 against #0011, 9 against #0012, 15 against #0013, 7
against #0014, 7 against #0015, 6 against #0016 and 11 against #0017. Her
weakest matchups are the fighters who keep her at range or out-reach her
lunge (#0007's weapons, #0016's pistols and arrow, #0014's grabs); her
best, those she gets behind (#0010, small and close, and #0013, whose
palms must come close). The CPU uses every move of hers, the Diary about
twice a match and the Axe Swing about once or twice.

Her first tuning had every launch of hers sideways (the Axe Swing too)
and won 0.49, 0.46 and 0.52, but a CPU opponent in danger never felt it:
a sideways hit on a grounded fighter at that Launch Point leaves it able
to get back, so no move of hers read as a finish to the CPU, and #0017's
CPU, its Launch Point dangerous, kept fighting her rather than breaking
off to recover (the CPU scenarios that pair each fighter with the next
caught it). An upward Katana Draw fixed that but made her far too strong
(0.65, 0.77, 0.78: a quick cut that finishes); the upward launch went to
the Axe Swing instead (the sheet's upward move, slow and breakable), which
won 0.55, 0.59 and 0.54; three fifths of a second to heft it (half a
second before) and its reach before her cut from 68 to 60 units brought
her to the figures above.

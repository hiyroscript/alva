# #0017

#0017's own specification: the robot girl in blue overalls, a winged pink
cap and round glasses, her purple hair down her back. Her art, each of her
moves, her guard and her Deflect, and how they play. She is a gag
character of boundless strength who runs on the logic of a cartoon, and
her fantasy is that nothing her opponent brings is safe from her: a
**touch** that copies the strongest projectile her opponent throws for her
to throw straight back (or, from one who throws nothing, takes all its
Energy), a **punch that splits the earth** round her, a **run at the speed
of sound**, and **headbutts** forward, up and straight down into the
ground, with a spinning kick and the stick she pokes things with up close.
That is in her moves (a copy taken off whoever she touches, a quake, a
roll along the ground, a rise, a plunge that lands), never her
locomotion: she runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter
does. It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules she relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage. Her
**Finders Keepers** is the roster's first `mimic`, a capability added to
the shared combat for her and open to any fighter: a touch that copies the
strongest projectile its target throws into her hands, which her next
Finders Keepers throws instead of touching (the projectile alone, without
what threw it), or that takes all of the Energy of a target with nothing
to copy, for herself. Her **Earth Chop** is a technique whose burst is a
`quake`, 10 to whoever stands on the ground round her; her **Kiiin!** is a
run along the ground (`motion: roll`); her **Headbutt Dive** a plunge that
lands (`motion: slam`) with a burst of its own (`impact`, a `quake`); her
**Rocket Headbutt** a lift (`motion: rise`) that carries its target up and
is her way back to the stage; her Spin Kick's first kick holds its target
for the arc that follows.

## Her canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a robot built to look like
a little girl, impossibly strong and impossibly durable in a world that
runs on cartoon logic. She splits the ground (and the planet) with a punch
(the Earth Chop), runs faster than sound with her arms spread like a
plane's wings, making the sound as she goes ("Kiiin!"), headbutts whatever
is in front of her, launches herself up into the sky and comes back down
head-first, pokes at whatever she finds with a stick, and treats anything
anyone brings against her as a toy to pick up and play with. She shrugs
off blows that would floor anyone else. She does not heal, summon,
make clones, home in or fire a shot of her own. So: her moves are her
kick, her stick, her run, her headbutts and her punch into the ground; the
cartoon logic is in Finders Keepers (whatever her opponent throws is hers
the moment she touches it); her guard is both fists raised, her Deflect a
hop and a shout that blows shots back, her Dash a sprint and her air dash
a forward roll. Her launch reaction is a robot's: held a little less than
most.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5', 'midair_attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the **Earth Chop**,
the ground split round her for 10 after a breakable wind-up; and **Finders
Keepers** on the ground and in the air, the touch that takes what her
opponent fights with: what she can do depends on whom she touches. They
sit under Special abilities in the controller editor, and an orange
exclamation mark floats over #0017 while she performs one. Her kick, her
stick, her run and her headbutts are her everyday tools: ordinary to the
player.

### Finders Keepers: whatever they throw is hers

An ordinary attack, on the ground and in the air, whose strike is a touch
(`mimic: { spawnAt, offset, siphon }`, [combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)).
Her finger poked out (1, no launch, a short stun) is a melee strike like
any other: it costs nothing, it has no cooldown, and a raised Shield
blocks it (and then it takes nothing). A **real hit** takes what its
target fights with:

- **A copy of its strongest projectile.** Of every projectile the target
  throws that flies (a trap, an obstacle or a call left where it appears
  is no shot to take: #0015's present, #0004's dancing double, #0009's
  Earth Pillar), the one whose throw deals one target the most (every
  strike of a piercing one and its finisher; a backstab by its harder
  hit), then the harder launch, then one that holds (a paralysis, a
  float, a charm, a fear): #0001's sphere, #0002's whirlwind, #0007's
  rocket (10 each), #0013's lions, #0006's scream, #0009's Air Blast,
  #0015's tornado, #0016's arrow, #0011's webbing, #0012's shuriken,
  #0014's wave, #0004's melody. The copy is in her hands
  (`CombatState.copy`; a mark over her shows it, its own art in a white
  ring): one at a time, until she throws it or loses her life. The target
  keeps its own.
- **All its Energy, for herself**, from a target that throws nothing to
  copy (#0003, #0005, #0008, #0010, and #0017 herself): `siphon: 100`
  takes everything it has off at once (to 0: exhausted, so no Dash, air
  dash, Deflect, Shield or construct move until its bar is full again),
  and every bit of it comes to her, never past her own full bar (a full
  bar ends her own exhaustion, as any does). The one way anything gives
  Energy back to a fighter ([Energy](../systems/energy.md#rules)).

Holding a copy, her next Finders Keepers, on the ground or in the air,
**throws it instead**: the same clip, her finger going out, and as it does
(two fifteenths of a second in on the ground, a fifteenth in the air) the copy
leaves her fingertip, 32 units before her at her shoulder (28 at her chest
in the air), the way she faces. It is **the projectile alone**, never what
threw it: #0007's rocket without the launcher, #0016's arrow without the
bow, #0001's sphere without the cast. It flies, strikes, pierces, pulls,
repels, erases and is blocked, turned back and stopped exactly as its
thrower's does, in its own art and its thrower's art-pixel size, and it is
hers now: its hits credit her and never strike her. The throw is a
projectile attack of its own, built for the copy by the shared attack
schema (`mimicThrow` in
[`js/game/combat/mimic.js`](../../js/game/combat/mimic.js)), so the
policies price it by what it throws: an ordinary copy costs its 15 Energy
as the throw starts and has no cooldown; one that holds (#0011's webbing)
waits 3 s and one that deals 10 (#0001's sphere, #0002's whirlwind,
#0007's rocket) waits 5 s from the throw, paying nothing, and since it is
the same move, the touch waits with it. A throw cut short before the copy
leaves her (a hit in its startup) keeps the copy in her hands.

The touch is deliberate (seven fifteenths of a second, two fifteenths
before her finger reaches) and short (out to 36 units): it has to land, unblocked, on
an opponent who knows what it does. What it gives her is different for
every opponent, and so is what the throw that follows is worth.

### Kiiin!: the speed of sound

Her arms flung out (a fifteenth of a second), then she is away along the
ground the way she faces (`motion: { type: 'roll', speed: 760, keep: 0.4,
maxSpeed: 1000, friction: 800, recoil: 260 }`): 760 units/s and 0.4 of the
running speed she had, up to 1000, running down at 800 units/s every
second, about 310 units from a standstill. Her arms out like wings, her
body strikes whoever she meets (3, Base Launch 3 sideways: whoever she
meets at the speed of sound is sent flying).
A raised Shield that blocks her stops her dead and sends her back (260
units/s); a wall stops her; off a ledge she runs on into the air. Two
thirds of a second in all, and she is back to her own run speed as it
ends. Ground only: its air move is the Headbutt Dive.

### The Headbutt Dive: head-first into the ground

In the air `attack2` curls her over (two fifteenths of a second, the hang)
and drives her straight down head-first at 1400 units/s (`motion: slam`)
for up to 0.8 s, her head striking whoever is under her (3, Base Launch 2
straight down: a spike). Where she meets the ground her head goes into it
and the ground bursts round her (`midair_attack2_impact`, four fifteenths
of a second): every opponent standing on the ground within 64 units
either side (`impact`, a `quake`: one in the air is passed by) takes 3 at
Base Launch 2, thrown up off its feet. Once per airtime; half her drift
kept. A dive that runs out in the air lands nothing and falls on.

### The Rocket Headbutt: her way back

`attack3` launches her up head-first (`motion: { type: 'rise', speed: 620
}`): on the ground after a fifteenth of a second set, in the air at once.
Her first strike carries whoever it meets up with her (1, `carry`), her
headbutt then sends it sky-high (3, Base Launch 3 straight up). A fifth of a
second rising, about 120 units, then she comes round and falls. On the
ground half her speed kept; in the air 0.6 of her drift, once per airtime
(landing or being hit gives it back).

### The Earth Chop: the ground split

A technique, a cast ([combat: techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)):
both arms raised high (`attack4_cast`, four frames at 10 fps, two fifths
of a second, nothing protects it: a hit breaks it and nothing splits),
then down on her punch into the ground, and the ground bursts round her
(`attack4_release`, five frames at 15 fps, a third of a second): every
opponent standing on the ground within 120 units of her, either side (its
`burst`, a `quake`: one in the air is passed by, so a jump clears it),
takes **10 at Base Launch 3 straight up**. A raised Shield blocks it.
Once every 5 seconds (the policy's for 10 damage), from the release; no
Energy. It pairs with Finders Keepers: an opponent she has emptied of
Energy cannot raise a Shield, and has only a jump left to answer it.

### Mash Combo

Her pair is the **Spin Kick** (`attack1`) and the **Stick Poke**
(`extra_attack`), her two close-range strikes (out to 40 and 34 units).
Finders Keepers is a touch whose next use throws, Kiiin! a roll, her
headbutts motions and the Earth Chop a technique: none could be a partner
(a mimic is refused as one, [mash combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo)).
Five rapid presses of either one's button on the ground set off her Mash
Combo: twelve turns, the mashed move first, then the other, alternating,
each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11 launching nothing (their damage still
counts) and the twelfth with its own launch.

## Movement

None of her own: she runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. She brings the art: her `mouvment` clip, a
sprint, her arms swinging (3 frames), shown across the whole Dash, and her
`midair_mouvment`, a forward roll tucked into a ball (4 frames: tucked,
over, upside down, round), across the whole air dash. Her canon's speed
is in her moves (Kiiin!), never in how she runs.

Her attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and her close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Her `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: her fists up,
held up before her, and lowered. In the air the Shield button is her
**Deflect** (her `deflect` entry, [defense: the
Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -12, y: -62, w: 58, h: 66 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

A hop, her mouth wide open (the startup), the shout, flashing white, for
two frames (live: 12 units behind her to 46 in front, from below her feet
to over her cap), and her mouth still open (its recovery): four fifteenths
of a second, keeping the whole of her drift. While it is live its box
strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here sideways, blown away,
and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower,
now #0017's. No cooldown; starting it costs nothing (never while
exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back costs 15 Energy, as
every Deflect does.

Her Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Nothing of her own creates
anything, so nothing of hers costs Energy but a throw of a copy (15 for an
ordinary one, nothing for one with a 3 s or 5 s cooldown); the Earth Chop
(5 s) costs nothing. Her Finders Keepers is the one move in the roster that
gives a fighter Energy: all of the Energy of an opponent with nothing to
copy, hers up to a full bar. She leaves `energy` out and refills at the
shared 12 a second.

Her `launchReaction`: a robot who shrugs off what would floor anyone
else, she is held a little less than most, 0.1 s more stun per 1000
units/s up to 0.35 s, tumbling only from 1250 units/s, and 25° of
steering: a held direction bends a launch as far as most. Her launches are as strong as anyone's, and she rebounds off the
scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of her own).

| `extra_attack` | 6 | 36 | 20 fps | the Stick Poke |

Where a row repeats a drawing, the clip plays the same file again: one
file on disk, never a copy (her stride as she runs off, her finger poked
out and drawn back, her arms raised before the chop, her shout). The sheet
draws some poses more than once (her arms-out stride in three rows, her
shout in three, her knocked-back poses twice, two of her rolls twice):
each was cut once. Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 43,
so every frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and
`visual.height` (43 × 88 / 52 ≈ 73 world units) puts #0017's art pixels at
the roster's common size.

Her kick's arc, the ground bursting round her feet, her stick, her arms
flung out and her finger would pull the automatic anchor (the upper art's
opaque middle) off her body, so every clip authors its own anchors
(`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art): the
middle of her overalls, which every frame draws on her body (the middle of
the ball for her rolls). The frames of one pose (the ground's burst, her
finger poked out, her head-first flight and its arc) are anchored where
their twins' body is, matched on her cap. Every clip stands on the bottom
of its art but the Somersault Headbutt, whose flight's white arc sweeps
under her: that frame stands where its twin without the arc does
(`anchorY`).

What of the sheet is no move: her standing, talking and greeting poses,
her ordinary run's stance, her falls, her lying and getting up, the
electrified and burnt versions of her knocked-back poses, her crouched
peering, her arms thrown back, her sitting on the floor, the dive onto her
belly, the costume she changes into, her head popping off and her body
coming apart, and the small copies of her poses at the bottom of the
sheet.

If her airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load she holds her first
stance frame; an attack, the Shield, the Deflect, the Dash or the air dash
whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A small, cartoon-strong brawler whose game plan changes with every
opponent. Up close, the Spin Kick and the Stick Poke are her quick strings
and her Mash Combo; Kiiin! closes distance in a blink and bowls over
whoever is in the way; the Rocket Headbutt is her anti-air and her way
back; the Headbutt Dive punishes from above and bursts the ground under
anyone waiting where she lands. Her two Specials are where she is played:
**Finders Keepers** has to land, short and slow, on an opponent who
knows what it does, and what it yields depends on that opponent: against
a shooter, a copy of its best shot (a 10-damage rocket, sphere or
whirlwind against some, a light shot against others) that she must then
land herself, with its Energy cost or its cooldown; against a fighter who
throws nothing, its whole Energy bar, leaving it with no Shield, Dash or
Deflect until it refills, the window for the **Earth Chop**, a 10-damage
split of the ground round her that only a jump escapes. Her weaknesses:
she is small and short-reached, her touch and her chop are long
commitments any blow breaks, her own moves create nothing (no shot of her
own until she has taken one), and every matchup asks a different plan.

**The CPU** plays her from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads her moves as any fighter's (strings, a roll, a lift,
a plunge with its landing, a cast whose burst is a quake), values her
touch by what it would take from its opponent (a copy's worth, or the
Energy it would empty), reads a held copy as the ranged throw it is, and
uses every one of her moves.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 384 a level): #0017 wins 0.49 at Medium, 0.49 at
Hard and 0.42 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range at Medium and
Hard and near its low end at Brutal, as #0014 (0.37) and #0006 (0.36) are:
a fighter hard to pilot is the hardest for the CPU too. At Hard: 12 of 24
against #0001, 10 against #0002, 19 against #0003, 17 against #0004, 6
against #0005, 5 against #0006, 10 against #0007, 13 against #0008, 14
against #0009, 11 against #0010, 14 against #0011, 5 against #0012, 13
against #0013, 13 against #0014, 14 against #0015 and 12 against #0016.
Her weakest matchups are the fighters whose reach and speed keep her
short touch and short kicks away (#0005's long neck and phantom hands,
#0006's three sisters flying, #0012's blade, 5 or 6 of 24); her best,
#0003's and #0004's: #0003 throws nothing, so her touch empties its
Energy and leaves it no Shield against the Earth Chop, and #0004's long
casts give her the room to touch and to chop. Against the shooters her
copies are a real part of her damage (against #0007, its own rocket,
thrown back for 10, landed about once a match at Brutal).

Her first tuning won about 0.47 at Medium, 0.45 at Hard and 0.25 at
Brutal (over two seeds and most of the roster): the Brutal CPU playing her lost the neutral to every
longer reach and took far more than it dealt, and the CPU chased a touch
on every opponent, even one whose best shot was light (a flat bonus on
every touch). The CPU now values a touch only by what its copy deals; her
Kiiin! and her Rocket Headbutt send their target flying at Base Launch 3
(2 before), her Stick Poke at 2 (1 before), the Earth Chop reaches 120
units either side (96 before), her poke comes out a fifteenth of a second
sooner, and a held direction bends a launch on her by 25° (15° before):
the figures above. Her siphon was kept at
the whole bar, as asked: against the four fighters that throw nothing it
is her plan (19 of 24 against #0003 at Hard, 11 against #0010, 13 against
#0008, 6 against #0005). The results swing by about 0.05 from one set of
seeds to the next.

# #0006

#0006's own specification: three small flying sisters who are one fighter.
Its art, its body, its three members and where each stands, each of its
moves and the sister who performs it, its Shield and its Deflect, its
controller layout and touch buttons, and how it plays. Its fantasy is a
trio who fly and fight side by side as one: three individuals, always seen
together, moving, guarding, getting hurt and flying off as a single
fighter, each lending the group her own powers. That is in how it is drawn
and in who performs each of its moves (an `ensemble`), never its
locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does.
It is part of the product specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md)
§7.2.9): the shared rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

## One fighter, three members

#0006 is the roster's first **ensemble**: a fighter drawn as several
members ([`js/data/ensemble.js`](../../js/data/ensemble.js),
[rendering: ensembles](../systems/rendering.md#ensembles)). It is still ONE
of everything that makes a fighter:

- one definition, one roster slot, one `Fighter` built from it, one
  controller (a player's or the CPU's) and one set of inputs;
- one physics body (position, velocity, ground contact, facing), one
  collider and one pushbox;
- one hurtbox: a hit on it is one hit, its damage added once to the one
  Launch Point;
- one combat state: one Launch Point, one Energy bar, one Shield, one set
  of cooldowns, one stun, one hitstop, one launch;
- one HUD card, one name tag, one Energy bar and one set of cooldown
  indicators over it; the camera frames its one body.

What it has three of is art. Each member has her own clips, her own place in
the group (her `home`, art pixels from the fighter's origin, facing right,
mirrored with the fighter's facing) and her own animator, and all three are
always drawn while it is in play.

| Member | Home | Drawn | Her art | Her moves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pink` | `{ x: 0, y: -14 }`: in the middle, hovering 14 art pixels over her sisters | first (behind) | red bow, orange hair, pink dress; 43 art pixels tall | the Power Punch, Heat Vision, Ice Breath |
| `green` | `{ x: -19, y: 0 }`: 19 behind | second | black hair, green dress; 30 tall | the Tornado Spin, the Flying Punch, the Deflect |
| `blue` | `{ x: 19, y: 0 }`: 19 in front | last (in front) | blonde pigtails, blue dress; 30 tall | the Flying Kick, the Sonic Scream |

All three perform the Triple Strike.

Everything that happens to #0006 happens to all three on the same fixed
step, each in her own clip: the same jump velocity, gravity and landing,
the same Dash, the same hit, the same launch. Their places are presentation
only: they never move the body, add a hurtbox or change a hitbox.

- **Idle**: each floats, up to 3 art pixels off her place and back every
  1.4 s, a third of a beat apart, faded in as she starts to idle and out as
  she stops (never below her place, never while running, jumping or
  fighting).
- **A move a member performs** (each move names its `members`): a single
  performer steps to the fighter's origin, where any fighter's art stands
  (so the move's hitbox, measured from her art, lines up), and the others
  step back behind her, the one nearest the front first, 18 art pixels
  behind and 18 apart, idling meanwhile. All three keep their places for
  the Triple Strike. When a move is over, everyone glides home. The
  performers are drawn in front.
- **Gliding**: a member never jumps between places: she moves toward hers
  at 260 art pixels a second, every step. Turning glides the group over to
  its mirrored places (green and blue pass each other, under a fifth of a
  second).

The tuning lives in its `ensemble` entry: `retreat` 18, `spacing` 18,
`glide` 260, `asideAnimation: 'idle'`, `float: { height: 3, period: 1.4 }`.

## Its canon

What the three sisters really do, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): three small sisters who fly,
with super strength and super speed, who fight as a team and stream through
the sky in trails of their own colours. Each has her own powers besides:
the leader, pink, the strongest at heat vision (beams from her eyes) and an
icy breath that freezes what it reaches; the toughest, green, a brawler who
spins herself into a tornado; the sweetest, blue, whose sonic scream blasts
whatever is in front of her. All three throw force fields up to guard.
So: the Power Punch, the Flying Kick and the Flying Punch are their
strength in a fight; the Tornado Spin is green's tornado; Heat Vision,
Ice Breath and the Sonic Scream are their own powers, each the sister's
whose it is; the Triple Strike is the three of them streaking at their foe
together in their colours; the Shield is their force fields. The sheet's
fireballs are no move (not a power of theirs), nor its being caught and
shocked, its knockout or its icons.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. Its movement is in its moves: the Tornado Spin's
roll, the Triple Strike's lock-on dash. It brings the art: each member's
two-frame `mouvment` (a lunge into flight, then flat out, fist first)

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: all three raise
a force-field bubble in their own colour at once (a frame each), one Shield:
one protection, one Energy bar, one block for each hit (15 Energy). The
shared Shield ring wraps the whole group.

In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry,
[defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), and **green
alone performs it** (`deflect.members: ['green']`): she steps to the front
while pink and blue drop back behind her.

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  members: ['green'],
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -4, y: -60, w: 36, h: 62 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  mobility: 'medium',
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Her arm drawn up (frame 1), then her whirl, an arc swept round in front of
her from over her head to her feet (frame 2, held two frames, live, out to
32 units), then done (frame 3): a little over a quarter of a second. Every
Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here sideways; 15 Energy as it starts; no
cooldown. While it is live it turns the other fighter's projectiles back at
their thrower, now #0006's.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)); its own is
only its refill, 14 / s (`energy: { regen: 14 }`). Its `launchReaction`:
0.15 s more stun per 1000 units/s up to 0.55 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s,
20° of steering (they fly: a launch is easier to fight).

## How it plays

A versatile trio for every range. Up close, pink's quick one-two and blue's
flying kick start its pressure, and green's flying punch, slow to wind up,
is the blow that ends lives. The Triple Strike closes the gap: all three
lock on and streak in together, on the ground or once in the air, its
other way to launch. From afar, pink's beam is the fastest shot in the
game, and blue's scream carries an opponent away, turns their shots back
and shoves a Shield. Ice Breath is its turn of the tide: whoever the gale
reaches stands frozen long enough to take a launch. Its weaknesses are its
commitments: every technique roots the sister who casts it, the Tornado
Spin is a long roll that a blocked or whiffed start leaves wide open, and
the Flying Punch's wind-up can be seen coming.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (melee, ranged, techniques,
a homing dash, a pull, a freeze) and presses them through the one fighter's
controls; nothing in it knows about members.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 120 a level): #0006 wins 0.51 at Medium, 0.43 at
Hard and 0.36 at Brutal (the other fighters 0.45 to 0.63 over the same
kind of runs); at Hard 8 of 24 against #0001, 7 against #0002, 9 against
#0003, 15 against #0004 and 13 against #0005. The results swing by about
0.1 from one set of seeds to the next.

As first written it won 0.85 at Hard: the Triple Strike locked on from 280
units after a twelfth of a second, struck a 70 × 80 box for 5 and carried
the whole kit. Its lock-on came down to 220, a sixth of a second, a 50 × 64
box; with nothing else to end lives it then won 0.05, until the Flying
Punch became its finisher (Base Launch 3, with a fifth of a second of
wind-up). Its first body, 60 units wide round the whole group with a
pushbox of 52, made it the easiest target in the roster and kept it out of
its own Deflect's reach against itself; it is now a single fighter's size.
Its launch reaction (more stun and tumbling on hard launches, which every
fighter has) had been left out, so it never tumbled and the CPU judged it
never in danger. Ice Breath's breath-in came down from 0.4 s to a third of
a second, its gale from 0.58 s to 0.47, and a blocked gale presses on the
Shield for 0.3 s: a CPU protecting a late lead was being hit through its
wind-up. Its Flying Kick and Flying Punch had step-ins, which move a
fighter only on the ground: they were removed.

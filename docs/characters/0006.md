# #0006

#0006's own specification: three small flying sisters who are one fighter.
Its art, its body, its three members and where each stands, its Shield and
its Deflect, and what is still to come. Its fantasy is a trio who fly and
fight side by side as one: three individuals, always seen together, moving,
guarding, getting hurt and flying off as a single fighter. That is in how
it is drawn and in who performs each of its moves (an `ensemble`), never
its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter
does. It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies on
are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and
nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

## One fighter, three members

#0006 is the roster's first **ensemble**: a fighter drawn as several
members ([`js/data/ensemble.js`](../../js/data/ensemble.js),
[rendering: ensembles](../systems/rendering.md#ensembles)). It is still ONE
of everything that makes a fighter:

- one definition, one roster slot, one `Fighter` built from it, one
  controller (a player's or the CPU's) and one set of inputs;
- one physics body (position, velocity, ground contact, facing), one
  collider and one pushbox;
- one hurtbox round the group: a hit anywhere on it is one hit, its damage
  added once to the one Launch Point;
- one combat state: one Launch Point, one Energy bar, one Shield, one set
  of cooldowns, one stun, one hitstop, one launch;
- one HUD card, one name tag, one Energy bar and one set of cooldown
  indicators over it; the camera frames its one body.

What it has three of is art. Each member has her own clips, her own place in
the group (her `home`, art pixels from the fighter's origin, facing right,
mirrored with the fighter's facing) and her own animator, and all three are
always drawn while it is in play.

| Member | Home | Drawn | Her art |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pink` | `{ x: 0, y: -14 }`: in the middle, hovering 14 art pixels over her sisters | first (behind) | red bow, orange hair, pink dress; 43 art pixels tall |
| `green` | `{ x: -19, y: 0 }`: 19 behind | second | black hair, green dress; 30 tall |
| `blue` | `{ x: 19, y: 0 }`: 19 in front | last (in front) | blonde pigtails, blue dress; 30 tall |

Everything that happens to #0006 happens to all three on the same fixed
step, each in her own clip: the same jump velocity, gravity and landing,
the same Dash, the same hit, the same launch. Their places are presentation
only: they never move the body, add a hurtbox or change a hitbox.

- **Idle**: each floats, up to 3 art pixels off her place and back every
  1.4 s, a third of a beat apart, faded in as she starts to idle and out as
  she stops (never below her place, never while running, jumping or
  fighting).
- **A move a member performs** (a move of an ensemble names its `members`):
  a single performer steps to the fighter's origin, where any fighter's art
  stands (so the move's hitbox, measured from her art, lines up), and the
  others step back behind her, the one nearest the front first, 18 art
  pixels behind and 18 apart, idling meanwhile. Two or three performers
  keep their places, centred on the origin; a move may place anyone itself
  (`formation`). When it is over, everyone glides home. The performers are
  drawn in front.
- **Gliding**: a member never jumps between places: she moves toward hers
  at 260 art pixels a second, every step. Turning glides the group over to
  its mirrored places (green and blue pass each other, under a fifth of a
  second).

The tuning lives in its `ensemble` entry: `retreat` 18, `spacing` 18,
`glide` 260, `asideAnimation: 'idle'`, `float: { height: 3, period: 1.4 }`.

## Buttons and moves

None yet: it has no `actions`, no `attacks`, no summons and no techniques.
Its moves are to be designed from its canon; until they are, nothing is
invented to fill its buttons, and the loadout rules wait for them (a
fighter whose moves are still to come: unplayable and moveless,
[loadouts](../systems/combat.md#loadouts)). Every button does nothing for
it; the CPU never presses one.

When they are designed, each names who performs it:

```js
attack1: { animation: 'attack1', members: ['pink'], ... },          // the ordinary case: one member
attack3: { animation: 'attack3', members: ['pink', 'blue'], ... },  // a combination of two, written as one move
attack4: { animation: 'attack4', members: ['pink', 'green', 'blue'], formation: { ... }, ... }, // all three
```

and each performer has the move's clip among her own (`attack1` in pink's
`animations`, `0006_attack1_pink_1.png` ...). A combination is one move:
one press, one cooldown, one set of authored strikes, never a strike per
member.

## The Deflect

The one move it already has is the universal one the Shield button makes in
the air. **Green alone performs it** (`deflect.members: ['green']`): she
steps to the front while pink and blue drop back behind her. Her arm drawn
up (frame 1), then her whirl, an arc swept round in front of her from over
her head to her feet (frame 2, held two frames, live: `{ x: -4, y: -60, w:
36, h: 62 }`), then done (frame 3): 4 frames at 15 fps, a little over a
quarter of a second. Every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here sideways
(`directionalLaunch: 'horizontal'`); hitstun 0.3 s, blockstun 0.12 s,
hitstop 0.05 s; 15 Energy as it starts; no cooldown; steered as a medium
strike. While it is live it turns the other fighter's projectiles back.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

- **Shield** (on the ground): all three raise a force-field bubble in their
  own colour at once (`prepshield`, `shielding`, `releaseshield`, a frame
  each), one Shield: one protection, one Energy bar, one block for each hit
  (15 Energy). Its perfect Shield opens for 0.1 s after 0.25 s down. The
  shared Shield ring wraps the whole group.
- **Energy**: the universal bar and costs, refilling at the default 12 a
  second.
- **Launch reaction and rebounds**: the shared defaults (no
  `launchReaction` or `launchBounce` of its own).

## Art and animations

Every member's clips are cut at 1× from the supplied sheet, the flat grey
background made transparent, each frame one sprite of the sheet (and any
stray pixels of its own inside its box), cropped tight; none redrawn,
recoloured or scaled. Each clip is sized by its tallest frame over its
member's idle (`heightRatio`), and stands on the bottom of its art. A frame
shown twice in a clip is one file.

| Codename | pink | green | blue | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` (hovering, a loop) | 7 frames (files 1–5, 1, 6) | 3 | 3 | 6 fps |
| `run` (flying along low, a loop at 0.6–1.6 × speed) | 7 | 5 | 6 | 12 fps |
| `jump` (rising, an arm up) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 10 fps |
| `fall` (dropping, arms out) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 10 fps |
| `land` (hands together, a knee bent) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 15 fps |
| `hurt` (dazed) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 12 fps |
| `midair_hurt` (spinning, a loop) | 3 | 3 | 3 | 12 fps |
| `prepshield`, `shielding`, `releaseshield` (her bubble forming, held, shrinking) | 1 each | 1 each | 1 each | 15 fps |
| `mouvment` (the Dash: a lunge, then flat out) | 2 | 2 | 2 | once across the Dash |
| `midair_mouvment` (the air dash: flying forward) | 1 | 1 | 1 | once across the air dash |
| `deflect` (green's whirl) | | 3 (4 shown: 1, 2, 2, 3) | | 15 fps |

What the sheet lacks, and what was chosen in its place from its own frames
(nothing generated):

- **No walk or run on the ground**: they fly. The run is their low,
  horizontal flight; the Dash its fastest pose.
- **No jump or fall cycle**: one rising pose (an arm thrust up) and one
  dropping pose (arms out) each.
- **No hurt pose standing**: the first, upright frame of each one's
  knock-out spin is the hurt; its three other turns are the mid-air hurt.
- **The bubbles grow larger still** on the sheet (55–59 art pixels); the
  held Shield is their middle size (42–45), so the three cluster rather than
  bury each other.

The sheet's other frames (punches, kicks, a dash punch, eye beams, an icy
breath, a sonic scream, fireballs, a whirlwind, being caught and shocked,
the knock-out's last frame, the icons) are not used yet: they are art for
moves still to be designed.

The **portrait** (the HUD and the roster) is the group: all three idling at
home, cropped round their heads (`visual.portrait`, `centerY` 0.4, `size`
0.8 of the group's width). The roster's preview shows all three idling.

## What is still to come

#0006 is registered and drawn, but not playable. Before `available: true`:

- its moves, designed from its canon (attack1 and attack2 at least, each
  with its mid-air version, each naming its performer(s)), with their art
  cut from the sheet under each performer's own codenames;
- its controller layout, chosen by the comfort rules for those moves (today
  `controller.layout` is `{}`: nothing to bind);
- its touch buttons, ability names and descriptions, in both languages;
- its Discover profile (a difficulty and a play-style description);
- its balance against the roster.

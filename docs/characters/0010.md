# #0010

#0010's own specification: the traitor in the red spacesuit. Its art,
each of its moves, its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its
fantasy is one of a spaceship's crew to look at, a small figure in a suit
and a visor, and secretly the thing hunting them: it kills when nobody is
looking and must wait between kills, slips through the ship's vents to
come out somewhere else, calls the crew to the emergency button when it
suits it, and under the suit is no person at all: its head opens on a
mouth and a long tongue, and its body bursts into tentacles. That is in
its moves (a kill twice as strong on a back turned to it, a way under the
floor, a meeting that calls everyone to it and holds them, a tongue that
reels its target in), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes
exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: Punches
and the Pistol Whip are strings (`hits`) that hold their target for the
last blow; the Tongue Lash reaches far ahead and reels whoever it strikes
in to its feet (`pull`, its front only); the Kill is a stab twice as strong
on a back turned to it (`backstab`, a capability any hit may now use), and
its 10 earns it the policy's 5 s, its kill cooldown; the Emergency Meeting
calls everyone near to the button (a call that stands where it goes up,
with a `pull` either side) and holds whoever reaches it there
(`paralyze`); and the Vent takes it under the floor and out further along
it (`motion: tunnel`, a motion any ground attack may now use), where all
of it there is to hit is the vent's hatch, bursting out at whoever stands
there.

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): it lives among a ship's
crew, looking just like them, and hunts them. Its one weapon of note is
the kill, made up close and unseen: a knife in the back, a pistol fired
point-blank, its tongue shot through a victim from a distance; and after
each kill it must wait before it can kill again. It travels through the
ship's vents, diving in at one and climbing out of another, unseen on the
way; it sabotages the ship, and it can call (or have called) an emergency
meeting, when everyone is brought to the table at once and nobody moves
until it is over. Under the suit is no person: its head opens on a toothed
mouth and a long tongue, and its body can burst into tentacles. It heals
nobody, summons nobody, throws nothing of its own, never homes in on
anyone and cannot vent in mid-air. So: its moves are its fists, its pistol
and its knife, its tongue and its tentacles, the vents and the emergency
button; its kill is strongest unseen, and waits between uses; its guard is
its fists raised; its Deflect its body rolled over in a spin; and nothing
it does is a power its canon does not give it.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; and an extra attack, its Vent (ground only). The sheet's walk,
crouch and somersault, its taunts, wins and losses, its tilts and smashes
(the bone swing, the turn, the overhead spark, the little figure it
throws up off its head, the ground spikes, the low spin, the stomp), its
knife's other slash, its blaster, its dive, its belly's mouth, its egg of
tentacles, its grab and throw, its falls and getting up, its revival
platform, its target-lock taunt and sights, its ejection and the rest of
the crew are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'extra_attack', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Kill, twice as
strong on a back turned to it and then held back for its kill cooldown;
the Vent, a way under the floor to somewhere else; and the Emergency
Meeting, which calls everyone near to it and holds them there. All three
sit under Special abilities in the controller editor, and an orange
exclamation mark floats over #0010 while it performs one (the Kill 0.2 s
+ 0.4 s, the Meeting 0.25 s + 1/3 s, the Vent 0.7 s). The Tongue Lash's
reel is a pull like others' and ordinary to the player.

### The kill: unseen, then wait

The Kill is the roster's first hit with a **backstab**
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):
its burst's hit declares `backstab: { damage: 10, baseLaunch: 3,
directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.6, hitstop: 0.16 }`. A target
whose back is turned to where the hit comes from (`backTurned` in
[`hit-effects.js`](../../js/game/combat/hit-effects.js): the hit comes
from the side it faces away from) takes that instead of the hit's own 5 at
Base Launch 2: twice the damage, thrown away far harder, the event marked
`backstab`. A Shield blocks it like any hit, either way round; a blocked
hit is no backstab. The cooldown policy weighs a move by the harder of its
hits, so the Kill's 10 gives it the policy's 5 s from its release: its
kill cooldown, the wait between kills its canon has. Getting behind an
opponent is the whole game of it: crossing over them in a jump, coming
out of the Vent past them, catching them turned away mid-move or
fleeing. Any hit of any fighter may declare a backstab; the CPU values one
where its target's back is turned (`values.js`).

### The Vent: a way under the floor

The Vent is the roster's first **tunnel**
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
an attack motion for the ground: `motion: { type: 'tunnel', distance:
240, hide: 0.15, at: 0.35, show: 0.45, hurtboxes: [{ x: -22, y: -30, w:
44, h: 30 }] }`. From `hide` #0010 is inside the floor: it passes through
fighters, no pull moves it, and all of it there is to hit is the vent's
hatch (`hurtboxes`, 44 units wide and 30 tall on the floor). It is never
out of reach: there is no invulnerability anywhere in Alva, and a low blow
on the hatch flushes it out, ending the move, while a blow at head height
passes over. At `at` it moves at once (drawn nowhere in between) up to
240 units the way it faces along the ground it stands on (`tunnelExit` in
[`movement.js`](../../js/game/fighters/movement.js): its middle stays over
ground at the height its feet stand at, so never past a ledge, a gap or a
step, never into the Void and never inside a solid, though under a wall
standing on that ground), and at `show` it climbs out, its own hurtbox
back. Its clip draws what is seen meanwhile: the vent alone, falling to
pieces behind it, then another vent's lid and opening where it comes out.
The CPU predicts the move along the floor exactly as the Fighter makes it
(the same `tunnelExit`) and reads the Vent's reach as its strike swept along
the floor, so it plans to come out under its opponent.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Punches** (`attack1`) and the **Pistol Whip** (`attack2`),
its two close-range strikes (out to 38 and 60 units). The Tongue Lash
reaches far and pulls, the Vent travels, and its techniques are no strikes
of the body, so none could be a partner. Five rapid presses of either
one's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its own
launch (48 at the most over the series).

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its two-frame `mouvment`
clip, a headlong lunge then flung forward, played once across the whole
Dash, and its one-frame `midair_mouvment`, its body flung out flat, visor
first, across the whole air dash. Its canon's venting is in its moves (the
Vent), never in how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: braced (one
frame), its fists brought up before it (one), settling into a squat as they
come down (one). In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
its body rolled over sideways:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 20, active: 3 / 20, recovery: 1 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: -30, y: -50, w: 60, h: 52 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the startup (curled), then the spin (frames 2-4, live: all round
it 30 units either side, from its feet to well over its head), its recovery
a frame more (curled again): a quarter of a second, keeping the whole of
its drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base
Launch 2, here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets
back at their thrower, now #0010's. No cooldown; starting it costs nothing (never while exhausted), and the first projectile it turns back costs 15 Energy, once however many it turns back, as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a projectile back, each blocked hit 15;
none of its moves creates anything, so its bar is all for moving, guarding
and Deflecting. It leaves `energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a
second.

Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from
1100 units/s, 15° of steering. Its launches are as strong as anyone's, and
it rebounds off the scenery as everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its
own).

### Small

At its own true height (27 art pixels at the roster's common art-pixel
size, 88 / 52 world units) it stands about 46 units tall, the roster's
smallest by far (#0002, the next, about 66). Its hurtbox is measured from
its art like everyone's, so what is aimed high passes over its head
standing: #0001's Jab (its box from 82 to 56 units up), #0001's Red and
#0007's Pistol and Rocket Launcher shots; every other fighter's Jab meets
it. The CPU reads its body from the same hurtbox and aims accordingly. Its
own strikes are low and short, measured from its art, so it must come
close.

again: one file on disk. The meeting's call is drawn centred where it is,
at the fighter's art-pixel size, never fitted to its height. Each clip's
`heightRatio` is its tallest frame over 27, so every frame normalizes at
exactly one art pixel per file pixel, and `visual.height` (27 × 88 / 52 ≈
45.7 world units) puts #0010's art pixels at the roster's common size. The
sheet's final move draws #0010 at a larger scale than the rest of it: its
frames are no clip.

Its fists, its pistol and the pistol's trail, its knife and the knife's
slash, its tongue, the emergency button and the vent are drawn beside or
round it, and the automatic anchor (the upper art's opaque middle) would
follow them off its body, so every clip authors its own anchors
(`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art): the
middle of its suit, the centroid of the suit's own colours in its upper
rows (the Kill's release: 23.5, 12.5, 14.5, 12, 11.5, 13.5; the meeting's
slam: 14, 14, 12.5, 12.5); a frame of the vent alone on the vent's middle.
Everything stands on the bottom of its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

## How it plays

A small, sly ambusher that wins by catching opponents off guard. Up close,
Punches and the Pistol Whip are its quick strings and its Mash Combo; a
step further out the Tongue Lash reels its target in to its feet, and the
Emergency Meeting calls everyone near to the button and holds them there.
The Vent takes it under the floor and out somewhere else, past an opponent
or away from one; the Kill is worth twice as much on a back turned to it,
so its game is getting behind: a jump over, a vent past, a target turned
away mid-move. In the air the Air Kick and the Tentacle Sweep cover it. Its
weaknesses: its basic blows are short and modest, its kill waits 5 s
between uses and is half as strong from the front, its techniques are
rooted casts any hit breaks, and in the Vent a low blow on the hatch
flushes it out. Its small body is a blessing and a curse: some high blows
pass over it, but it must come close to touch anyone.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, a reel, a long
cast, a call that pulls and holds, a way under the floor whose strike comes
out where the floor allows) and uses every one of them, values a backstab
where its target's back is turned, and predicts the Vent's move along the
floor exactly as the Fighter makes it.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24
matches against each opponent, 216 a level): #0010 wins 0.54 at Medium,
0.42 at Hard and 0.45 at Brutal, at the edge of the roster's usual range
(the other fighters 0.43 to 0.63 over the same kind of runs). At Hard: 21
of 24 against #0001, 3 against #0002, 7 against #0003, 12 against #0004,
10 against #0005, 6 against #0006, 11 against #0007, 7 against #0008 and
14 against #0009. Its one-sided matchup is #0001's (21 of 24 at Medium and
Hard, 17 at Brutal): #0001's Jab and its Red pass over #0010's head
([small](#small)). Its weakest is against #0002, whose rushdown is as low
and as quick as its own. Its CPU uses its Mash Combo most, then the Pistol
Whip, the Emergency Meeting (about twelve a match), Punches and its
aerials; the Tongue Lash five or six times a match, the Kill about twice
and the Vent about once. The results swing by about 0.1 from one set of
seeds to the next.

# #0007

#0007's own specification: the street soldier. Its art, each of its
moves, its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is a
fighter of the streets who fights two ways, half and half: with its fists
and feet, always up close, and with the weapons it carries, every one of
them as it uses them in its canon, fired standing on the ground. That is
in its moves (a string that holds, a launcher, a spike, a fast shot, a fire
that burns where it lands, blasts to both sides at once, a heavy rocket
that roots it while it aims), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and
Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: the
Street Combo is a string (`hits`) that holds its target to the last blow;
the Uppercut a launcher whose hit-confirmed window lets a jump chase its
target; the Hammer Fist a spike; the Molotov Cocktail's fire a projectile
that never moves (`speed: 0`) and strikes again and again (`pierce`),
appearing ahead of #0007, never at its feet; the Sawn-Offs a burst round
#0007 that reaches both sides at once; the Rocket Launcher a long,
breakable cast and a 10-damage rocket that shoves a raised Shield back
(`blockPush`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a man of the streets who
fights hand to hand (he learns boxing and other fighting styles: punches
thrown in combinations, kicks, a block with the fists raised) and with the
weapons he picks up: pistols, sawn-off shotguns (one in each hand once he
has mastered them), molotov cocktails that set the ground ablaze where they
smash, and a rocket launcher, a heavy weapon he cannot jump, run or crouch
with, slow to bring to bear and devastating when it lands. He rolls aside
while aiming and dives when he leaps from a sprint. He fires no weapon in
mid-air. So: the Street Combo, the Uppercut, the Flying Kick and the Hammer
Fist are his fists and feet; the Pistol, the Molotov Cocktail, the
Sawn-Offs and the Rocket Launcher are his weapons, every one fired standing
(a technique, or a ground-only shot); its guard is a boxer's high guard;
its Dash is the combat roll and its air dash the dive. Nothing it does is a
power its canon does not give it: no summon, no heal, nothing conjured.

The owner asked for its moves to be half melee and half weapons, every
melee move close range: four of each. Its melee are its ordinary attacks
(the only moves it has in the air), each reaching no further than an arm's
or a leg's length (72 units at the most, the Flying Kick's foot); its
weapons are its three techniques and its extra attack. The Deflect, the
Shield button's move in the air that every fighter has, is a melee swat.

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` and `attack2`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions (its melee), `attack3` to
`attack5` techniques (its weapons, ground only); and an extra attack, its
Pistol (ground only). The sheet's intro, its fighting stance, its other
guns (the sniper rifle, the submachine gun, the assault rifle), its katana
and shovel, its rocket launcher fired in the air (a heavy weapon is never
fired off the ground in its canon), its motorcycle and train, its wins and
losses and its spare poses are no move.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its four-frame `mouvment`
clip, the combat roll (curled into a ball and rolled over once), played
once across the whole Dash, and its two-frame `midair_mouvment`, the dive

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: a boxer's high
guard, the fists raised before its face (one frame), held tight (one),
lowered to a fighting pose (one). In the air the Shield button is its
**Deflect** (its `deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
the two-fisted swat:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -4, y: -104, w: 64, h: 108 },
  directionalLaunch: 'reverseVertical', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the fists drawn back, then the swat (frames 2-3, live: the front of
the body from over the head to under the feet, out to 60 units, the arc the
fists sweep), the fists low a frame more: a little over a quarter of a
second, keeping the whole of its drift. While it is live its box strikes
for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here straight down, the way the
fists swat, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at
their thrower, now #0007's. No cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts,
as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15,
and its Pistol, Molotov Cocktail and Sawn-Offs 15 each. Its own is only
its refill, 14 / s (`energy: { regen: 14 }`), as most of the roster's.
Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from
1150 units/s, 20° of steering.

## How it plays

A street fighter split down the middle. Up close, the Street Combo is its
everyday string, holding its target to the hook, and the Uppercut throws
it up for a chase; in the air the Flying Kick sends far and the Hammer Fist
drives down, a spike off the stage. From afar it fights with what it
carries: the Pistol is a quick, long shot that stops an approach, the
Molotov Cocktail sets the ground ahead on fire (an approach walks into it,
and it never lands at #0007's feet), the Sawn-Offs answer an opponent who
gets either side of it, and the Rocket Launcher, slow to bring to bear,
ends lives from across the stage. Its weaknesses are its weapons' cost and
commitments: three of them spend Energy it also needs to Dash and guard,
none works in the air, and the rocket's long heave is broken by any hit.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (melee strings, a launcher,
a spike, a shot, a lingering fire, a burst round it, a long cast) and uses
every one of them.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 144 a level): #0007 wins 0.43 at Medium, 0.49 at
Hard and 0.56 at Brutal (the other fighters 0.45 to 0.63 over the same
kind of runs); at Hard 15 of 24 against #0001, 13 against #0002, 8 against
#0003, 8 against #0004, 17 against #0005 and 9 against #0006. Strongest
against #0005 and #0001; weakest against #0003 and #0004. The results swing
by about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the next. With its first idle, the
crouched fighting stance (a body 72 units tall), it won 0.47, 0.52 and
0.59: standing, its body is 90 units tall, an easier target.

As first written it won 0.67 at Medium, 0.59 at Hard and 0.67 at Brutal:
its Pistol fired a fifteenth of a second after the press, and its CPU fired
it more than anything else. Given a sixth of a second to draw, it fell to
0.33 at Medium; it now draws in 2/15 s (a third of a second in all, its
range 560 units from 630), and its Energy refills at 14 a second, as most
of the roster's, since three of its weapons spend it. Three values were set
by the CPU's match-level behaviour scenarios
([`cpu-behaviour.test.mjs`](../../tests/systems/cpu-behaviour.test.mjs)):
the Rocket Launcher's heave is 0.75 s (at 0.5 s the CPU judged a rocket
lethal wherever it pushed an opponent off the ledge, whatever the Void
closing in overtime), the Flying Kick launches at Base Launch 2 and the
Hammer Fist deals 3 (at Base Launch 1 and 5, the CPU by a ledge reached for
the Hammer Fist's damage, a spike that cannot end a life on the stage,
more often than for a launch that can).

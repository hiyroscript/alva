# #0004

#0004's own specification: the virtual singer. Its art, each of its moves,
its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is a singer whose
voice is its power: its song charms whoever hears it, it sings notes across
the stage, its signature leek is its weapon, and its little leek-waving
double dances at its side; its strikes are dance steps. That is in its
moves (a charm, a dancing obstacle, a note that turns projectiles back, a
climb that carries its target), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and
Dashes exactly as every other fighter does. It is part of the product
specification ([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared
rules it relies on are specified there and explained in
[`docs/systems/`](../systems/), and nothing here is a rule for any other
fighter.

Every move is a mechanic of its own, not just a pose and damage. Two of
them are features #0004 brought to the shared system, any fighter's to use
([combat: attack
mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)):

- **The charm** (`charm: seconds`, a hit effect): a real hit charms its
  target for that long. Until it runs out, every hit the charmed fighter
  would land on whoever charmed it (a strike, a projectile, anything) is no
  hit at all: it heals that fighter instead, taking the hit's damage off
  its Launch Point, and launches, stuns and freezes nothing. The charmed
  fighter can still move, jump, Dash and Shield, and hit anyone else; a
  raised Shield still blocks it, and a blocked hit charms nobody. A pink
  heart floats over a charmed fighter, and the charmer glows as each
  charmed hit heals it. A charm is an exceptional effect: its move waits
  the policy's 3 s.
- **A burst's art** (a technique burst's `effect`): the art the burst plays
  as it goes out, an effect clip of its own, centred at an offset from the
  fighter and played once where it went out, behind the fighters. Art only.

It also uses strikes (`hits`), `carry`, attack `motion` (`rise`, `slam`), a
stationary piercing projectile (`speed: 0`, `pierce`, `finisher`), a
projectile that turns others back (`repel`), `blockPush`, `airUses` and a
Deflect that turns projectiles back (`deflectProjectiles`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a singer whose voice is
its power; the leek is its signature item (its official appearances have
it fight by dancing through enemies with one, and its rhythm-game
minigames throw leeks); its signature song ability charms nearby enemies,
and a charmed enemy's hits heal it instead of hurting it; its chibi double
waves a leek to the polka its leek became famous through; it dances. So:
World is Mine (a song of its own) is the charm, exactly as its canon has
it; Ievan Polkka is the little leek-waving double; the leek is the Leek
Swing and the Leekspin; the Melody is its voice; the Dance Combo, the Air
Dance, the Crescent Kick and the Stage Dive are dance steps. Nothing it
does is a power its canon does not give it: no fireball, no teleport.

ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`

victory and defeat poses and its full-screen cut-in pictures are no move.

### Each move in detail

- **Dance Combo** (`attack1`, 7 frames at 20 fps, 0.35 s): the crouch and the
  spin, the hair flying up (frames 1-2), the backhand swipe with its trail
  (frames 3-4, a box out to 36 units at the chest; 1, no launch, hitstun
  0.3: it holds its target for the punch), the arm drawn back, the punch
  (frame 6, out to 36 units; 3, Base Launch 1 sideways), back. Its first
  strike at 0.1 s, so a Dash attack still lands at speed. Hit-cancel from
  frame 7 once it has hit. A quick strike: it keeps all the speed it
  starts with and the run carries on through the string while held (let
  go, it slides on under 0.6 of the ground deceleration). Ground only. A
  Shield that blocks the swipe stops the string.
- **Air Dance** (`midair_attack1`, 7 frames at 20 fps, 0.35 s): a knee and a
  leg swung back (frames 1-2), the low sweep (frame 3, its trail under the
  body; 1), the rising crescent and the high kick (frames 4-5; 3, Base Launch
  2 upward), back (frames 6-7). A quick strike, steered almost as well as
  it drifts; hit-cancel from frame 6.
- **Leek Swing** (`attack2`, 3 frames at 10 fps, 0.4 s): the leek raised
  overhead (frame 1, 0.1 s), the swing (frame 2, live: from over the head to
  the ground in front, out to 48 units, its longest reach), held low (frame
  3, two frames). 3, Base Launch 2 sideways, `blockPush` 280. Ground only, no
  follow-up. A medium strike.
- **Leekspin** (`midair_attack2`, 5 frames at 15 fps): the leek gathered up
  (frame 1, a hang), then `rise` at 480 units/s through frames 2-4 (about 96
  units, and on up under gravity). The upward swing and the turn overhead
  (frames 2 and 3; 1 each, no launch) carry whoever they catch up with it
  (`carry`, lift 0: the target takes its velocity); the swing down (frame
  4, a box round it; 3, Base Launch 2 sideways) flings the target away from
  the top of the climb. Drifting on frame 5. Once per airtime (`airUses:
  1`); a medium strike, its lift steering 0.4, toward 0.4 of top speed.
- **Crescent Kick** (`attack3`, 8 frames at 20 fps, 0.4 s): the stance and
  the knee drawn up (frames 1-3), the spin and the high kick (frames 4-6,
  live: the arc the leg sweeps, from the chest to well over the head, out
  to 42 units), the leg coming down and back (frames 7-8). 3, Base Launch 2
  straight up. Hit-cancel from frame 7 (a jump after it). Ground only. A
  medium strike.
- **Stage Dive** (`midair_attack3`, 3 frames at 15 fps, then
  `midair_attack3_impact`, 3 frames): `slam`, a 1/15 s tuck, then 1300
  units/s down (heavy, its dive steering 0.5, toward half of top speed)
  for up to 0.8 s; its feet's box just under the
  body (3, Base Launch 2 downward) strikes whatever it passes, and the dive
  goes on. Landing, the impact (`{ x: -56, y: -34, w: 112, h: 38 }` round
  the landing point; 1, Base Launch 1 upward: no quake, so one just off the
  ground is popped too), then 3/15 s crouched and rising, never cut by the
  landing cancel; hit-cancel from its second frame once anything has been
  hit. Once per airtime. Off the stage it dives into the Void: no ground,
  no landing.
- **Ievan Polkka** (`attack4`, a technique): the cast is `attack4_cast` (4
  frames at 10 fps, 0.4 s: the stance, a breath, a fist raised, the arm
  thrown out to call), the release `attack4_release` (that call held, 1
  frame at 4 fps, 0.25 s). On the release's first step its projectile
  (`attack4_object`) appears 62 units in front and 27 up (its middle: it
  stands on the ground), facing the way #0004 does: `speed: 0` (it never
  moves), `lifetime` 2.4 s, its box `{ -24, -27, 48, 54 }` (its body and the
  leek it waves), `pierce: { hits: 6, interval: 0.3 }`, 1 a strike with
  hitstun 0.34 (just longer than the gap, so a caught target stays caught),
  its `finisher` 5, Base Launch 2 upward. 10 in all: the policy's 5 s from
  the press. A Shield stops it at the first strike it blocks; a Deflect
  turns it, and its strikes, against #0004 (it still does not move).
- **World is Mine** (`attack5`, a technique): the cast is `attack5_cast` (1
  frame at 2 fps, 0.5 s: the microphone at its mouth, singing), the release
  `attack5_release` (1 frame at 3 fps, 1/3 s: the microphone thrust up). On
  the release's first step the burst, `{ x: -150, y: -150, w: 300, h: 160 }`
  round #0004 (the ground and a jump's height above it, either side): 1, no
  launch, hitstun 0.3, `charm` 2.5; its `effect`, the ring of sound
  (`attack5_object`, 39 frames at 30 fps, about 1.3 s), plays centred 30

  cast. While a fighter it reached is charmed, every hit it would land on

- **Melody** (`extra_attack`, 4 frames at 15 fps, 0.4 s): a breath and a fist

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield',
groundAnimation: 'shielding', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: a
hand raised before it, held. In the air the Shield button is its
**Deflect** (its `deflect` entry, [defense: the
Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)), the uppercut:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 2 / 20, active: 2 / 20, recovery: 1 / 20,
  hitbox: { x: 0, y: -98, w: 40, h: 88 },
  directionalLaunch: 'vertical', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  mobility: 'medium',
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frames 1-2 the fist drawn back, then the uppercut (frames 3 and 4, live:
the front of the body from below the knees to over the head, out to 40
units, the arc the fist sweeps), the fist raised a frame more: a quarter of
a second, keeping the whole of its drift. While it is live its box strikes
for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2, here upward, and turns the other
fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now #0004's. No
cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts, as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 25, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15.
Its own is only its refill, 14 / s (`energy:
{ regen: 14 }`). Its `launchReaction`: 0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.55
s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, 18° of steering: a dancer's balance, quick to
come round in the air.

## How it plays

A rhythm controller. Its Melody and its dancing double hold space at range
(the note turns an incoming projectile back; the double stands where it was
called, and walking into it is six hits and a launch), its Leek Swing
punishes from further than its other strikes, and its Dance Combo and
kicks are quick and light. World is Mine is its turn of the tide: half a
second of singing that an opponent who sees it coming can walk out of or
Shield, but that charms anyone caught (held by the double, landing, in a
stun) for 2.5 s, in which every hit they throw at #0004 heals it: they have
to stop attacking, and #0004 can press on unanswered. In the air it has a
rising leek twirl that carries its target up and flings it, and a dive that
is a spike on the stage and a fall off it. Its weakness is the time its
setups take: rushed at close range before they are in place, it has no
heavy blow to take back the space.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): it values a
charm by the seconds it buys (worth little while one is still running),
sings when its song can reach someone who cannot get away, and, charmed
itself, strikes nothing at its charmer until the charm runs out and keeps
out of its reach.

## Balance

Tuned from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches (full Watch Mode battles, first to 3,
on Desert and City, five seeds each way round: 20 matches per cell; points
#0004 : opponent). Against #0001: Medium 31 : 50, Hard 44 : 45, Brutal
47 : 37. Against #0002: Medium 38 : 51, Hard 53 : 34, Brutal 54 : 36. Against
#0003: Medium 35 : 53, Hard 35 : 49, Brutal 43 : 39. 380 : 394 in all: about
even, weaker when the CPU plays it loosely (Medium) and stronger when it
plays it well (Brutal), as a setup fighter is. At first the CPU never used
its techniques at all: it valued a charm at 2 a second (now 4, worth little
while one is still running), and World is Mine reached only head height
(now a jump's height above #0004). With that it scored 342 : 413 on the same
seeds; a quicker launch reaction (0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.55 s, from
0.18 up to 0.6; tumbling from 1100 rather than 1050; 18° of steering rather
than 15) and Energy refill (14, from the default 12) got it here. (Its Dance
Combo's first strike had already been brought forward to 0.1 s, so that a
Dash attack lands at speed, as every fighter's does.)

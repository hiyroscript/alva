# #0014

#0014's own specification: the long-haired martial artist in a white top,
a black skirt and red gloves. Its art, each of its moves, its guard and its
Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is a bare-handed brawler whose
strength grows out of the beating it takes: it rushes in with fists and
feet, seizes its opponents to drive them into the floor or carry them up
and hurl them down, sends a wave along the ground, and every real hit it
suffers fills its **Limit** gauge until it can unleash the **Sunburst Fist**,
its final Limit Break. That is in its moves (grabs that only go on if they
seize someone, a leap that carries its target, spikes into the floor, a
wave that only reaches the grounded, a technique only a full Limit gauge
opens), never its locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every
other fighter does. It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: its two
throws are **grabs** (`grab`, an attack mechanic any fighter may now use:
the rest of the move follows only a seizure, and a grab that seizes nobody
lets go at once); Meteor Strike leaps off the ground carrying its target
(`motion: rise` and `carry`) and hurls it down; both throws spike their
target into the floor, which a target hurt enough rebounds off (the shared
launch bounce); the Divekick is a plunge (`motion: slam`); the Somersault a
rising kick, its recovery; Waterkick's wave runs along the ground and
reaches only a fighter standing on it (`quake`), so a jump clears it; and
the Sunburst Fist is a **Limit Break** (`limit`, a technique's: open only once
its Limit gauge, filled by the damage it takes, is full, and spending the
whole gauge as it lets go).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a martial artist of a
master's fighting style who fights with its fists and its feet in weighted
gloves. Its Limit gauge fills as it takes damage, and once it is full its
Limit Breaks come out: Beat Rush (a flurry of blows), Somersault (a
flipping kick), Waterkick (a kick that sends water along the ground),
Meteodrive (it seizes its foe and suplexes it into the ground), Meteor
Strike (it seizes its foe, leaps up with it and hurls it down into the
ground) and, the last of them, its strength gathered into one punch that
explodes; in its later fighting it also strikes with a
whirling uppercut and a diving kick. It heals nobody, summons nobody,
makes no clone, throws nothing of its own and never homes in; its spells
come from the equipment anyone may carry, not its own fighting. So: its
moves are its fists, its kicks, its two throws, its wave and its Limit
Breaks, its Limit is the game's own gauge of the damage it has taken, its
guard is its fists raised, its Deflect a high kick, its Dash a low lunge.
The one move its owner left out is not in the game, nor cut from the
sheet.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Sunburst Fist, its Limit
Break, a 10-damage punch that only a full Limit gauge opens. It sits under
Special abilities in the controller editor, and an orange exclamation mark
floats over #0014 while it performs it. Its rushes, its throws, its leap,
its dive and its wave are its everyday tools: ordinary to the player.

### The Limit and the Sunburst Fist

`limit` is a shared technique field
([combat: techniques](../systems/combat.md#techniques)), and #0014 is the
first to use it. Every fighter has a Limit gauge (`CombatState.limitGauge`),
empty at the start of every life: every real hit it takes adds its damage
to it (`CombatState.fillLimit`, in `CombatSystem.applyHit`), whoever deals
it, and nothing else touches it. Unlike the Launch Point it never recovers,
and a heal (a perfect Shield's included) never takes anything off it; a
blocked hit deals nothing and fills nothing. A technique with a `limit`
opens only once the gauge holds that many Launch Points (`limitReady`):
below it, a press does nothing at all (nothing paid, nothing kept for
later), the CPU never plans it (`moveReady`) and the ability HUD shows its
tile held back, the gauge's fill drawn in its sweep (`moveRestriction` gives
`limit`, `abilityStatus` its `limitFill`), with a brief highlight as it
fills. Letting it go breaks the Limit: on its release step the whole gauge
is spent (`CombatState.spendLimit`), whatever it held; a cast broken by a
hit before its release spends nothing (and the hit fills the gauge more).
A respawn starts a fresh, empty gauge.

The **Sunburst Fist** (`attack5`, `limit: 40`): thirteen twentieths of a second
gathering its power into its raised fist (nothing protects it: a hit
breaks the cast), then the punch, its whole body behind it: everyone in
the blast before it, `{ x: 4, y: -110, w: 126, h: 112 }` (from its fist to
130 units out and from the floor to well over its head, the explosion the
art draws there), takes 10 at Base Launch 3, thrown the way it punches,
hitstun 0.6, hitstop 0.15. The explosion bursts there (its burst `effect`,
`attack5_object`, centred 70 units out at its chest, played once behind
the fighters). A fifth of a second more in its follow-through. The
cooldown policy's 5 s, for its 10, from the release; no Energy. A raised
Shield blocks it like any hit, and the gauge is spent all the same.

So the more #0014 is beaten, the sooner it can strike back: 40 Launch
Points is a few strings and a launcher taken.

### Grabs: Meteodrive and Meteor Strike

`grab` is a shared attack field
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and #0014 is the first to use it: `grab: { recovery }` on a multi-hit
attack makes its first strike a seizure, and the rest of the move follows
only a seizure. If that strike has made no real hit by the end of its
window (it found nobody, or a raised Shield blocked it), the grab has
missed: no later strike is live, and the attack ends `recovery` seconds
after the window (`whiffAt`), well before its full length. Until it has
seized someone, its motion never moves it, so a lift (`motion: rise`) that
misses never leaves the ground. Nothing passes a Shield: a blocked grab is
a missed one.

- **Meteodrive** (`attack2`, `grab: { recovery: 0 }`): it reaches out
  (held a moment) and seizes (live from 2/15 s for one frame, `{ x: 4, y:
  -72, w: 36, h: 60 }`, in front of it out to 40 units; 1, no launch,
  hitstun 0.5, and `carry: { lift: 0 }`: the seized target takes #0014's
  own speed, held for the throw, so it never slides out of it), heaves its fist up over
  its head and bends back over into a bridge (live from 4/15 s for two
  frames, `{ x: -30, y: -96, w: 76, h: 96 }`, from behind it to in front,
  floor to over its head, where it swings its target through; 5, Base
  Launch 3 straight down, hitstun 0.45, hitstop 0.1: a suplex into the
  floor), then kneels up, rises and is in its stance: three fifths of a
  second in all. A spike into the floor rebounds by the shared launch
  bounce once it is hard enough (the floor sends back 0.6 of it), so a
  barely hurt target stays where it fell and one hurt enough is thrown back
  up off the floor. A miss is over at a fifth of a second. Hit-cancel from
  7/15 s once it has hit. The shared Step-in. Ground only.
- **Meteor Strike** (`attack3`, `grab: { recovery: 0 }`): it reaches out
  (held a moment) and seizes (live from 2/15 s for one frame, `{ x: 2, y:
  -84, w: 44, h: 76 }`; 1, and `carry: { lift: 500 }`: the seizure throws
  its target up as fast as #0014 rises), and leaps off the ground at 500
  units/s (`motion: rise`), still holding it, its fist raised (live from
  4/15 s, `{ x: -6, y: -116, w: 52, h: 104 }`, in front of it and over its
  head; 1, carried on), then turns over in a red arc and hurls it down
  (live from 5/15 s, `{ x: -20, y: -112, w: 64, h: 112 }`; 5, Base Launch
  3 straight down, hitstun 0.45, hitstop 0.1): driven into the floor,
  which a target hurt enough rebounds off. Four fifteenths of a second
  rising (about 130 units), then it hangs, turned, and falls: seven
  fifteenths of a second in all, half the speed it had kept as it leaps.
  A miss is over at a fifth of a second, on the ground. Ground only (its
  air move is the Somersault).

### Waterkick

A technique: a fifth of a second lunging low into a sweep along the ground
(nothing protects it), then the kick sweeps up off the ground in a red
arc, and its wave (`attack4_object`) leaves 44 units in front of it, its
box clear of the floor (20 units up, half its box's 32), the way it
faces. A fifth of a second more as it turns out of the kick. The wave runs
at 520 units/s for half a second (about 260 units), `{ x: -30, y: -16, w:
60, h: 32 }`: a wave along the floor, it reaches only a fighter standing
on the ground (`quake`): one in the air is passed by, nothing used up, so
a jump clears it. Whoever it meets takes 3 at Base Launch 1, swept off its
feet the way it runs (hitstun 0.4); a Shield that blocks it is shoved back
(`blockPush` 300) and stops it. No cooldown (3 is ordinary); the wave is a
construct, 15 Energy. Ordinary to the player: no Special.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Beat Rush** (`attack1`) and the **Whirling Uppercut**
(`extra_attack`), its two close-range strikes (out to 62 and 46 units).
Its throws are grabs that leap or spike, Waterkick and the Sunburst Fist are
techniques, so none could be a partner. Five rapid presses of either
one's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, the mashed move
first, then the other, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to 11
launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its
own launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its one-frame `mouvment`
clip, a low lunge, its fists forward, its hair streaming, shown across the
whole Dash, and its one-frame `midair_mouvment`, the same lunge drawn a
second time, across the whole air dash (the sheet draws its dash as two
lunges and a hop: the lunges are cut, one each). Its canon's speed is in
its moves, never in how it runs or jumps.

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement
([attack movement](../systems/movement.md#5-attack-movement)): nothing
held ever steers one on the ground, every aerial keeps the universal air
control, and its close strikes step in by themselves.

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', perfectWindow: 0.1,
perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: its fists raised, then its arms crossed before it.
The sheet draws no lowering, so there is no lowering clip: the Shield goes
down at once. In the air the Shield button is its **Deflect** (its
`deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)):

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -14, y: -98, w: 56, h: 100 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Its knee drawn up (the startup), then its kick swept up before it through
a red arc to its leg held high for two frames (live: 14 units behind it to
42 in front, from its feet to over its head, the arc the art draws), held
(its recovery): four fifteenths of a second, keeping the whole of its
drift. While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base
Launch 2, here sideways, kicked away before it, and turns the other
fighter's projectiles it meets back at their thrower, now #0014's. No
cooldown; starting it costs nothing (never while exhausted), and the first
projectile it turns back costs 15 Energy, once however many it turns back,
as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15 only for turning a
projectile back, each blocked hit 15. Waterkick, its one construct, costs
15; the Sunburst Fist has its 5 s instead. It leaves `energy` out and refills at
the shared 12 a second. Its Limit gauge is not Energy: it costs nothing and
nothing pays into it but the hits it takes.

Its `launchReaction`: light, it is flung a little longer, 0.15 s per 1000
units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from 1100 units/s, and 20° of steering. Its
launches are as strong as anyone's, and it rebounds off the scenery as
everyone does (no `launchBounce` of its own); its throws' spikes rebound
their targets by those same shared rules.

Where a row repeats a drawing, the clip plays the same file again: one
file on disk, never a copy within a codename. The sheet draws two of its
stance's poses twice (the stance plays them again). Where two
rows draw the same picture, each clip still plays its own codename's file
([`codename_rule`](../../codename_rule)): the two throws reach out and
seize alike, Beat Rush's guard is the same drawing as the Sunburst Fist's
drawing back, and the crouch the jump springs from is the landing's and
Meteodrive's last frame. Each clip's `heightRatio` is its tallest frame
over 50, so every frame normalizes at exactly one art pixel per file
pixel, and `visual.height` (50 × 88 / 52 ≈ 84.6 world units) puts #0014's
art pixels at the roster's common size.

Its long hair streams out behind it, and its punches, kicks and arcs are
drawn out round it: they would pull the automatic anchor (the upper art's
opaque middle) off its body, so every clip authors its own anchors
(`anchorX`, art pixels from the left of each frame's visible art):
standing with both feet planted (its stance, its guard, its landing, its
strikes and throws on the ground, the Sunburst Fist's gathering, its hurt),
between its feet; anywhere else (running, in the air, its legs off the
ground), on the middle of its white top. Every frame stands on the bottom
of its art.

What of the sheet is no move: its walk, crouch, taunt, intro, wins and
losses, its portrait, its fall to the floor and getting up, its crouching
attacks, its four-hit combo's jabs, straights and kicks (its jabs and

are cut, the Whirling Uppercut), its row of extra kicks and blue-blazing punches, its spells
(fire and ice: magic its equipment lends it, not its own fighting), the
Limit Break row (a pose, an aura and a gauge drawn on their own: the Limit
is the HUD's to show), the hop after its Dash, the stance each move row
opens with where the move starts from its stance anyway, and the kick the
air combo lands from. The one row its owner left out is not cut at all.
The explosion the sheet draws at the end of its Meteor Strike row is the
Sunburst Fist's: where a hurled target lands no art can be let go (only a
technique's burst plays an effect), and the Sunburst Fist's own row draws no
blast, though its punch explodes in its canon.

## How it plays

A close-range brawler and grappler. Up close, Beat Rush and the Whirling
Uppercut are its quick strings and its Mash Combo, Beat Rush's last
straight a heavy blow and the uppercut its launcher. Its throws are its
reward for getting in: Meteodrive slams its target into the floor, Meteor
Strike carries it up and hurls it down, and either sends a hurt target
rebounding off the floor; a grab that misses costs only a fifth of a
second, but a raised Shield stops it like any hit. Waterkick catches an
opponent who stays on the ground at range, the Divekick spikes from
above, and the Somersault is its anti-air and its recovery. The Sunburst Fist
is its comeback: the more it is hit, the sooner its Limit is full, and a
full Limit is a 10-damage punch waiting for an opening. Its weaknesses:
it is light and short of reach, its one ranged tool is jumped, its Limit
Break needs a breakable moment to gather, and against an opponent who
guards well its grabs have nothing to seize.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, grabs, a lift that
carries, plunges, a ground-only projectile, a long cast with a 10), never
presses the Sunburst Fist before its Limit is full (`moveReady` asks
`limitReady`), and uses every one of its moves.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 312 a level): #0014 wins 0.50 at Medium, 0.50 at
Hard and 0.37 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range at Medium and
Hard and at its low end at Brutal (as #0006, 0.36). At Hard: 15 of 24
against #0001, 11 against #0002, 14 against #0003, 19 against #0004, 12
against #0005, 16 against #0006, 6 against #0007, 11 against #0008, 15
against #0009, 14 against #0010, 9 against #0011, 8 against #0012 and 7
against #0013. Its weakest matchups are #0007's, whose guns keep it out of
reach, and #0013's and #0012's, whose shots its Deflect alone answers (6 to
8 of 24). At Brutal it is blocked about twice as often as at Hard, and its
grabs have nothing to seize behind a Shield. Its CPU casts the Sunburst Fist
about twice a match, uses Meteor Strike three to five times and the
Somersault six to eight.

Its first tuning won 0.71 at Medium: Beat Rush and its partner struck at a
twentieth of a second (faster than the roster's tenth of a second for a
jab), its Limit was 30, and Waterkick's wave ran 340 units at Base Launch
2, the move that finished most. Their first blows were given a tenth of a
second, the Limit 40 and the wave a push (Base Launch 1) over 260 units:
0.58 at Medium, but 0.35 at Hard and 0.20 at Brutal. It dealt as much as
its opponents but finished fewer (about 1.2 falls a match at Brutal
against #0013's 1.7), and its throws, which then leapt and swung whether
they seized anyone or not, were punished whenever they missed (Brutal
without Meteor Strike won 0.08 more). Its throws' spikes were made Base
Launch 3, its Deflect turned sideways, the Sunburst Fist's gathering shortened
to 0.65 s, Beat Rush to half a second, and the throws made grabs that let
go at once when they seize nobody (0.53, 0.41 and 0.39); Beat Rush's last
straight became its heavy blow, 5, in a string of three (1, 1, 5, as
#0013's Gentle Fist): 0.55, 0.54 and 0.38. With a fourth blow before that
5 it won 0.61, 0.66 and 0.60; with the straight 3 at Base Launch 2, 0.50,
0.49 and 0.36. Neither a quicker Sunburst Fist (0.43 s) nor the wave at
Base Launch 2 lifted Brutal (0.39 and 0.40 over the same seeds, the wave
taking Medium to 0.62).

Last, its CPU's finishing: off a ledge it chose its Mash Combo, whose
twelve turns (a high kick, a low fist and the uppercut each time) ran just
over four seconds; the Whirling Uppercut was cut to its whirl and its
uppercut (the high kick was the basic combo row's, not the move's), and
the seizure of Meteodrive given `carry`, so a seized target that was
moving can no longer slide out of the suplex: the numbers above. The
results swing by about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the next.

# #0008

#0008's own specification: the rubber pirate. Its art, each of its moves,
its guard and its Deflect, and how they play. Its fantasy is a young
pirate whose whole body is rubber and who fights with nothing but that
body: its fists and feet stretched far out, coiled into a barrage, flung
like a slingshot, blown up into a balloon or swollen to a giant's, and its
meal. That is in its moves (a string that holds, a long arm that is still
its body, legs that reach down from high above, a homing slingshot, a body
that bounces shots back, a heal, a giant fist after a long, breakable
wind-up) and in how its body rebounds when it is launched, never its
locomotion: it runs, jumps and Dashes exactly as every other fighter does.
It is part of the product specification
([`ALVA_SPEC.md`](../../ALVA_SPEC.md) §7.2.9): the shared rules it relies
on are specified there and explained in [`docs/systems/`](../systems/),
and nothing here is a rule for any other fighter.

Every move is built from the shared mechanics
([combat: attack mechanics](../systems/combat.md#attack-mechanics-beyond-a-timed-hitbox)),
and most are a mechanic of their own, not just a pose and damage: Jab and
Straight and the Gatling are strings (`hits`) that hold their target to
the last blow; the Pistol, the Gatling and the Stamp stretch a limb far
out, and while they play the limb is part of its body (`hurtboxes`), so a
blow or a shot that meets the outstretched arm or legs strikes #0008; the
Stamp reaches 122 units below its feet; the Rocket is a homing slingshot
(`motion: homing`); the Balloon turns projectiles back
(`deflectProjectiles`) on the ground, and floats (`motion: hover`) in the
air; the Feast heals (`heal`); the Giant Pistol is a long, breakable cast
and a 10-damage burst far out in front that shoves a raised Shield back
(`blockPush`); and its whole body rebounds off walls, floors and ceilings
sooner and harder than anyone's (`launchBounce`).

## Its canon

What the character really does, and only that, as the owner's direction
asks ([`character_rule`](../../character_rule)): a pirate who ate a fruit
that turned his whole body into rubber, and fights with it. He stretches
his arm far back and lets it snap forward (the Pistol), throws a storm of
stretched punches (the Gatling), stretches his legs to stamp on what is
far below (the Stamp), flings himself at an enemy like a slingshot (the
Rocket), blows himself up with air so that bullets and cannonballs bounce
off him (the Balloon), bites his thumb and blows into his bones until a
fist is a giant's (the Giant Pistol, in its later fighting style), and
recovers by eating enormous amounts of meat. Blunt blows bounce off a
rubber body: launched into the scenery, it springs back. A stretched limb
is still his body, and can be hit or caught. He carries no weapon and
throws nothing. So: every move is its own body; its guard is its arms
wound round each other before it; its Deflect a whirl of its limbs; and
nothing it does is a power its canon does not give it: no summon, nothing
conjured.

## Buttons and moves

Five numbered attacks, each a button of its own: `attack1` to `attack3`
ordinary attacks with their mid-air versions, `attack4` and `attack5`
techniques; an extra attack, its Gum-Gum Pistol (ground only); and its
Mash Combo partner, the Crescent Kick, which no button performs. The
sheet's upright stance, its blurred speed versions of its moves (the
Rifle, the Jet Pistol, the Jet Gatling, the Jet Bazooka, the Jet Whip, the
Jet Stamp), its Bazooka, its rising Whip, its Axe, its twisting rising
fist, its giant twin fists, its open-mouthed lunge, its long grab, its
ledge hang, its wins and losses and its spare poses are no move.

### Special abilities

`specialAbilities: ['attack4', 'attack5']`
([combat](../systems/combat.md#special-abilities)): the Feast, the one
move that heals it (as #0003's Mystical Palm Technique is Special), and
the Gum-Gum Giant Pistol, a 10-damage giant fist that can end a life from
across the stage. Both sit under Special abilities in the controller
editor, and an orange exclamation mark floats over #0008 while it performs
either (the Feast 5/6 s + 7/12 s, the Giant Pistol 0.75 s + 0.6 s). The
Gatling's barrage, the Balloon's bounce and the Rocket's homing are
ordinary to the player.

### Mash Combo

Its pair is **Jab and Straight** (`attack1`) and the **Crescent Kick**
(`mash_attack`). Every other ground move of #0008 is no close-range strike:
the Gatling and the Pistol reach far out (110 and 96 units), the Balloon
surrounds it and turns projectiles back, the Feast strikes nobody and the
Giant Pistol is a technique. So its partner is a close-range strike of its
own body with no button: a kick swept up through a crescent right in front
of it (out to 52 units), performed only as the series' partner turn, the
twelfth its finisher (3, Base Launch 2 straight up). Five rapid presses of
Jab and Straight's button on the ground set off its [Mash
Combo](../systems/combat.md#mash-combo): twelve turns, Jab and Straight
first, then the Crescent Kick, alternating, each twice as fast, turns 1 to
11 launching nothing (their damage still counts) and the twelfth with its
own launch.

## Movement

None of its own: it runs, turns, jumps (the triple jump), fast-falls,
Dashes and air dashes on the universal values
([movement](../systems/movement.md#2-the-universal-values)), exactly as
every other fighter does. It brings the art: its two-frame `mouvment`
clip, a blurred lunge, played once across the whole Dash, and its
one-frame `midair_mouvment`, a flat flight arms first, across the whole

Its attacks move by the shared attack movement

## Defense, Energy and launch reaction

Its `defense` is a guard, on the ground: `{ type: 'shield', groundAnimation:
'shielding', groundStartAnimation: 'prepshield', groundReleaseAnimation:
'releaseshield', perfectWindow: 0.1, perfectRearm: 0.25 }`: an arm
brought across (one frame), both arms wound round each other into a rubber
wall before it (one), let go, springing loose (one). In the air the Shield
button is its **Deflect** (its `deflect` entry, [defense: the Deflect](../systems/defense.md#the-deflect)),
a whirl of arms and legs:

```js
deflect: {
  animation: 'deflect',
  startup: 1 / 15, active: 2 / 15, recovery: 1 / 15,
  hitbox: { x: -36, y: -82, w: 72, h: 84 },
  directionalLaunch: 'horizontal', hitstun: 0.3, blockstun: 0.12, hitstop: 0.05,
  deflectProjectiles: true,
},
```

Frame 1 the startup, then the whirl (frames 2-3, live: all round its body
from the feet to over the head, 36 units either side), its recovery a frame
more: a little over a quarter of a second, keeping the whole of its drift.
While it is live its box strikes for every Deflect's 3 at Base Launch 2,
here sideways, and turns the other fighter's projectiles it meets back at
their thrower, now #0008's. No cooldown; it costs 15 Energy as it starts,
as every Deflect does.

Its Energy is every fighter's ([Energy](../systems/energy.md)): 100, a
Dash, an air dash or a Dash cancel 15, a Deflect 15, each blocked hit 15.
None of its own moves costs anything (it creates nothing), so it leaves
`energy` out and refills at the shared 12 a second. Its `launchReaction`:
0.15 s per 1000 units/s up to 0.5 s, tumbling from 1150 units/s, 20° of
steering.

**Its rubber body** is the one `launchBounce` of its own in the roster
([launch: launch bounce](../systems/launch.md#launch-bounce)): launched
into a wall, the floor or a ceiling, it rebounds from an impact of 400
units/s (everyone else 500) and sends back more of it: walls 0.9, the floor
0.8, ceilings 0.85 (everyone else 0.72, 0.6, 0.65). A launch that stops
anyone else dead against a wall sends #0008 springing back across the
stage. The rest of the bounce settings are the shared ones: a rebound
still stuns it for at least 0.2 s, and rebounds still stop at five until it
recovers.

its body (the Pistol's arm stretched back is twice its width behind it; the
giant fist is three bodies wide), and the automatic anchor (the upper

below it, the clip says where its feet would be (`anchorY`, art pixels down
from the top: 29, 40, 40, 40, 33), so its body stays where it is and the
legs reach below. Everything else stands on the bottom of its art.

If its airborne, landing or hurt frames fail to load it holds its first
idle frame; an attack, a technique's clip, the Shield, the Deflect, the
Dash or the air dash whose frames are missing is refused.

Each of its ordinary buttons keeps its face button on the ground and in the

can be changed in Settings › Controls › Controller controls. It recommended
no layout before settings version 5, so it keeps no `controller.previous`.

**Gum-Gum Pistol** shows `extra_attack` frame 5 (the fist out at full
stretch, `extra_attack_5`); **Jab and Straight** `attack1` frame 7 (the
straight, `attack1_6`) / in the air **Gum-Gum Stamp** `midair_attack1`

`midair_attack2` frame 2 (flung head first, `midair_attack2_3`);
**Gum-Gum Balloon** `attack3` frame 3 (blown up, `attack3_4`), in the air too; **Feast**
`attack4_release` frame 0 (the meat, `attack4_6`); **Gum-Gum Giant
Pistol** `attack5_release` frame 2 (the giant fist out, `attack5_12`). Its
touch cluster uses slots 1 to 5; the Shield button keeps its universal
glyph, named and drawn as Deflect while #0008 is in the air.

## How it plays

A rubber brawler that fights at every range with nothing but its body. Up
close, Jab and Straight is its quick string and the start of its Mash
Combo; a step further out, the Gatling's stretched fists hold and fling,
and the Pistol strikes from almost two bodies away and sends its target
far. In the air the Stamp reaches down on whoever waits below and the
Rocket slings it at its opponent. Against shots it blows itself up and
bounces them back; at a safe moment it eats; and its giant fist, slow to
swell, ends lives from across the stage. Its weaknesses are its own body:
every stretched limb stays open to a hit while it is out, the Pistol, the
Gatling and the Balloon are long to finish, and the Feast and the giant
fist's long wind-ups are broken by any hit.

**The CPU** plays it from the data ([AI](../systems/ai.md)): the one
universal CPU reads its moves as any fighter's (strings, long reaches, a
spike from above, a homing dash, a hover, a heal, a long cast) and uses
every one of them, its Balloon too: a shot coming at it is answered with
any move of its own that turns projectiles back, pressed so its box is
live as the shot arrives.

## Balance

Measured from seeded CPU-vs-CPU matches at equal levels (full Watch Mode
battles, first to 3, on Desert and City, both sides, six seeds: 24 matches
against each opponent, 168 a level): #0008 wins 0.61 at Medium, 0.52 at
Hard and 0.44 at Brutal, inside the roster's usual range (the other
fighters 0.43 to 0.63 over the same kind of runs). At Hard: 16 of 24
against #0001, 12 against #0002, 12 against #0003, 13 against #0004, 16
against #0005, 6 against #0006 and 13 against #0007. Strongest against
#0001 and #0005; weakest against #0006, whose beam, scream and breath keep
it out, and, at Medium, #0007, whose shots punish its long reaches. Its CPU
uses the Gatling and its Mash Combo most, then the Rocket, the Pistol and
the Stamp. The results swing by about 0.1 from one set of seeds to the
next.

As first written it won 0.64 at Medium but 0.44 at Hard and Brutal: its
Pistol launched at Base Launch 2 and its Balloon dealt 3 to whoever
touched it. Holding the Gatling's wind-up a frame longer (0.15 s) cost it
far too much (0.42, 0.41, 0.27), so the Gatling kept its 0.1 s. The
Pistol now launches at Base Launch 3 (in its canon it sends its target
flying; #0005's Long Bite is the roster's other slow, long reach at 5 and
Base Launch 3), and the Balloon is a bounce, not a blow (1): it gives way
to any request, so its recovery can always be cut short, and a stronger
hit would make it a blow that is never punished.
